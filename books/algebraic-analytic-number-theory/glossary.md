
# Glossary and Proof Finder

**Average order:** a comparison of summatory functions. Chapter one distinguishes
it from pointwise and normal order and proves its error budgets.

**Character:** a multiplicative root-of-unity-valued coordinate on a finite
abelian group, extended by zero for nonunits in a Dirichlet character.
Chapter two proves completeness and the progression filter, with analytic
nonvanishing identified separately.

**Completed zeta:** pi to minus s-over-two times Gamma(s/2) zeta(s).
Chapter four proves its reflection by Gaussian Poisson summation and a
convergent theta integral. Its pole list differs from zeta's pole list.

**Newman lemma:** boundedness and a holomorphic boundary extension of a
Laplace transform imply convergence of the improper integral. Chapter six
proves the contour kernel estimates and the PNT monotonicity step.

**Dedekind factorisation:** unique products of nonzero prime ideals in the
full integer ring. Chapter nine proves the local uniformizer theorem,
global reassembly, ideal norms and index-good polynomial splitting.

**Class group:** nonzero fractional ideals modulo principal ideals. Chapter
ten proves its finite Minkowski bound and exact small quadratic examples.

**Fundamental units:** lifts of a basis of the full logarithmic unit
lattice. Chapter ten proves its rank and explains why an independent
unit list needs an index argument before it is called fundamental.

**Cyclotomic integer ring:** the full ring Z[zeta-n]. Chapter eleven proves
maximality also at divisor primes, using actual local integer rings.

**Local field:** a nondiscrete locally compact complete valued field. Chapter
twelve constructs the p-adic example, proves Hensel root and factor lifting,
and establishes the unramified and Eisenstein cases used in cyclotomy.

**Pic-zero:** degree-zero divisor classes modulo principal divisors. Chapter
thirteen derives the associative chord law from its point bijection, with
Riemann–Roch explicitly imported.

**Mordell–Weil:** finite generation of elliptic points over a number field.
Chapter fourteen proves uniform halving extensions, weak quotient finiteness,
Northcott, height comparison and contraction; curve-specific rank computation
is a further task.

**Modular L-function:** a coefficient Dirichlet series with entire Mellin
completion and reflection under the full-level cusp hypotheses. Chapter fifteen
separately proves Euler products under normalized common Hecke eigenconditions.

**Discrete logarithm:** a verified exponent in a specified cyclic subgroup.
Chapter sixteen proves bounded baby-step giant-step and Pohlig–Hellman,
distinguishing exact witnesses, complete failure ranges and heuristic costs.
