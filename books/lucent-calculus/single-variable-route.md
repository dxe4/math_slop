# The 150-hour single-variable calculus route

This route turns the Calculus I and II chapters into 150 hours of scheduled
work. It is a planning specification, not a credit claim: the times have not
yet been calibrated with learners. Record actual time, scores, and error types.
An instructor can then shorten or extend a block without deleting its
independent or delayed work.

The route assumes the readiness diagnostic is mostly secure. A learner who
needs the algebra or elementary-functions bridge should complete that work
before starting the 150 hours. Each block has four kinds of activity:

- **Preparation** recalls prerequisites, reads the opening question, and makes a
  prediction before a visual is operated.
- **Developed study** follows definitions, proofs, visual arguments, and worked
  examples while reconstructing decisive steps.
- **Independent work** uses recitations only for the guided portion; problem
  sets and exam attempts begin without solutions.
- **Retrieval or assessment** revisits an older idea without a method label,
  attempts a timed paper, or corrects a marked attempt.

The hours in every row sum to the stated block total. The five units sum to
exactly **150 hours**.

<details><summary>B5.1 changed-return model: the field is faster</summary>

For d>0, h>0, and v_g>v_r>0, put u=d−s≥0 on 0≤s≤d. Since
u/sqrt(u²+h²)<1, the same time model has
T′(s)=1/v_r−u/[v_g sqrt(u²+h²)]>1/v_r−1/v_g>0.
The MVT therefore makes time strictly increasing on the feasible interval,
so its unique global minimum is s=0. The earlier stationary-point formula is
unavailable for this changed speed order; the derivative argument supplies
the boundary answer directly.

</details>

## Fixed public forms for E5 and E6

These are ordered public self-study forms. Use exactly the task and form

### Exposure and feedback for both public forms

Before each sitting, record whether you have already seen any listed prompt,
hint, primary model, changed prompt, or changed model, including use outside
this reader. Record help during the sitting too. Keep the first attempt before
comparison and retain the correction separately. A previously viewed model or
helped attempt is labeled assisted reconstruction; close the answer and try
again after at least one day, recording the earlier exposure. Native saved
exercises record only the local openings actually observed by that reader.
Record route disclosures, prior views, and outside help yourself; these remain
unverified declarations.

These published forms, their model answers, and their self-awarded marks do
not establish fresh protected assessment, independently verified unaided work,
or mastery. Use the marks to find a failed dependency and choose a repair.
The existing assessment studio, complete source papers, optional questions,
and source grading weights remain available for their stated purposes.

## Assessment and reference policy

For a conventional marked offering, use this weighting:

| Evidence | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Independent problem sets and revisions | 30% |
| Unit examinations A–D | 35% |
| Three-hour cumulative paper and oral defence | 20% |
| Reproducible modeling capstone | 10% |
| Recitation explanations and error-log portfolio | 5% |

Ordinary lessons and first problem-set attempts are open-note, but reading a
worked answer before producing a complete attempt invalidates that attempt as
assessment evidence. Unless an assessment says otherwise, timed unit papers
permit one handwritten A4 sheet of definitions and theorem statements, a basic
calculator, and no worked examples, CAS, notebook, or interactive graph. The
cumulative paper uses the same policy. The capstone permits the Lucent language
and other declared tools because reproducibility and critique are assessed.

The focused unit papers and the fixed public E5/E6 forms provide explicit
self-study paths. A conventional marked offering can use the published weights
and reference rules with declared marking arrangements. The public models and
self-reported exposure do not make those tasks protected or their grade
boundaries calibrated; actual workload and marking calibration remain open.
