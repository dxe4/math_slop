# Calculus I: change and accumulation examination

**Role:** cumulative written assessment. Attempt after limits, differentiation, global shape, Riemann integration, and the FTC. Suggested time: 100 minutes.

Work without opening hints or solutions for the first pass. Record the argument, not only a numerical result. The browser saves drafts and revisions, but a written proof is self-reviewed rather than automatically certified. Each task is worth 10 marks: 2 for a defensible setup and hypotheses, 5 for the central reasoning, 2 for checking an edge case or competing method, and 1 for a clear conclusion. A result without the requested reasoning earns at most 2.

**Outcomes assessed:** C2 limits and quantified control; C5 optimization and hypotheses; C6 accumulation and moving endpoints.

## Revision conference

After checking, choose one false start and write the exact line where its hypothesis failed. Rework the changed case without consulting the first solution; the changed-case response is saved separately. A strong answer names the theorem used, tests its hypotheses, and explains what the computation means.
