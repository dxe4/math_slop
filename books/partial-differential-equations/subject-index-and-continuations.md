
# Subject Index and Continuations

The classification chapter covers order, linearity, principal matrices, elliptic/parabolic/hyperbolic model distinctions, exact modes, characteristic coordinates, data compatibility and an unstable elliptic Cauchy family.

The heat chapter covers bounded-cylinder maximum and comparison principles, final-time regularity, uniform data/source estimates, mass and energy under stated endpoint assumptions, Gaussian construction, smooth forcing and whole-space uniqueness with bounded growth.

The authored route also covers transverse flow charts, conservative jump balance and entropy orientation, harmonic mean value and boundary barriers, ball/half-plane Poisson kernels, wave cone energy and reflection, separated series, complete regular Dirichlet spectra, transform compatibility, causal source integrals and selected numerical convergence estimates. The detailed finder links those topics to their chapter numbers.

The L4 continuation is [PDE: Weak Theory, Waves and Patterns](#/books/pde-weak-theory-waves-patterns), with its actual [Sobolev opening](../pde-weak-theory-waves-patterns/sobolev-spaces.md). The retained elliptic/parabolic weak reading is an available companion with its own preparation. [Functional Analysis: Semigroups](../functional-analysis/semigroups.md) proves abstract generator and Duhamel results; identifying a concrete PDE domain remains separate work.
