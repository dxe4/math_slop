
# Glossary and Index

This is a retrieval aid. Compact entries cannot replace the full definitions or hypotheses in their chapters. The primary route has fourteen chapters; entries for Model Categories, Higher Categories, Topos Theory, Categorical Logic, Monoidal Categories, Enriched Categories and the earlier categorical project refer to retained catalogue companions outside that native order.

## Primary additions

**Flat module.** A left module whose tensor functor on right modules preserves injections, equivalently is exact. Projectivity implies flatness; the converse fails for $\mathbb Q$ over $\mathbb Z$. “Projective, Injective and Flat Modules”.

**Projective module.** A module admitting a lift along every surjection; equivalently a direct summand of a free module. “Projective, Injective and Flat Modules”.

**Balanced tensor product.** The abelian-group quotient imposing bilinearity and $(nr)\otimes m=n\otimes(rm)$ for a right and a left module over the same ring. “Tensor Products and Hom”.

**Yoneda extension.** A short exact sequence with specified endpoints, considered up to an isomorphism inducing the identity on both endpoints. Its equivalence class belongs to $\operatorname{Ext}^1$; Baer addition supplies the group law. “Extensions and Ext¹”.

**Group cohomology.** The right derived functors of invariants, $H^n(G,M)=\operatorname{Ext}^n_{\mathbb ZG}(\mathbb Z,M)$. The homogeneous bar resolution proves the cochain model used here. “Group Cohomology”.

## Retained glossary

**Abelian category.** An additive category in which every morphism has a kernel and a cokernel, every monomorphism is a kernel, and every epimorphism is a cokernel. "Abelian Categories".

**Adjunction.** A pair of functors $F: \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}$ and $G: \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}$ with a natural isomorphism $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(F(X), Y) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X, G(Y))$. "Adjunctions and Exactness".

**Cartan--Eilenberg resolution.** A compatible system of injective resolutions of the terms, cycles, boundaries, and homology objects of a complex. It supports hypercohomology filtrations; its rows and columns are not simply all exact. "Project: Categorical Proof".

**Chain complex.** A sequence $\cdots \to C_{n+1} \xrightarrow{d_{n+1}} C_{n} \xrightarrow{d_{n}} C_{n-1} \to \cdots$ with $d_{n} d_{n+1} = 0$. "Derived Categories".

**Classifying topos.** A topos $\mathcal{E}$ such that geometric morphisms $\mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{E}$ correspond to models of a geometric theory in $\mathcal{F}$. "Topos Theory".

**Cohomological functor.** A functor $H: \mathcal{T} \to \mathcal{A}$ from a triangulated category to an abelian category sending distinguished triangles to long exact sequences. "Triangulated Categories".

**Coherent category.** A regular category with finite unions of subobjects stable under pullback. "Categorical Logic".

**Cokernel.** The quotient of the codomain by the image; categorically, the universal morphism $c: B \to C$ with $cf = 0$. "Abelian Categories".

**Derived category.** The localization of the homotopy category of complexes at quasi-isomorphisms. "Derived Categories".

**Derived functor.** A functor repairing the failure of exactness: $R^{n}F$ for left exact $F$, $L_{n}G$ for right exact $G$. "Derived Categories".

**Distinguished triangle.** A member of the chosen class of triangles $A \to B \to C \to A[1]$ in a triangulated category. The class collectively satisfies TR1--TR4. "Triangulated Categories".

**Dualizing complex.** A complex on a scheme that represents the relevant duality functor. For a proper structural morphism $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, this book normalizes the absolute complex as $f^!k$; arbitrary shifts remain dualizing complexes but do not satisfy the displayed trace formula with that normalization. "Duality and Local Cohomology".

**Enriched category.** A category whose hom-sets are replaced by objects of a monoidal category $\mathcal{V}$. "Enriched Categories".

**Exact couple.** A diagram $D \xrightarrow{i} D$, $D \xrightarrow{j} E$, $E \xrightarrow{k} D$ exact at each vertex, giving rise to a spectral sequence. "Spectral Sequences and Filtrations".

**Exact sequence.** A sequence exact at each internal object: $\operatorname{im} f_{i-1} = \ker f_{i}$. "Modules: linear algebra over a ring".

**Fibration.** A morphism with the right lifting property against acyclic cofibrations in a model category. A map lifting against every cofibration is an acyclic fibration. "Model Categories".

**Filtration.** A nested sequence of subcomplexes $F_{p}C_{\bullet}$. Exhaustiveness, boundedness, separation, and completeness are additional properties used in convergence theorems. "Spectral Sequences and Filtrations".

**Five lemma.** A diagram lemma propagating isomorphisms through a morphism of exact sequences. "Abelian Categories".

**Free module.** A module satisfying the universal property of free objects: $F(S)$ with basis $S$. "Modules: linear algebra over a ring".

**Freyd-Mitchell embedding theorem.** Every small abelian category embeds fully faithfully and exactly into $R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ for some ring $R$. "Abelian Categories".

**Geometric morphism.** An adjoint pair $f^{*} \dashv f_{*}$ between topoi with $f^{*}$ preserving finite limits. "Topos Theory".

**Global dimension.** The supremum of projective dimensions of all modules over a ring. "Homological Dimension".

**Grothendieck spectral sequence.** A spectral sequence computing the composition of derived functors: $E^{p,q}_{2} = R^{p}G(R^{q}F(A)) \Rightarrow R^{p+q}(G \circ F)(A)$. "Project: Categorical Proof".

**Grothendieck topos.** A category equivalent to sheaves of sets on a site. "Topos Theory".

**Heyting algebra.** A lattice with implication satisfying $a \wedge b \leq c \iff a \leq b \Rightarrow c$. "Categorical Logic".

**Homotopy category.** The category of complexes with morphisms homotopy classes of chain maps. "Derived Categories".

**Injective module.** A module $I$ such that $\operatorname{Hom}(-, I)$ is exact. "Derived Categories".

**Internal hom.** The right adjoint $[A, -]$ to $-\otimes A$ in a closed monoidal category. "Monoidal Categories".

**Kan complex.** A simplicial set in which every horn has a filler. "Model Categories".

**Kernel.** The universal morphism $k: K \to A$ with $fk = 0$. "Abelian Categories".

**Leray spectral sequence.** The Grothendieck spectral sequence for $f_{*}$ and global sections: $H^{p}(Y, R^{q}f_{*}\mathcal{F}) \Rightarrow H^{p+q}(X, \mathcal{F})$. "Project: Categorical Proof".

**Local cohomology.** The right derived functor of the torsion functor $\Gamma_{I}$. "Duality and Local Cohomology".

**Local duality.** For a Cohen--Macaulay local ring admitting a canonical module and a finitely generated module $M$, $H^{i}_{\mathfrak{m}}(M) \cong \operatorname{Ext}^{d-i}_{R}(M, \omega_{R})^{\vee}$. "Duality and Local Cohomology".

**Mapping cone.** A complex encoding the cokernel and kernel of a chain map. "Triangulated Categories".

**Model category.** A category with weak equivalences, fibrations, and cofibrations satisfying MC1--MC5. "Model Categories".

**Module.** An abelian group with a scalar action satisfying distributivity and associativity; in this book modules over unital rings also satisfy $1m=m$. "Modules: linear algebra over a ring".

**Monad.** A monoid in the category of endofunctors; induced by an adjunction $F \dashv G$ as $T = G \circ F$. "Adjunctions and Exactness".

**Monoidal category.** A category with a tensor product $\otimes$, unit $I$, and coherence isomorphisms. "Monoidal Categories".

**Noetherian module.** A module in which every ascending chain of submodules stabilizes. "Modules: linear algebra over a ring".

**Projective module.** A module $P$ such that $\operatorname{Hom}(P, -)$ is exact. "Derived Categories".

**Quasicategory.** A simplicial set in which every inner horn has a filler. "Higher Categories".

**Quasi-isomorphism.** A morphism of complexes inducing isomorphisms on all homology groups. "Derived Categories".

**Serre duality.** $H^{i}(X, \mathcal{F})^{*} \cong \operatorname{Ext}^{n-i}_{X}(\mathcal{F}, \omega_{X})$ for a smooth projective variety. "Duality and Local Cohomology".

**Sheaf.** A presheaf satisfying the gluing axiom for covering sieves, equivalently for the covering families of a chosen pretopology. "Topos Theory".

**Short exact sequence.** A five-term exact sequence $0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0$. "Modules: linear algebra over a ring".

**Smith normal form.** A diagonal matrix $\operatorname{diag}(d_{1}, \dots, d_{r})$ with $d_{i} \mid d_{i+1}$ equivalent to a given integer matrix. "Modules: linear algebra over a ring".

**Snake lemma.** A diagram lemma producing a connecting homomorphism $\partial$ in a morphism of short exact sequences. "Abelian Categories".

**Spectral sequence.** A sequence of bigraded complexes with $E_{r+1}\cong H(E_r,d_r)$. Convergence is extra data identifying $E_\infty$ with the associated graded of a specified filtered abutment. "Spectral Sequences and Filtrations".

**Stable $(\infty,1)$-category.** An $(\infty,1)$-category with finite limits and colimits and a zero object in which a square is a pushout exactly when it is a pullback. "Higher Categories".

**Subobject classifier.** An object $\Omega$ classifying monomorphisms in a topos. "Topos Theory".

**Tensor product.** The universal balanced construction $M \otimes_{R} N$ for a right $R$-module $M$ and a left $R$-module $N$; for commutative $R$, left modules use the induced right action. "Modules: linear algebra over a ring"; "Monoidal Categories".

**t-structure.** A pair of subcategories $(\mathcal{T}^{\leq 0}, \mathcal{T}^{\geq 0})$ on a triangulated category with heart an abelian category. "Triangulated Categories".

**Triangulated category.** An additive category with shift $[1]$ and distinguished triangles satisfying TR1--TR4. "Triangulated Categories".

**Universal property.** A characterization of an object by morphisms into or out of it. "Abelian Categories".

**Weak equivalence.** A morphism inverted in the homotopy category of a model category. "Model Categories".

**Yoneda lemma.** A natural isomorphism $[\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}}, \mathcal{V}](\mathcal{C}(-, A), F) \cong F(A)$ in the enriched setting. "Enriched Categories".

## Claim finder with status guards

### Theorems

**Hilbert basis theorem ("Modules: linear algebra over a ring").** If $R$ is a commutative Noetherian ring, then $R[x]$ is Noetherian.

**Freyd-Mitchell embedding ("Abelian Categories").** Every small abelian category admits a fully faithful exact embedding into $R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$.

**Grothendieck spectral sequence ("Project: Categorical Proof").** Under standard acyclicity hypotheses, $E^{p,q}_{2} = R^{p}G(R^{q}F(A)) \Rightarrow R^{p+q}(G \circ F)(A)$.

**Hilbert syzygy theorem ("Homological Dimension").** $\operatorname{gldim}(k[x_{1}, \dots, x_{n}]) = n$.

**Auslander-Buchsbaum-Serre ("Homological Dimension").** A Noetherian local ring is regular iff it has finite global dimension, equal to its Krull dimension.

**Local duality ("Duality and Local Cohomology").** For a Cohen--Macaulay local ring admitting a canonical module and a finitely generated module $M$, $H^{i}_{\mathfrak{m}}(M) \cong \operatorname{Ext}^{d-i}_{R}(M, \omega_{R})^{\vee}$. The tagged source first gives the normalized-dualizing-complex form and a completed Ext-to-Matlis-dual statement.

**Serre duality ("Duality and Local Cohomology").** For a smooth projective variety $X$ of dimension $n$, $H^{i}(X, \mathcal{F})^{*} \cong \operatorname{Ext}^{n-i}_{X}(\mathcal{F}, \omega_{X})$.
