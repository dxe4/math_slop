
# Glossary and Scope

**End:** a universal dinatural family from one object to the values of a bifunctor. **Coend:** its dual universal quotient or colimit. **Bicategory:** objects, one-cells and two-cells with composition associative and unital up to coherent invertible two-cells. **Operad:** operations with ordered inputs and associative substitution. **Site:** a category equipped with covering sieves or covering families satisfying Grothendieck axioms. **Topos:** here a category of sheaves on a small site, unless an elementary topos is named explicitly. **Model category:** a complete and cocomplete category with weak equivalences, fibrations and cofibrations satisfying the stated lifting and factorization axioms. **Quasicategory:** a simplicial set with fillers for every inner horn.

This glossary gives orientation only. Each chapter supplies the quantified definition and examples needed for proof. A term in this list does not import all the general theorems normally associated with it.
