
# Notation and Conventions

Composition g∘f applies f first. Hom_C(X,Y) contains arrows from X to Y. A small category has sets of objects and arrows; locally small means each hom collection is a set. Large examples are handled within a fixed ambient size convention, never by forming an unrestricted set of all sets. Opposite categories reverse arrows and composition. Isomorphism of objects does not mean literal equality of their chosen representatives.
