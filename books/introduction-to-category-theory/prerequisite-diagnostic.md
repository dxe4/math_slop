
# Prerequisite Diagnostic

Before starting, explain function composition and its order, prove that a bijective group homomorphism has a homomorphic inverse, distinguish the identity map from an arbitrary idempotent, and describe a quotient group with its quotient map. For the first claim, composition applies the rightmost function first. For the group claim, apply the bijection to products and use injectivity. For the third, a projection can satisfy p²=p without being bijective. For the fourth, normality makes coset multiplication well-defined and the quotient map preserves products.
