
# References and Provenance {#section-1-references-and-provenance}

Thomas Jech, *Set Theory*, and Kenneth Kunen, *Set Theory*, are the commissioned comparison texts. Their coverage supplies the intended progression from hierarchy and combinatorics to forcing, large cardinals and definability. Exact theorem locators and historical-priority notes remain independent citation-review work.

Provenance: the former Mathematical Logic chapters `forcing`, `chapter-16`, `large-cardinals`, `descriptive-set-theory` and `set-theoretic-topology` are transferred with their original mathematical statements, worked answers and chapter identifiers. Original source files are retained. The hierarchy, cardinal/cofinality, club/stationary, combinatorial, constructible-universe, forcing-axiom, ultrapower and determinacy chapters are new text. The other five source chapters are expanded with full proofs and additional supported exercises while preserving the original statements and solutions.

The source license is CC BY-SA 4.0. No external book or paper manuscript is copied into this book. The references provide further reading. The relative-consistency, forcing, constructibility and determinacy boundaries are stated where each result is used.
