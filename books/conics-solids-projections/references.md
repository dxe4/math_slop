
# References and Further Reading {#section-1-references-and-further-reading}

This bibliography identifies sources by the job they perform in the manuscript. A citation supports the named result or historical context; it does not transfer a source's notation, exercise sequence, or production status to this book. Edition years below identify the editions consulted or recommended.

<a id="section-3-conics-quadrics-and-transformations"></a>
## Conics, quadrics, and transformations {#section-5-conics-quadrics-and-transformations}

- Berger, M. (1987). *Geometry I*. Translated by M. Cole and S. Levy. Universitext, Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-93815-6. A broad reference for Euclidean, affine, projective, spherical, and hyperbolic geometry, including conics and transformation groups.
- Coxeter, H. S. M. (1969). *Introduction to Geometry*, 2nd ed. Wiley. A classical source for conics, inversive and projective geometry, regular figures, and transformation viewpoints.
- Hilbert, D., and Cohn-Vossen, S. (1952). *Geometry and the Imagination*. Translated by P. Neményi. Chelsea. Geometric motivation for ruled quadrics, surfaces, models, and visual constructions.
- Salmon, G. (1917). *A Treatise on Conic Sections*, 6th ed., reprint. Longmans, Green. A historical analytic reference for focal, tangent, and coordinate properties of conics.
- Pottmann, H., and Wallner, J. (2001). *Computational Line Geometry*. Springer. Matrix, projective, ruled-surface, and applied geometric constructions.

<a id="section-11-non-euclidean-and-projective-geometry"></a>
## Non-Euclidean and projective geometry {#section-13-non-euclidean-and-projective-geometry}

- Anderson, J. W. (2005). *Hyperbolic Geometry*, 2nd ed. Springer. The Poincare disk, upper half-plane, hyperboloid models, distance, geodesics, and isometries.
- Greenberg, M. J. (2008). *Euclidean and Non-Euclidean Geometries: Development and History*, 4th ed. W. H. Freeman. Axiomatic and historical boundaries among Euclidean, spherical, and hyperbolic geometry.
- Hartshorne, R. (1967). *Foundations of Projective Geometry*. W. A. Benjamin. Homogeneous coordinates, projective frames, incidence, and projective transformations.
- Richter-Gebert, J. (2011). *Perspectives on Projective Geometry: A Guided Tour Through Real and Complex Geometry*. Springer. Coordinate constructions, conics, duality, and cross-ratio.
- Ratcliffe, J. G. (2019). *Foundations of Hyperbolic Manifolds*, 3rd ed. Springer. A deeper reference for hyperbolic models, groups, and metric formulas.

<a id="section-19-spherical-geometry-and-cartography"></a>
## Spherical geometry and cartography {#section-21-spherical-geometry-and-cartography}

- Snyder, J. P. (1987). *Map Projections—A Working Manual*. U.S. Geological Survey Professional Paper 1395. Authoritative formulas, scale factors, domains, and distortion conventions for the principal map projections.
- Snyder, J. P., and Voxland, P. M. (1989). *An Album of Map Projections*. U.S. Geological Survey Professional Paper 1453. Comparative projection diagrams and parameter tables.
- Bugayevskiy, L. M., and Snyder, J. P. (1995). *Map Projections: A Reference Manual*. Taylor & Francis. Derivations and comparison of cylindrical, conic, azimuthal, and compromise projections.
- Todhunter, I. (1886). *Spherical Trigonometry*, 5th ed. Macmillan. A historical source for spherical laws of sines and cosines and triangle computations.

<a id="section-26-descriptive-geometry-drawing-and-tilings"></a>
## Descriptive geometry, drawing, and tilings {#section-28-descriptive-geometry-drawing-and-tilings}

- Monge, G. (An VII/1799). *Géométrie descriptive: Leçons données aux écoles normales, l’an 3 de la République*. Baudouin. The foundational historical source for paired orthographic views and constructive descriptive geometry.
- Giesecke, F. E., et al. (2016). *Technical Drawing with Engineering Graphics*, 15th ed. Pearson. Modern conventions for first-angle and third-angle projection, auxiliary views, and axonometric drawing.
- Grünbaum, B., and Shephard, G. C. (1987). *Tilings and Patterns*. W. H. Freeman. Systematic treatment of tilings, symmetry groups, and regular and semiregular patterns.
- Senechal, M. (1995). *Quasicrystals and Geometry*. Cambridge University Press. A bridge from periodic symmetry to nonperiodic geometric structure.

<a id="section-33-surfaces-measurement-optics-and-applications"></a>
## Surfaces, measurement, optics, and applications {#section-35-surfaces-measurement-optics-and-applications}

- do Carmo, M. P. (1976). *Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces*. Prentice-Hall. Surface parametrization, metric, area, curvature, and local geometry.
- Apostol, T. M., and Mnatsakanian, M. A. (2012). *New Horizons in Geometry*. Mathematical Association of America. Centroid theorems, including Pappus-type applications, with geometric derivations.
- Born, M., and Wolf, E. (1999). *Principles of Optics: Electromagnetic Theory of Propagation, Interference and Diffraction of Light*, 7th expanded ed. Cambridge University Press. https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139644181. Ideal reflection, focusing surfaces, and the optical scope behind reflector examples.
- Vallado, D. A. (2013). *Fundamentals of Astrodynamics and Applications*, 4th ed., with technical contributions by Wayne D. McClain. Microcosm Press. Newtonian two-body conics, orbital elements, energy, and period calculations.
- Kaplan, E. D., and Hegarty, C. J., eds. (2017). *Understanding GPS/GNSS: Principles and Applications*, 3rd ed. Artech House. Pseudorange observation models, receiver clock bias, and the distinction from simplified TDOA geometry.

<a id="section-41-reading-boundaries"></a>
## Reading boundaries {#section-43-reading-boundaries}

Historical sources are included for provenance, while modern sources govern notation and technical scope. Numerical map, orbit, and positioning examples use ideal spherical or two-body models unless their chapters state otherwise. Hardware design, geodetic reference frames, atmospheric corrections, manufacturing tolerances, and safety certification require specialist sources beyond this volume.
