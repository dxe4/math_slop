## Cross-domain contrasts

- **Identification versus optimization:** identification asks whether evidence distinguishes unknowns; optimization asks which feasible choice scores best under a written objective. See `identifiability-inverse-problems` and `optimization-duality-certificates`.
- **Prediction versus intervention:** prediction conditions on an observed regime; intervention changes an assignment or mechanism. See `validation-causal-boundaries`.
- **Equilibrium versus optimum:** equilibrium blocks unilateral deviations; an optimum minimizes or maximizes a declared aggregate objective. See `games-equilibria-response`.
- **Stability versus validity:** stability controls response to perturbations inside a representation; validity concerns adequacy for use. See `sensitivity-conditioning` and `validation-causal-boundaries`.
- **Residual versus error:** residual measures equation defect; error measures distance to a target and depends on conditioning. See `sensitivity-conditioning` and `reproducible-model-computation`.
- **Physical versus pricing probability:** one models observed uncertainty; the other supports valuation under market assumptions. See `finance-pricing-risk`.
- **Structural versus practical identifiability:** exact injectivity does not ensure finite noisy recoverability. See `identifiability-inverse-problems`.
- **Model uncertainty versus Monte Carlo error:** the first concerns representation; the second concerns a finite simulation estimate. See `uncertainty-evidence-claims` and `reproducible-model-computation`.
