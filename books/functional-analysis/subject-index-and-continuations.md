
# Subject Index and Continuations

The opening chapter covers norms, seminorms, closed subspaces, completeness of continuous-function and sequence spaces, absolutely summable series, finite-dimensional norm equivalence, quotient Banach spaces, Riesz separation and unit-ball compactness.

The uniform-boundedness chapter covers pointwise quantifiers, Baire closed threshold sets, local-to-global estimates, incomplete-domain counterexamples, strong versus norm convergence, dense-set convergence under uniform stability and residual divergence of unbounded families.

The middle route develops bounded operators and duals, Hahn–Banach, open mapping, closed graphs, weak compactness and Hilbert geometry. The final route develops compact and normal spectral theory, Fredholm theory, unbounded operators, convex duality, extreme points and semigroups. Each topic states its own hypotheses; continuous spectral calculus is distinguished from Borel representation, and contraction generation from a general exponential-growth version.

For compactness and category return to [Real Analysis](#/books/proof-safe-real-analysis). Measure-based examples depend on [Measure and Integration](#/books/measure-and-integration). The semigroup chapter links the commissioned evolution/PDE continuation and a currently available weak elliptic/parabolic reading, keeping the PDE domain and energy assumptions explicit.
