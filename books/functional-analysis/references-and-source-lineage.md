
# References and Source Lineage {#section-1-references-and-source-lineage}

The syllabus benchmarks are John B. Conway, *A Course in Functional Analysis*, and Haim Brezis, *Functional Analysis, Sobolev Spaces and Partial Differential Equations*. They supply established scope and context; their exercises are not reproduced.

The available normed-spaces and uniform-boundedness chapters are original exposition with original supported exercises. Local source lineage includes the Atlas Real Analysis Banach/Lp continuation and the Quarto real-analysis functional-analysis, Baire/Ascoli and Banach-pillars chapters. Baire’s theorem is proved once in the Euler Real Analysis primary and linked at the point of use.

The later source chapters commissioned by the roadmap concern Hilbert spectra, convex duality, semigroups, unbounded spectral theory, Fredholm theory and extreme points. Their existence as earlier readings does not establish that the corresponding chapters of this new book are complete. No text or solution bank is silently reassigned between editions.

 The present source record describes available writing and remaining scope. The conclusions of an operator theorem rest on its stated hypotheses and proof, not on catalogue labels.
