## Model and output finder

This secondary index starts from a problem rather than a term. Each route names the primary chapter and the verification output that should be reported.

**Population or chemical balance with positivity.** Start at `models-wellposedness`, then use `scalar-comparison-invariant-regions` for invariant intervals or orthants. Report units, balance residual, positivity, parameter regime, and whether the bound also proves continuation.

**Thermal runaway or finite escape.** Use `scalar-comparison-invariant-regions`. Report the comparison equation, reciprocal-growth integral, lower/upper barrier direction, and whether the computed horizon approaches a theorem or only a solver failure.

**Two-point mechanical or transport boundary problem.** Use `boundary-value-continuation`. Report boundary residual, energy/coercivity or resonance test, shooting sensitivity, mesh refinement, and evidence for branch uniqueness.

**Small oscillation or coupled linear compartments.** Use `linear-systems-phase`. Report eigenstructure, nonnormal transient norm, exact matrix-exponential benchmark when possible, and output sensitivity to parameter perturbations.

**Nonlinear equilibrium near loss of stability.** Use `nonlinear-stability-bifurcation` and `center-manifolds-normal-forms`. Report hyperbolicity, crossing/nondegeneracy coefficients, local chart radius, invariance residual, and exit rule.

**Self-sustained oscillator.** Use `periodic-orbits-planar-limits`. Report trapping/exclusion theorem, section and orientation, period, closure and event residuals, transverse multiplier, and phase-independent distance.

**Sensor/actuator design.** Use `control-reachability-observation` and `robust-sampled-control`. Report scaled rank/singular values, gain, saturation region, exact sampled matrices, delay/uncertainty set, disturbance response, and intersample peaks.

**Minimum-energy or minimum-time trajectory.** Use `optimal-control-variations`. Report admissible controls, endpoint conditions, state/costate residuals, switching structure, constraint violation, mesh refinement, and the sufficiency theorem or explicit absence of one.

**Heat, diffusion, or elliptic potential.** Use `pde-energy-fourier`, `elliptic-parabolic-weak-theory`, and `numerical-pde-validation`. Report domain and traces, energy identity, weak residual, mesh/time refinement, boundary flux, positivity, and manufactured/analytic comparison.

**Wave or propagation problem.** Start at `pde-energy-fourier`; use `hyperbolic-conservation-laws` if discontinuities form. Report wave speed and boundary work, domain of dependence, energy or entropy balance, CFL, and reflection/boundary treatment.

**Shock or traffic-like conservation law.** Use `hyperbolic-conservation-laws`. Report Rankine–Hugoniot speed, entropy selection, numerical flux, mass balance, invariant range, L1 refinement, and shock-location error.

**Stationary path, membrane, or obstacle minimization.** Use `variational-direct-methods`. Report admissible space, coercivity/closure/lower-semicontinuity ledger, Euler or inequality residual, energy under refinement, and uniqueness assumptions.

**Spatial spots, stripes, or chemical patterns.** Use `reaction-diffusion-patterns`. Report reaction Jacobian, domain eigenvalues, unstable band and discrete modes, measured early growth, positivity/mass, mesh refinement, and nonlinear stationarity evidence.

**Fast decay coupled to slow motion.** Use `verified-computation-long-time` and `stiff-geometric-integrators`. Report stability multipliers, rejected steps, implicit residuals, tolerance refinement, work counts, and fast/slow output accuracy.

**Long Hamiltonian orbit.** Use `stiff-geometric-integrators`. Report step, periods elapsed, phase and energy errors separately, symplectic/reversibility checks, near-resonance stress tests, and floating-point floor.

**Irregular orbit or proposed chaos.** Use `chaos-symbolic-entropy`. Report the exact chaos definition, invariant set/coding evidence, finite-time Lyapunov protocol, tangent reorthogonalization, step/precision/horizon blocks, and what remains asymptotic.

**Long-time average or histogram.** Use `ergodic-theorems-statistics`. Report the named invariant measure or physical-basin hypothesis, observable, transient rule, block means, autocorrelation/effective variance convention, multiple starts, and discretization sensitivity.

**Data-calibrated differential model.** Use the model, theorem, computation, and inference ledgers across `models-wellposedness`, `verified-computation-long-time`, and the relevant domain chapter. Report observation map, identification limits, parameter and model discrepancy, held-out validation, numerical error, and the intended decision boundary.
