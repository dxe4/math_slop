
# Glossary

**Representation:** a homomorphism from a group to invertible linear transformations over a specified field.

**Invariant subspace:** a subspace preserved by every action operator; it defines a subrepresentation.

**Intertwiner:** an equivariant linear map between representations.

**Irreducible:** a nonzero representation with no proper nonzero invariant subspace.

**Faithful:** having trivial action kernel.

**Completely reducible:** isomorphic to a direct sum of irreducible representations.

**Character:** the trace function of a representation; it is constant on conjugacy classes.

**Regular representation:** the action on the vector space with basis indexed by G by left translation.

**Induced representation:** the G-module kG⊗_(kH)W constructed from an H-module W.

**Modular characteristic:** positive characteristic dividing the group order. The usual averaging denominator is then unavailable.
