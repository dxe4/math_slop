
# Notation and Conventions

G is finite unless explicitly stated otherwise, e is its identity, and k is a field. All vector spaces and representations are finite-dimensional unless an exception is stated. A representation is a homomorphism ρ:G→GL_k(V); write gv for ρ(g)v when the action is fixed. Products act from the left: (gh)v=g(hv). Permutations compose by applying the rightmost one first. Coordinates are column vectors.

A G-map, intertwiner or equivariant linear map T:V→W satisfies Tρ_V(g)=ρ_W(g)T for every g. The space of such maps is Hom_G(V,W). Irreducible means nonzero with no proper nonzero invariant subspace. Simple means the same in module language. Completely reducible means a direct sum of irreducibles. Faithful means that the action homomorphism has trivial kernel; it does not mean irreducible.

For complex representations, χ_V(g)=tr(ρ_V(g)). The inner product of class functions is
$$\langle f,h\rangle_G=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}f(g)\overline{h(g)}.$$
It is linear in the first argument. Hermitian products on vector spaces are also linear in the first argument. A class sum over representatives must include conjugacy-class sizes. Over an arbitrary field, taking a complex conjugate is undefined and is never silently inserted.

D_n has order 2n, with r^n=s²=e and srs=r^(−1). Thus D_4 is the eight-element square-symmetry group. Q_8 denotes the quaternion group. The standard representation of S_n is the sum-zero subspace of its coordinate permutation representation over characteristic zero.

For induction from H≤G, kG is a left kG–right kH bimodule and Ind_H^G W=kG⊗_(kH)W. Left coset representatives gH give its direct-sum model. The precise double-coset convention is stated in the induction chapter.
