This book develops convex formulations, separation, duality, first-order and Newton methods, constraints, conic models and regularised statistical problems. Read each algorithm with its stated function class, norm, feasible domain and stopping conclusion. The [reader guide](reader-guide.md) gives the ordered route and preparation; [Discrete and Global Optimisation](#/books/discrete-global-optimisation) continues into integer formulations and search.
