## Glossary {#section-5-glossary}

**Action functional.** $S[q] = \int_{t_{0}}^{t_{1}} L(q, \dot{q}, t)\,dt$. A scalar functional whose stationary points determine physical trajectories. Ch. 4.

**Action variable.** $I = \frac{1}{2\pi}\oint p\,dq$ for periodic motion. Adiabatically invariant under slow parameter changes. Ch. 13.

**Adiabatic invariant.** A quantity conserved to leading order when a Hamiltonian parameter changes slowly compared to the natural period. Ch. 13.

**Canonical transformation.** A phase-space transformation preserving the Poisson bracket structure. Equivalently, a symplectomorphism in Darboux coordinates. Ch. 8.

**Cyclic coordinate.** A coordinate on which the Lagrangian does not explicitly depend; its conjugate momentum is conserved. Ch. 4.

**Darboux coordinates.** Local coordinates $(q_{1}, \dots, q_{n}, p_{1}, \dots, p_{n})$ in which the symplectic form is $\omega = \sum_{i} dq_{i} \wedge dp_{i}$. Ch. 11.

**Darboux's theorem.** Every symplectic manifold is locally symplectomorphic to $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$ with the standard form; symplectic geometry has no local invariants. Ch. 11.

**Energy function.** $E = \sum_{i}\dot{q}_{i}p_{i} - L$. Equals the Hamiltonian $H$ when the Legendre transform is invertible. Ch. 4.

**Euler--Lagrange equation.** $\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{q}_{i}}\right) - \frac{\partial L}{\partial q_{i}} = 0$. The condition for stationary action. Ch. 4.

**Euler's equations.** $I_{1}\dot{\omega}_{1} = (I_{2} - I_{3})\omega_{2}\omega_{3}$ and cyclic permutations. Govern torque-free rigid body rotation in the body frame. Ch. 10.

**Exponential map.** $\exp(X) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} X^{k}/k!$ for $X \in \mathfrak{g}$. A local diffeomorphism from Lie algebra to Lie group. Ch. 12.

**Field strength tensor.** $F_{\mu\nu} = \partial_{\mu}A_{\nu} - \partial_{\nu}A_{\mu}$. The gauge-invariant electromagnetic field. Ch. 15.

**Gauge symmetry.** A local redundancy in the description of a field theory, not a physical symmetry in the Noether sense. Ch. 15, Ch. 16.

**Generalized coordinates.** $q = (q_{1}, \dots, q_{n})$ parameterizing the configuration manifold. Ch. 4.

**Generalized momentum.** $p_{i} = \partial L/\partial \dot{q}_{i}$. Ch. 4.

**Generating function.** A scalar function $F$ determining a canonical transformation via its partial derivatives. Four standard types ($F_{1}, F_{2}, F_{3}, F_{4}$). Ch. 8.

**Hamilton's equations.** $\dot{q}_{i} = \partial H/\partial p_{i}$, $\dot{p}_{i} = -\partial H/\partial q_{i}$. Equivalent to the Euler--Lagrange equations for regular Lagrangians. Ch. 7.

**Hamilton--Jacobi equation.** $H(q, \partial S/\partial q, t) + \partial S/\partial t = 0$. A partial differential equation whose complete integral generates the general solution of Hamilton's equations. Ch. 9.

**Hamiltonian.** $H(q, p, t) = \sum_{i} p_{i}\dot{q}_{i} - L$, defined by the Legendre transform. Ch. 7.

**Hamiltonian vector field.** $X_{H}$ defined by $\iota_{X_{H}}\omega = dH$. Its flow gives Hamilton's equations. Ch. 11.

**Holonomic constraint.** A constraint expressible as $f(q, t) = 0$. A regular family of independent constraints reduces the local degrees of freedom by its rank; singular constraints require separate analysis. Ch. 5.

**Inertia tensor.** $I_{ij} = \int\rho(\mathbf{a})(|\mathbf{a}|^{2}\delta_{ij} - a_{i}a_{j})\,d^{3}a$. Symmetric; diagonalizable to principal moments. Ch. 10.

**Integrable system (Liouville).** A Hamiltonian system with $n$ functionally independent conserved quantities in involution. Ch. 13.

**KAM theorem.** Kolmogorov--Arnold--Moser: for sufficiently small perturbations of a nondegenerate integrable system, most invariant tori survive. Ch. 14.

**Lagrangian.** $L(q, \dot{q}, t)$, typically $T - V$. Ch. 4.

**Lagrangian density.** $\mathcal{L}(\phi, \partial\phi, x)$. The spatial integral of $\mathcal{L}$ is the Lagrangian $L$. Ch. 15.

**Laplace--Runge--Lenz vector.** $\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{p} \times \mathbf{L} - \mu k\mathbf{r}/r$. A conserved vector for the attractive Kepler problem; hidden $SO(4)$ symmetry describes the negative-energy sector. Ch. 13.

**Legendre transform.** The map $(q, \dot{q}, t) \mapsto (q, p, t)$ with $p_{i} = \partial L/\partial \dot{q}_{i}$. It is locally invertible where the velocity Hessian is nonsingular; positive definiteness is sufficient but not necessary. Ch. 7.

**Lie algebra.** $\mathfrak{g} = T_{e}G$ equipped with the Lie bracket $[\cdot, \cdot]$. Ch. 12.

**Lie group.** A group that is also a smooth manifold, with smooth group operations. Examples: $SO(3)$, $SU(2)$, $GL(n, \mathbb{R})$. Ch. 12.

**Liouville's theorem.** Hamiltonian flow preserves the symplectic form and hence phase-space volume. Ch. 11.

**Moment map.** $\mu: M \to \mathfrak{g}^{*}$ for a Hamiltonian group action. Its components are conserved along a Hamiltonian flow when the Hamiltonian has the corresponding symmetry. Ch. 12.

**Noether current.** $j^{\mu} = \frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial(\partial_{\mu}\phi_{a})}\Psi_{a} - T^{\mu}_{\phantom{\mu}\nu}X^{\nu} - K^{\mu}$. Satisfies $\partial_{\mu}j^{\mu} = 0$ for symmetries of the action. Ch. 16.

**Noether's theorem.** Every continuous symmetry of the action corresponds to a conserved quantity (particle mechanics) or conserved current (field theory). Ch. 6, Ch. 12, Ch. 16.

**Nonholonomic constraint.** A constraint that cannot be written as $f(q, t) = 0$; typically velocity-dependent and non-integrable. Ch. 5.

**Phase space.** The $2n$-dimensional space with coordinates $(q_{1}, \dots, q_{n}, p_{1}, \dots, p_{n})$. Ch. 7.

**Poisson bracket.** $\{f, g\} = \sum_{i}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial q_{i}}\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_{i}} - \frac{\partial f}{\partial p_{i}}\frac{\partial g}{\partial q_{i}}\right)$. Ch. 8.

**Poincaré--Lindstedt method.** A perturbation technique that removes secular terms by allowing the frequency to depend on the perturbation parameter. Ch. 14.

**Regular Lagrangian.** A Lagrangian whose velocity Hessian $\partial^{2}L/\partial\dot{q}_{i}\partial\dot{q}_{j}$ is invertible. Ch. 7.

**Rigid body.** A system of particles with fixed relative distances. Configuration space includes center-of-mass position and rotation matrix $\Lambda \in SO(3)$. Ch. 10.

**Separation of variables.** Writing $S = \sum_{i}W_{i}(q_{i}, P) - Et$ in the Hamilton--Jacobi equation, reducing a PDE to ODEs. Ch. 9, Ch. 13.

**Small oscillations.** Approximation of motion near equilibrium by a quadratic Lagrangian; frequency $\omega = \sqrt{V''(q_{0})/m(q_{0})}$. Ch. 3.

**Stationary path.** A path for which the first variation of the action vanishes under fixed-endpoint perturbations. Ch. 4.

**Stormer--Verlet method.** A second-order, symplectic, time-reversible integrator for separable Hamiltonians. retained `geometric-numerical-integration` reading.

**Symplectic form.** A closed, nondegenerate 2-form $\omega$. The standard form is $\omega = \sum_{i}dq_{i} \wedge dp_{i}$. Ch. 11.

**Symplectic integrator.** A numerical method whose one-step map preserves the symplectic form. retained `geometric-numerical-integration` reading.

**Symplectic manifold.** A manifold $M$ equipped with a symplectic form $\omega$. Ch. 11.

**Symplectomorphism.** A diffeomorphism $\phi: M \to M$ preserving the symplectic form: $\phi^{*}\omega = \omega$. Ch. 11.

**Virtual work.** $\delta W = \sum_{i}F_{i}\delta q_{i}$. For ideal constraints, constraint forces do no virtual work. Ch. 5.
