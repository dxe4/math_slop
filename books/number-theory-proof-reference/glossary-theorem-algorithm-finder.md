
# Glossary, Theorem, and Algorithm Finder

This finder uses stable chapter identifiers rather than draft page numbers. Terms are brief retrieval cues, not replacements for hypotheses and proofs. Identifiers for analytic number theory, number fields, ideals and elliptic curves refer to the registered retained advanced readings, rather than to chapters in the current eighteen-chapter elementary route.

## Integer and modular vocabulary

**Bezout certificate.** Integers $x,y$ satisfying $ax+by=\gcd(a,b)$. It certifies the gcd and supports inverses. See `division-euclid-bezout`.

**Carmichael function.** $\lambda(n)$, the exponent of $U(n)$; every unit raised to it is one. See `units-orders-exponent-theorems`.

**Chinese remainder theorem.** Reconstruction of a residue from local classes. Noncoprime moduli require agreement modulo each gcd and give uniqueness modulo the lcm. See `congruences-linear-equations-crt`.

**Congruence.** $a\equiv b \bmod n$ means $n\mid a-b$. See `congruences-linear-equations-crt`.

**Divisibility.** $a\mid b$ means $b=ak$ for an integer witness $k$. See `integer-logic-induction-extremal`.

**Euclidean algorithm.** Repeated division with remainder preserving the gcd. See `division-euclid-bezout`.

**Euler totient.** $\varphi(n)=\lvert U(n)\rvert$; for $n=\prod p^e$, it is $n \prod_{p\mid n}(1-1/p)$. See `units-orders-exponent-theorems`.

**Generalized CRT.** Two residue conditions modulo $m,n$ are compatible exactly when $\gcd(m,n)$ divides the residue difference. See `congruences-linear-equations-crt`.

**Order.** Least positive $r$ with $a^r=1$ in a group. See `units-orders-exponent-theorems`.

**Prime.** Positive integer greater than one whose positive divisors are one and itself. Prime elements in other rings require a product-divisibility definition. See `primes-factorization-valuations` and `quadratic-integers-ideals`.

**Primitive root.** Generator of a cyclic unit group. Such roots modulo $n$ exist exactly for $1,2,4,p^k,2p^k$ with odd prime $p$. See `primitive-roots-cyclic-units`.

**Unit modulo $n$.** A residue with multiplicative inverse, equivalently one represented by an integer coprime to $n$. See `congruences-linear-equations-crt`.

**Valuation.** $v_{p}(n)$, the exponent of prime $p$ in a nonzero number. Products add valuations; sums obey an ultrametric inequality. See `primes-factorization-valuations`.

## Polynomial and residue vocabulary

**Hensel lifting.** Construction of roots modulo increasing prime powers. A nonzero derivative modulo $p$ gives a unique next digit. See `polynomial-congruences-prime-powers`.

**Jacobi symbol.** Multiplicative extension of Legendre symbols to odd composite denominators. Value one need not imply a square. See `quadratic-residues-reciprocity`.

**Legendre symbol.** $(a/p)$ records zero, nonzero quadratic residue, or nonresidue modulo odd prime $p$. See `quadratic-residues-reciprocity`.

**Quadratic reciprocity.** Relation between $(p/q)$ and $(q/p)$ for distinct odd primes, with a sign only when both are $3 \bmod 4$. See `quadratic-residues-reciprocity`.

**Singular root.** Polynomial root whose derivative is zero modulo the prime; it can branch or disappear during lifting. See `polynomial-congruences-prime-powers`.

## Diophantine vocabulary

**Affine lattice.** A base integer solution plus the integer kernel of a linear map. See `linear-diophantine-lattices`.

**Badly approximable.** An irrational with a positive lower bound $c/q^{2}$ for all rational approximation errors; equivalently bounded continued-fraction partial quotients. See `diophantine-approximation-transcendence-boundaries`.

**Continued fraction.** Nested integer and reciprocal expansion generated by Euclid. Convergents give canonical rational approximations. See `continued-fractions-pell`.

**Descent.** Construction of a strictly smaller positive-integer solution from a hypothetical solution. See `descent-valuations-local-obstructions`.

**Diophantine equation.** Polynomial equation seeking integer or rational solutions. Its domain is part of the problem. See Part III.

**Frobenius number.** For coprime integers $a,b>1$, the largest nonnegative integer not generated by $a,b$ is $ab-a-b$. See `linear-diophantine-lattices`.

**Liouville number.** An irrational real number $\alpha$ such that, for every positive integer $n$, there are integers $p,q$ with $q\ge2$ and $0<\lvert\alpha-p/q\rvert<q^{-n}$; every such number is transcendental. See `diophantine-approximation-transcendence-boundaries`.

**Local obstruction.** Failure over the reals, modulo a prime power, or over a completion, proving no global solution. Passing local tests is not generally sufficient. See `descent-valuations-local-obstructions`.

**Pell equation.** $x^{2}-Dy^{2}=1$ for positive nonsquare integer $D$; continued fractions find its fundamental solution. See `continued-fractions-pell`.

**Primitive Pythagorean triple.** Coprime positive solution of $x^{2}+y^{2}=z^{2}$, uniquely parametrized by coprime opposite-parity $m,n$. See `pythagorean-triples-sums-squares`.

**Transcendental number.** Complex number satisfying no nonzero integer polynomial. See `diophantine-approximation-transcendence-boundaries`.

## Arithmetic-function vocabulary

**Arithmetic function.** Function on positive integers. See `arithmetic-functions-convolution-inversion`.

**Average order.** A function matching another arithmetic function at the level of summatory asymptotics, not necessarily pointwise. See `average-orders-summation`.

**Dirichlet convolution.** $(f*g)(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}f(d)g(n/d)$. See `arithmetic-functions-convolution-inversion`.

**Dirichlet character.** Periodic, completely multiplicative function arising from a character of $U(q)$ and extended by zero to nonunits. See `characters-primes-progressions`.

**Möbius function.** $\mu(n)$ is zero on nonsquarefree integers and $(-1)^r$ on products of $r$ distinct primes. It inverts divisor summation. See `arithmetic-functions-convolution-inversion`.

**Partial summation.** Discrete integration by parts transferring estimates between weights. See `average-orders-summation`.

**Summatory function.** $F(x)=\sum_{n\le x}f(n)$. See `average-orders-summation`.

## Analytic vocabulary

**Abscissa of absolute convergence.** Boundary of the right half-plane where a Dirichlet series converges absolutely. See `dirichlet-series-euler-products`.

**Analytic continuation.** Extension of a function beyond its original formula's domain while agreeing on overlap. It does not make the original series converge there. See `dirichlet-series-euler-products`.

**Dirichlet $L$-function.** Series and Euler product attached to a character. Nonvanishing at one is decisive for primes in progressions. See `characters-primes-progressions`.

**Euler product.** Product of prime-power local factors representing a multiplicative Dirichlet series in an absolute-convergence region. See `dirichlet-series-euler-products`.

**Prime number theorem.** $\pi(x)\sim x/\log x$, equivalently $\theta(x)\sim x$ or $\psi(x)\sim x$. See `prime-distribution-sieves`.

**Riemann hypothesis.** Open conjecture that all nontrivial zeta zeros have real part one half. See `dirichlet-series-euler-products`.

**Sieve.** Inclusion-exclusion or weighted method removing integers with small prime divisors. Survival may mean rough or almost prime unless the sieve range is complete. See `prime-distribution-sieves`.

**Tauberian theorem.** Result recovering coefficient asymptotics from transform boundary behavior under extra hypotheses. See `dirichlet-series-euler-products`.

**Von Mangoldt function.** $\Lambda(p^k)=\log p$, zero otherwise; coefficients of the logarithmic derivative of zeta. See `dirichlet-series-euler-products`.

## Algebraic-number vocabulary

**Algebraic integer.** Root of a monic integer polynomial. See `number-fields-norms-prime-ideals`.

**Class group.** Nonzero fractional ideals modulo nonzero principal fractional ideals; it measures failure of ideals to be principal. See `quadratic-integers-ideals`.

**Dedekind domain.** Domain in which nonzero proper ideals factor uniquely into prime ideals; rings of integers of number fields are examples. See `quadratic-integers-ideals`.

**Discriminant.** Determinant of the trace pairing on an integral basis; its prime divisors are exactly ramified rational primes. See `number-fields-norms-prime-ideals`.

**Ideal norm.** For a nonzero integral ideal $I$, $N(I)=\lvert \mathcal{O}_{K}/I\rvert$ is a positive integer, multiplicative under ideal products. See `number-fields-norms-prime-ideals`.

**Integral basis.** $\mathbb{Z}$-basis of the full ring of integers. A convenient power basis need not be integral. See `number-fields-norms-prime-ideals`.

**Number field.** Finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}$. See `number-fields-norms-prime-ideals`.

**Ramification index.** Exponent $e_{i}$ of a prime ideal above $p$. Together with residue degrees it satisfies $\sum e_{i} f_{i}=[K:\mathbb{Q}]$. See `number-fields-norms-prime-ideals`.

**Residue degree.** Degree $f_{i}$ of $\mathcal{O}_{K}/p_{i}$ over $\mathbb{F}_{p}$. See `number-fields-norms-prime-ideals`.

**Split, inert, ramified.** Three prime-decomposition behaviors, defined by the prime ideal factorization rather than informal polynomial appearance alone. See `number-fields-norms-prime-ideals`.

## Elliptic and algorithmic vocabulary

**Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture.** Open general relation between the central vanishing of an elliptic $L$-function and arithmetic rank, with a leading-coefficient formula. See `elliptic-curves-group-descent`.

**Canonical height.** Quadratic height with $\widehat{h}([n]P)=n^{2}\widehat{h}(P)$. See `elliptic-curves-group-descent`.

**Elliptic curve.** Nonsingular genus-one curve with chosen point; in short form, a nonsingular cubic plus $O$. See `elliptic-curves-group-descent`.

**Hasse bound.** $\lvert \#E(\mathbb{F}_{q})-(q+1)\rvert\le 2\sqrt{q}$. See `elliptic-curves-group-descent`.

**Mordell-Weil group.** Finitely generated group $E(K)$ of rational points over a number field. See `elliptic-curves-group-descent`.

**Selmer group.** Computable local-condition group containing the descent image and giving a rank upper bound. See `elliptic-curves-group-descent`.

**Certificate.** Finite proof object checked independently of its producer. See `algorithms-certificates-crypto-boundaries`.

**Miller-Rabin witness.** Base whose strong probable-prime sequence proves an odd input composite. A nonwitness is not generally a primality proof. See `algorithms-certificates-crypto-boundaries`.

**Pratt certificate.** Recursive primality certificate using a factored $p-1$ and an element proved to have order $p-1$. See `algorithms-certificates-crypto-boundaries`.

**Security reduction.** Conditional algorithm turning a scheme-breaking adversary into a solver for a named hard problem. See `algorithms-certificates-crypto-boundaries`.

## Theorem finder by conclusion

### Existence

- prime divisor and prime factorization: `integer-logic-induction-extremal` and `primes-factorization-valuations`;
- integer linear solution: `division-euclid-bezout` and `linear-diophantine-lattices`;
- linear congruence: `congruences-linear-equations-crt`;
- simultaneous residue: `congruences-linear-equations-crt`;
- polynomial lift from a simple root: `polynomial-congruences-prime-powers`;
- square root modulo an odd prime: `quadratic-residues-reciprocity`;
- primitive root: `primitive-roots-cyclic-units`, only for classified moduli;
- Pell solution: `continued-fractions-pell` for positive nonsquare integer $D$;
- sum of two squares: `sums-two-four-squares` via prime-exponent criterion;
- prime in every reduced progression: `characters-primes-progressions` (retained reading);
- rational-point finite generation: `elliptic-curves-group-descent` (retained reading), not an explicit generator algorithm.

### Uniqueness or exact count

- quotient and remainder: `division-euclid-bezout`;
- prime factorization: `primes-factorization-valuations`, up to order;
- linear-congruence count: `congruences-linear-equations-crt`;
- generalized CRT modulus: `congruences-linear-equations-crt`;
- simple Hensel lift: `polynomial-congruences-prime-powers`;
- roots of a nonzero polynomial over a field: `polynomial-congruences-prime-powers`;
- primitive-triple parameters: `pythagorean-triples-rational-points` after conventions;
- prime ideal factorization: `quadratic-integers-ideals` and `number-fields-norms-prime-ideals` (retained readings).

### Obstruction

- gcd divisibility: `division-euclid-bezout`, `congruences-linear-equations-crt`, and `linear-diophantine-lattices`;
- valuation parity: `primes-factorization-valuations` and `descent-valuations-local-obstructions`;
- quadratic nonresidue: `quadratic-residues-reciprocity`;
- local congruence or prime-power failure: `descent-valuations-local-obstructions`;
- two-square prime exponent: `sums-two-four-squares`;
- discriminant singularity: `elliptic-curves-group-descent` (retained reading);
- failed Fermat or Miller-Rabin test: `units-orders-exponent-theorems` and `primality-tests-certificates`.

### Asymptotic or distribution

- summatory divisor and totient main terms: `average-orders-summation` (retained reading);
- primes in reduced progressions: `characters-primes-progressions` (retained reading);
- Euler-product coefficient transforms: `dirichlet-series-euler-products` (retained reading);
- prime number theorem and sieve bounds: `prime-distribution-sieves` (retained reading).
