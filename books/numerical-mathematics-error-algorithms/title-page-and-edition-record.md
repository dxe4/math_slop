This is the self-contained first-course volume in the Math Atlas numerical shelf. It follows error from finite arithmetic through roots, approximation, quadrature, linear algebra, time stepping, boundary-value discretization, inverse problems, and reproducible software audits. *Optimization by Structure: Continuous, Discrete, and Certified Methods* is its complementary advanced reference; it follows structural certificates rather than the error ledger used here.
