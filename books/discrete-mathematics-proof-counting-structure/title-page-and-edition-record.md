This is the self-contained first-course volume in the Math Atlas discrete-mathematics shelf. It develops proof, counting, recurrence, graph structure, finite orders, designs, matroids, exact experiments, and cumulative projects. Its complementary volume, *Combinatorial Structures: Graphs, Extremal Methods, and Matroids*, assumes more proof maturity and organizes the field around structural theorems, exchange, counterexamples, and exact certificates.
