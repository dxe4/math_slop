## Structural research routes

**Inverse branching:** exact predecessor congruences, layer multiplicity,
collision warnings, and branching heuristics in [sec-collatz-inverse-branching](34-inverse-branching.md#sec-collatz-inverse-branching).

**Rare excursions:** exact binomial tails, Chernoff bounds, rate functions,
model-to-map caveats, and finite data checks in [source section](../collatz-conjecture/35-large-deviations.md#sec-collatz-large-deviations).

**Extremal averages:** weighted cycle means, invariant measures, coboundary
potentials, and finite certificate scope in [source section](../collatz-conjecture/36-ergodic-optimization.md#sec-collatz-ergodic-optimization).

**Machine-checked logic:** propositional encodings, SAT/SMT certificates, proof
assistants, trusted kernels, and specification risk in
[sec-collatz-formal-verification](37-formal-verification.md#sec-collatz-formal-verification).

**Verified datasets:** integer kernels, parallel shards, manifests, hashes,
independent checkers, and performance claims in [sec-collatz-verified-datasets](38-verified-datasets.md#sec-collatz-verified-datasets).

**Neighboring maps:** accelerated and affine relatives, invariant residue
classes, cycles, escape mechanisms, theorem-status comparison, and the limits of
analogy in [sec-collatz-neighboring-maps](39-neighboring-maps.md#sec-collatz-neighboring-maps).

The six routes begin with local repair kits for branching processes, probability,
graph optimization, formal logic, parallel computing, and dynamical systems.
These research routes assume proof fluency, congruence arithmetic, elementary real analysis and probability, finite graphs and algorithms. Use the [toolkit's preparation guide](#/books/collatz-research-toolkit) before the local repair kits; brief introductions do not replace those foundations.

1. parity structure: Chapters 4, 21, 40, 41;

## Executable Python route

The [Collatz Python laboratory](h-python-laboratory.md) supplies twenty-two
self-contained programs for exact orbit prefixes, stopping-time censoring,
shortcut valuations, parity words, affine branches, residue transitions,
inverse predecessors, finite interval verification, partition coverage,
cross-implementation checks, excursion records, first descent, inverse layers,
bounded cycle filters, provenance-aware tables, affine-word coefficients,
binomial parity counts, survival curves, logarithmic drift, and cycle
denominators. The final status column and work limits are part of every
computational claim; no example is presented as a proof of universal
convergence.

Formula companions inside the numbered chapters add direct implementations of
constrained-language coefficients and affine thresholds, Shannon information
budgets, rational drift certificates, maximal couplings and overlap variance,
fixed-horizon semilinear tables, and rational interval enclosures. Read the code
immediately after the formula it translates; use the appendix route when you
want a longer end-to-end laboratory.
