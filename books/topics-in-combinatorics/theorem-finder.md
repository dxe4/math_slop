Every primary chapter ends with ten exercises. Their two hints suggest an
entry and a decisive step; the complete solution supplies the proof or
calculation. The solutions remain beside their questions to preserve the
meaning and identity of each exercise rather than duplicating a separate
answer sequence here.

For a proposed application, record the finite sets or distributions,
field, normalization and limiting parameters. Then identify the theorem's
actual conclusion: count, bound, certificate, structure or existence.
An example, a coefficient identity and an asymptotic do not establish
one another without the connecting argument.
