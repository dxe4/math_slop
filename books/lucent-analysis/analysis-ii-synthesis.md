# Real-functions examination: limits of theorems

**Role:** cumulative written assessment after continuity, differentiation, Riemann integration, elementary functions, and uniform function limits. Suggested time: 130 minutes; 50 marks. Attempt every task before opening hints. Written proofs are saved for self or instructor review, without automatic proof certification.

The rubric under each task allocates its ten marks. A conclusion without its hypotheses and argument earns at most two marks.

**Outcomes assessed:** A6 uniform continuity; A8 integrability; A9 elementary functions; A10 uniform limits and differentiation. Problems 1–4 are unseen transfer; Problem 5 is deliberate retrieval of the logarithm construction.

## Examination problems
