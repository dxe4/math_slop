# Metric continuation examination: compactness and approximation

**Role:** cumulative written assessment after metric spaces, contraction mappings, constructive approximation, and the Picard application. Suggested time: 120 minutes; 40 marks. Attempt each task before hints. Written proofs are saved for self or instructor review and are not automatically certified.

Each task has its own ten-mark rubric. State the domain and hypotheses that make a chosen theorem applicable.

**Outcomes assessed:** A11 completeness, total boundedness and uniform maps; A12 fixed-point estimates, approximation and ODEs.
