
# References and Source Lineage {#section-1-references-and-source-lineage}

The syllabus benchmarks are Elias M. Stein and Rami Shakarchi, Fourier Analysis: An Introduction, and Gerald B. Folland’s treatments of Fourier and real analysis. They identify established scope; their exercises and proprietary exposition are not reproduced.

The developed periodic and finite-transform chapters are original exposition with original supported exercises. Local lineage includes the retained Real Analysis Fourier chapter, the Quarto real-analysis Fourier/distribution readings and the Quarto calculus transform chapter. The original Real Analysis Fourier source has moved into this family and its sections have been cut into the corresponding coefficient, Dirichlet, Fejér, mean-square, Gibbs, transform and discrete chapters. All twenty ANA-CH21 exercise identities, hints, complete solutions and its project remain together in chapter 2. Their mathematical content is preserved; the retained measure-based formulation states its additional density and Tonelli inputs.

The retained Functions and Equations chapter `chapter-13` is a local predecessor for the periodic and whole-line development. Its cycles-per-unit transform convention is related to this book’s angular frequency by ξ=2πν. The transform and inversion chapters state that conversion explicitly and preserve the original source.

The first projection chapter keeps the commissioned chapter identity fourier-approximation-and-density in the new book. The Real Analysis native manifest declares the old full chapter resource as an alias to this new chapter resource, preserving its one semantic destination. The old physical source path is retired by the transfer. No learner records are overwritten or merged by authoring the text.
