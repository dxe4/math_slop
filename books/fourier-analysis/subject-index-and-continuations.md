
# Subject Index and Continuations {#section-1-subject-index-and-continuations}

The developed coefficient chapter covers normalization, orthogonality, projection, finite Bessel identities, phase rules, period changes, derivative/jump terms, correlation and finite mode selection. The Dirichlet chapter proves piecewise C1 jump-average convergence, local uniformity, kernel norm growth, localization and a Dini sufficient condition.

The Gibbs chapter proves the sine-integral scaling profile, positive jump fraction, canonical finite-degree error and the range protection supplied by Fejér positivity. The discrete chapter proves inversion, Parseval, butterflies, operation counts, convolution padding and aliasing under explicit hypotheses.

All fifteen chapter sources now exist, including the real-line, sampling, uncertainty, distribution, character and application arguments identified in the theorem finder.

For equations use [Classical PDE](#/books/partial-differential-equations), whose full twelve-chapter classical manuscript is authored. [Functional Analysis](#/books/functional-analysis) develops the general Hilbert projection and compact spectral mechanisms. Measure-based extensions require [Measure and Integration](#/books/measure-and-integration) with its actual available scope.
