
# Glossary

A pi-system is closed under finite intersections. A lambda-system contains the whole space and is closed under complements and countable disjoint unions. Their interaction supplies the event-extension argument of Chapter 1.

Uniform integrability is uniform disappearance of absolute tail integrals. It turns convergence in probability into first-mean convergence under the Vitali criterion. A de la Vallee Poussin function is a convex superlinear growth function whose expectations uniformly bound the family.

A triangular array has a changing finite row for each sample size. Lindeberg controls variance contributed by large row entries; Feller controls the largest individual variance. Their roles in necessity and sufficiency are separately stated in Chapter 9.

An upcrossing records a completed passage from below a lower level to above a higher one. Finite expected upcrossings across rational levels control oscillation. A closed martingale is the conditional-expectation process of one terminal integrable variable; uniform integrability is what supports that representation at the limit.

An invariant event is unchanged under the time shift modulo a null event. Ergodicity makes every invariant event trivial. Stationarity preserves finite-dimensional joint laws under shifts and does not itself make time averages deterministic.

A Feller kernel preserves bounded continuous functions in the setting where it is invoked. A global Doeblin minorization shares one positive probability component across all starting states. Detailed balance is symmetry of the stationary pair measure and proves invariance, independently of any convergence claim.

A modification agrees at every prescribed time outside a time-dependent null event. Indistinguishability means one common full-probability event gives equality at all times. Continuous modifications agree indistinguishably when they live on the same probability space.

A rate function is a nonnegative lower-semicontinuous function governing logarithmic event probabilities. A good rate has compact sublevels. Exponential tilting changes the law by a normalised exponential likelihood, making a selected rare average typical under the changed measure.
