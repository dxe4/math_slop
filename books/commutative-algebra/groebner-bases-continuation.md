
# Gröbner Bases: Computational Continuation

The computational treatment is [Gröbner Bases and Elimination](#/books/finite-algebra-computation/groebner-bases-elimination). It proves the normal-form and Buchberger criteria, Dickson termination and the elimination theorem, with exact examples and supported exercises. Read that chapter when you need a finite generating calculation; this book supplies the commutative-algebra hypotheses and structural context. This commissioned bridge directs you to the single teaching home rather than duplicating its manuscript.
