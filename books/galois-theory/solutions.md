
# Using the Solutions

Exercise hints and complete solutions appear directly beside each authored exercise in staged disclosures. Begin with the statement and attempt a proof before opening a hint. A solution that invokes the tower law must identify all fields in its tower; a splitting-field calculation must prove generation and the degree rather than only list roots. The prerequisite diagnostic includes its own full answer disclosure. Each of the thirteen main chapters now supplies its own exercise support. Older exercises and learner records remain attached to their original registered sources.
