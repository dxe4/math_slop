# Advanced Analysis Topics

This **L5 specialist continuation** collects fifteen substantial readings at the
intersection of measure, functional analysis, Fourier analysis, geometry and
probability. It assumes completed proof-based analysis. A brief repair kit in a
chapter refreshes terminology; it does not remove the preparation needed to use
that chapter's theorems or applications.

The starting Euler texts are [Real Analysis](#/books/proof-safe-real-analysis),
[Measure and Integration](#/books/measure-and-integration) and
[Functional Analysis](#/books/functional-analysis). Their relevant chapters
should be completed before entering the corresponding specialist topic.
[Fourier Analysis](#/books/fourier-analysis),
[Complex Analysis](#/books/complex-analysis) and
[Probability: Models, Bounds, and Proofs](#/books/probability-models-proofs)
provide additional preparation where specified below. Mathematical prerequisites are distinct from reading marks.

The order below retains the original source sequence. It is a dependency guide,
not a claim that every topic has the same prerequisites or that every reader
must read the entire volume linearly.

One functional-analytic route follows Radon representation, convex duality,
semigroups, unbounded spectral theory, Banach-valued integration, Fredholm theory
and extreme points. A probability route follows Radon representation, weak
convergence, Polish spaces/disintegration, transport, ergodic theory and
stochastic processes. Interpolation and geometric measure readings draw on
separate Fourier and Euclidean preparation. At a cross-reference, verify the
referenced theorem's hypotheses instead of treating a shared word such as
compactness or duality as sufficient preparation.

## Source identities and the shorter analysis route

These readings retain the semantic identities, mathematical exposition,
exercises and solutions of original Real Analysis chapters **33–47**. Their
numbered filenames record that lineage. They form this separate specialist
volume; they do not change the L3 label of the shorter
[Quarto Real Analysis route](#/books/real-analysis).

That shorter route uses its introductory calculus bridge and chapters 1–14
excluding the measure and functional-analysis vista chapters 11 and 13. Original
chapters 15–32 remain retained source continuations with their existing
identities. Links back to those readings name supporting material, not an
additional unannounced primary sequence. The source registration preserves the
old chapter identities and reading places rather than binding them to positions
in this table.

For each reading, keep a hypothesis ledger: the domain and its topology, the
measurable structure, finite or sigma-finite mass assumptions, the operator
domain where relevant, and the mode of convergence in the conclusion. The
chapter's proof and counterexamples determine the scope of a result. Numerical
or visual illustrations remain illustrations unless accompanied by the exact
argument needed for the claim.
