
# Sources and Further Reading

The explanations, worked cases and exercise sequence in this book are new
exposition commissioned by roadmap task 13.A31. Two scope benchmarks are:

- I. M. Gelfand and A. Shen, *Algebra*.
- OpenStax, *Prealgebra*.

These identify the surrounding elementary curriculum; they do not transfer
another work's exercise wording or sequence into this book.

Two existing Euler courses provide local context for the commission and
remain available in their original form:

- [Everyday Number Sense: Estimate, Compare, and Decide](../../course-texts/atlas-everyday-number-sense.md), for quantities, fractions, rates, estimation and contextual comparisons.
- [Signed Numbers to Equations](../../course-texts/atlas-signed-numbers-to-equations.md), for directed number-line changes and the later transition to equations.

Counting, decimal grouping and elementary geometric area arguments are developed
in the chapters themselves. Examples are specified mathematical models: pack
prices assume the stated contents and charges; proportional models state their
fixed rate; measurement ranges account for the named uncertainty; geometric
regions use their supplied dimensions. Changing those assumptions can change
the conclusion.

For the next sustained route, use
[Algebra from Stories](#/books/algebra-from-stories).
For a shorter contextual return to the material, use the two courses above.
For proof language later on, [Introduction to Proof](#/books/introduction-to-proof)
provides the next level of formal argument. It is further study, not a
prerequisite for this arithmetic book.
