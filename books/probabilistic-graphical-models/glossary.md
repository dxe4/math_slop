
# Glossary

A graphical model is a family of joint laws with specified factorization restrictions. A conditional-independence statement factors a conditional law, while a covariance zero records only a second-moment relation unless extra Gaussian structure is present.

D-separation blocks every path by its noncollider and activated-collider rules. Faithfulness says that the graph also captures every independence of the particular law. A moral graph joins co-parents after the relevant ancestral restriction. A Markov blanket is sufficient neighboring information for a vertex full conditional in its model.

A potential is an unnormalized nonnegative factor. A partition function normalizes their product. Running intersection requires bags containing the same variable to form a connected subtree. An elimination order induces fill edges and a computational width; the minimal such width is treewidth.

Filtering conditions on the observed past and present. Smoothing additionally uses future observations. A Viterbi path is a whole-sequence posterior mode. Posterior path sampling draws a complete random sequence from its conditional law.

The ELBO is log evidence minus reverse divergence to the posterior. Mean field restricts the approximate distribution to a product. A coordinate-stationary point need not be a global maximum. An EM responsibility is a posterior component probability, and an expected count is its sum over observations or transitions.

Detailed balance makes a target pair flow symmetric. Invariance alone does not establish convergence from a chosen initial state. A minorization supplies a common positive transition component. A self-normalized importance estimate is a ratio of weighted sample sums and is generally biased in finite samples.

A CPDAG represents a class of observationally equivalent DAGs with only compelled arrows directed. Compatible Dirichlet priors link graph scores through one reference joint distribution. BIC is a penalized criterion with a regular Laplace interpretation only under its stated assumptions.

A structural intervention replaces a generating equation while preserving the declared remaining mechanisms. Backdoor adjustment uses pretreatment separation and positivity to identify its distribution. Counterfactual joint laws can require information beyond the marginal intervention laws identified by randomization.
