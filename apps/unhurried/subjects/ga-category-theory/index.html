Category theory

Objects, arrows and universal constructions in concrete examples. A selected gateway through path categories, finite commuting squares and a concrete functor. A proof-oriented route from finite functors and naturality to products and pullbacks in Set. A sustained proof dossier on Set products and pullbacks, the free-monoid adjunction, a finite Yoneda case and Cartesian interchange. Begin with functions; sets; reading a short proof. Its destination is modest and checkable: build one path category, decide whether a finite square commutes, and verify one functor. You will verify a functor and a natural transformation, then prove a Set product and pullback universal property. It assumes that you can read category and functor definitions, prove equality of functions, work with monoids and track contravariant arrows.
