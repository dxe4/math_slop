Linear algebra

Vectors, matrices and the geometry of linear maps. A first route through displacement, basis-arrow maps and small systems, ending with a two-step grid transformation. A geometric refresher connecting coordinates, systems, determinant, rank and real eigenlines. A proof-focused route through finite-dimensional rank-nullity, least-squares orthogonality and 2D singular values. Begin with signed numbers; coordinates. This route begins with walks on a coordinate grid and ends with a complete two-step linear transformation. You will add displacements, use the images of two unit arrows to move any point, and solve a pair of equations to recover an input. This route is for someone who has seen algebra and coordinates before and wants working fluency with real 2×2 maps. You will translate between vectors, basis coordinates, equations and matrices; diagnose invertibility through determinant, kernel and rank; and identify genuine eigenlines. This is a sustained, selected route for a reader already comfortable with 2×2 matrices, independent vectors, dot products and proof. Its main deliverables are two complete arguments: rank-nullity for a finite-dimensional domain and least-squares orthogonality under full column rank.
