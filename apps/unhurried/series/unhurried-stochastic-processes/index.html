Stochastic processes

Random paths, memory, arrivals and queues in small toy worlds. Four-lesson sampler linking finite paths, state sufficiency, matrix updates and Poisson counts. Seven-lesson route through hitting recurrences, stationary checks and the Poisson count–wait connection. All nine lessons with proofs for bounded walk, chain, M/M/1 and branching examples. Begin with conditional probability and expected value. This route asks two compact questions: where can a token land after a few random steps, and what can a small state table say about tomorrow? It then switches from steps to arrivals, where the random quantity is a count during an interval. It is for a reader comfortable with fractions and conditional probability who wants to calculate more than a few sample paths. This bounded deep route uses all nine topic lessons: finite walks and Markov chains, continuous-time arrivals and a single-server queue, and a branching family tree.
