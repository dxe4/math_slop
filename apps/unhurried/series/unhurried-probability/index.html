Probability

Chance from countable outcomes to distributions; each exploration uses small games and explicit assumptions. Count finite events, update after a clue, and score a two-dice game. Connect conditional updates, independence, moments, and binomial counts. Prove finite identities and qualify a normal approximation for a count. Begin with fractions and simple algebra. This sampler takes four lessons and one original two-dice design. Its bounded goal is to count a finite event, handle an overlapping condition, update a chance after a clue, and calculate the average score of a small game. This route joins eight lessons into one chain: finite event counts, conditional updates, independence, weighted scores, spread, and binomial counts. This deep route uses all nine probability lessons. It asks you to prove finite identities, derive a conditional update and moment formula, and state precisely what a smooth approximation does and does not say.
