Mathematical modelling

Build, test and critique models through art, sound, networks and physical sketches. A four-hour sampler in assumptions, dimensional scaling, finite sensitivity and reproducible dot rules, ending in a mural estimate and seeded exhibit card. A nine-hour selected route through ideal beat signals, a fictional network and held-out checks, with a two-panel modelling capstone. A sixteen-hour proof and validation route covering all nine modelling lessons, ending in a sound-and-network exhibit with an assumptions memo. Begin with functions, units and basic probability. It covers four selected lessons, not all of mathematical modelling. Its outcome is to derive a beat envelope, calculate a one-step network-spread probability, and test a chosen model on observations kept out of fitting. “Deep” means sustained work on the named results: dimensional scaling, local elasticity, a sine-sum identity, a conditional independence product, a pendulum approximation, and held-out error calculations.
