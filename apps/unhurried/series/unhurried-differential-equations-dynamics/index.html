Differential equations and dynamical systems

Understand evolving systems through solutions, phase portraits and stability. A bounded first route through cooling, fields, logistic growth and spring motion with model comparisons. A selective refresher of scalar equations, linear oscillation and local stability with exact and numerical checks. A proof route through a local contraction theorem, nonuniqueness, selected stability, a fold and a discrete map. Begin with Derivatives; Exponentials; Graphs. This route is for a reader who knows a derivative as a rate and has seen exponentials and basic graphs. Its outcome is modest and useful: turn two simple changing-system rules into checked predictions, read which way a state moves without solving first, and distinguish settling from oscillating in a linear spring. This route assumes prior exposure to first-order equations, characteristic roots and 2×2 matrices. It is a refresher focused on seven selected lessons, not a general differential-equations course. This is a selected proof route through local existence and uniqueness, scalar and planar stability, a fold, and one discrete map. It assumes comfort with derivatives, definite integrals, uniform convergence, completeness of real numbers and 2×2 eigenvalues.
