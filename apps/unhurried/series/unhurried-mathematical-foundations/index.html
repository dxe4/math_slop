Mathematical foundations and proof

A gentle toolkit for reading definitions, making conjectures, finding counterexamples, and writing finite proofs. Read definitions, refute one overbroad claim, and build a first direct proof from integer witnesses. Refresh contrapositive and induction while keeping existence and uniqueness obligations separate. Practice strong induction, invariants, and bijections through a proof portfolio with explicit scope checks. This course is for someone who can do integer arithmetic but has not written a proof recently. This refresher assumes school algebra is comfortable and asks for written proofs, not only correct answers. It assumes you have written a direct proof and an ordinary induction proof before.

About 150 minutes of reading and article exercises.
