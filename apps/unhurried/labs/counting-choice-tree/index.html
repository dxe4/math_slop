Choice tree and overcount

How does deciding whether order matters change a count of distinct picks?

Make a prediction

Ordered paths number n!/(n−k)!; each unordered k-set has exactly k! such paths.

A worked setting

Controls: Distinct available labels: 4; Labels to pick: 2; Order matters: Yes; List every outcome: No

What the result means

Pick k distinct labels without replacement from n labels. The explicit outcomes come from the branching choice tree.

Where the model stops

The division by k! needs distinct selections with no replacement. It does not apply unchanged to repeated picks or unequal symmetries.
