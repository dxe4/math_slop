Choose a delta that keeps the promise

Does a proposed δ certify the ε promise for every nearby x?

Make a prediction

For 2x, δ≤ε/2 suffices. For x² at a, δ(2|a|+δ)≤ε suffices.

A worked setting

Controls: Function: x²; Target ε: 0.5; Proposed δ: 0.25; Base point a: 1

For f(x)=x² at a=1, the proposed δ=0.25 does not certify ε=0.5 by the displayed all-points bound.

What the result means

An algebraic bound covers all 0<|x−a|<δ; checking finitely many sample points would not.

Where the model stops

The table certifies the specified global functions only. Equality in the bound is allowed because the δ-neighborhood is strict.
