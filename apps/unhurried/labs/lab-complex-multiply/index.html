Turn and stretch with one number

What do magnitude and angle do to a complex number?

Make a prediction

Multiplication scales length by |w| and adds arguments modulo a full turn when z and w are nonzero.

A worked setting

Controls: Input real part: 1; Input imaginary part: 1; Multiplier magnitude: 2; Multiplier angle (degrees): 90

Product = -2 + 2i; its length is 2.8284271. Its principal angle is 135°.

(a+bi)·r(cos θ+i sin θ) = r(a cos θ−b sin θ)+ir(a sin θ+b cos θ); arg(zw)≡arg(z)+θ mod 360° when z,w≠0.

What the result means

The Cartesian product is computed from w=r(cos θ+i sin θ), so the turn and stretch can be checked independently.

Where the model stops

Arguments are undefined at zero. Reported angles and trigonometric components are floating-point approximations.
