What happens to V − E + F when one triangular face is subdivided?

Make a prediction

A face subdivision adds one vertex, three edges and two net faces, so χ stays fixed.

What the result means

The sphere preset is the tetrahedron boundary. The torus preset is a 3 × 3 periodic square grid, each square cut along the same diagonal. Opposite square-grid edges are identified. Inserting one vertex inside a triangular face replaces that face by three triangles.

Where the model stops

These are closed triangulated surfaces with specified edge identifications. Missing faces, boundary or nonmanifold gluing change the setting; χ by itself does not classify every space.
