A lower bound and a feasible answer

When do a candidate solution and dual multipliers certify a gap?

Make a prediction

A primal feasible objective is at least every dual feasible bound. Matching values certify an optimum.

A worked setting

Controls: Objective preset: Minimize x+y; Primal candidate x: 1; Primal candidate y: 1; Dual demand multiplier u: 1; Dual x upper-bound multiplier v: 0; Dual y upper-bound multiplier w: 0

Both candidates are feasible. The primal value is 2, the dual lower bound is 2, and the certificate gap is 0.

Primal: min 1x+1y, x+y≥2, 0≤x,y≤3. Dual: max 2u−3v−3w, u−v≤1, u−w≤1, u,v,w≥0.

What the result means

Minimize c₁x+c₂y with x+y≥2 and 0≤x,y≤3. Dual variables cover demand and the two upper bounds.

Where the model stops

A gap is reported only when both sides are feasible. Quarter-grid controls make the displayed inequalities exact binary-rational checks for these presets.
