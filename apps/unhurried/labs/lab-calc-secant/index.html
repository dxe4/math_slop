From secant to tangent

What slope does a nearby secant have, and what happens at a corner?

Make a prediction

For x² the exact slope is 2a+h. At the origin, |x| has opposite one-sided slopes.

A worked setting

Controls: Function: x²; Base point a: 0; Distance |h|: 0.5; Approach side: Right (+h)

The signed secant slope is 0.5; the derivative at a is 0.

(f(a+h)−f(a))/h = 2a+h for h≠0.

What the result means

The quotient uses nonzero signed h. Comparing either side of the base point exposes the corner.

Where the model stops

Finite h values illustrate a limit; they do not prove one. Very small direct floating-point subtractions can lose digits.
