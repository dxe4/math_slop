Why do equal compass arcs bisect an angle?

Make a prediction

When OA=OB and AP=BP, triangles OAP and OBP are congruent by SSS.

A worked setting

Controls: Opening angle (degrees): 70; Construction stage: Join intersection; Arc radii: Equal

Equal arcs yield 35° on each side of OP. The numeric construction illustrates the SSS proof.

OA = OB = 1; AP = 0.8604; BP = 0.8604; OP is shared. Thus △OAP ≅ △OBP by SSS, so the angle halves are equal.

What the result means

Choose equal marks A and B on the rays. Intersect two circles centered there, then join their intersection P to O. Equal radii supply AP=BP; OP is shared, so SSS proves equal angles.

Where the model stops

This model uses ideal Euclidean coordinates; drawn circles and measured angles are illustrations. Unequal arc radii remove the SSS premise. The degenerate zero angle is excluded.
