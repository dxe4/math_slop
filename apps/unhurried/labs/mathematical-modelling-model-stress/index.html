Stress-test a toy network model

How does a one-time transmission rule change spread on a tiny network?

Make a prediction

Increasing the edge-open chance cannot reduce spread when the same random edge draws are reused.

A worked setting

Controls: Graph edges: ABC triangle with C–D, D–E, D–F; Edge-open chance: 0.4; Repeated networks: 300; Random seed: 17

Edges: A–B, A–C, B–C, C–D, D–E, D–F. Exact mean spread 2.34912 of 6 nodes; sampled mean 2.26333 over 300 networks. First run layers: 0: A; 1: C; 2: D; 3: E. Matched seeded edge draws make the sampled low/central/high means monotone.

P(edge open)=p independently; reached nodes = component of A in open-edge graph; exact E[spread]=ΣₖkP(spread=k)

What the result means

Start at A. Each undirected edge independently opens with probability p; all nodes connected to A through open edges are reached. This is a finite bond-percolation model.

Where the model stops

The rule omits timing, repeated contacts and behavior. It is an illustrative network model, not a forecast or causal estimate. Exact distribution enumerates at most 64 edge patterns.
