Playlist mood chain

How do three playlist moods change under one-step transition rules?

Make a prediction

Row normalization keeps each step a distribution; a stationary distribution can exist even when the sequence cycles.

What the result means

Each row gives relative weights for the next mood and is normalized to sum to one. The start weights are normalized likewise.

Where the model stops

Stationarity means πP=π. It alone does not ensure convergence from a given start; zero weights can make the stationary distribution non-unique.
