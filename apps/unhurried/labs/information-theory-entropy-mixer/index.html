Mix an alphabet

How much surprise does a chosen alphabet carry, and what does a second clue add?

Make a prediction

A more balanced distribution has higher entropy; a noisy parity clue adds conditional uncertainty.

A worked setting

Controls: Alphabet size: 3; A weight: 5; B weight: 3; C weight: 2; D weight: 0; Second variable Y: None; Parity-flip chance: 0.1

H(X) = 1.48548 bits. Enable Y to inspect the chain rule.

I(x)=−log₂P(x); H(X)=ΣₓP(x)I(x); H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y|X)

What the result means

Weights become stipulated source probabilities. Optional Y is the parity of the symbol index, independently flipped with the chosen chance.

Where the model stops

These are model probabilities, not frequencies estimated from observed messages. Zero-probability symbols have undefined self-information and contribute zero to entropy.
