Two tones and a beat envelope

What slow pattern appears when two pure tones have nearby frequencies?

Make a prediction

With two nonzero amplitudes and different frequencies, the envelope repeats at the absolute frequency difference.

A worked setting

Controls: First frequency (Hz): 4; Second frequency (Hz): 5; First amplitude: 1; Second amplitude: 1; Displayed time (s): 2

Frequency difference 1 Hz; envelope repetition period 1 s. Equal-amplitude product identity applies exactly. The table samples 2 seconds.

A sin(2πf₁t)+A sin(2πf₂t)=2A cos(π(f₁−f₂)t) sin(π(f₁+f₂)t)

What the result means

Values are algebraic sums of two ideal sinusoids sampled at displayed times. For equal amplitudes, the product-to-sum identity gives a carrier and beat envelope.

Where the model stops

This ideal sound-pressure model omits timbre, hearing context, phase differences and the behavior of real instruments. No audio is played.
