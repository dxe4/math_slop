How do equilibria of x′=μ−x² change as μ crosses zero?

Make a prediction

Two equilibria exist for μ>0, one nonhyperbolic equilibrium for μ=0, and none for μ<0.

What the result means

Solving μ−x²=0 gives the equilibria; derivative −2x classifies simple equilibria. A bounded numerical trajectory adds context.

Where the model stops

At μ=0 linearization is inconclusive; arrows give one-sided behavior. Trajectories that grow past the display cap are stopped before overflow, not continued through blow-up.
