Which poles contribute to a circle integral?

Make a prediction

The contour integral equals oriented 2πi times the sum of enclosed residues, provided no pole lies on the circle.

A worked setting

Controls: Integrand: 1/z; Circle center real: 0; Circle center imaginary: 0; Circle radius: 1.5; Orientation: Counterclockwise

Enclosed poles: 0. Analytic integral 0 + 6.2831853i.

What the result means

The poles are 0 for 1/z and ±i for 1/(1+z²). Midpoint quadrature separately estimates real and imaginary components.

Where the model stops

The residue value uses the stated residue theorem; quadrature is only a numerical comparison and may lose accuracy near a pole.
