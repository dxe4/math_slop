When downhill steps overshoot

Which fixed steps converge on a positive quadratic bowl?

Make a prediction

For Q=diag(λ₁,λ₂)>0, every initial point converges when 0<α<2/max(λ₁,λ₂).

A worked setting

Controls: Positive curvature λ₁: 1; Positive curvature λ₂: 3; Initial x: 2; Initial y: 1; Step size α: 0.5; Iterations: 8

The all-starting-points convergence condition α<0.66666667 is satisfied at α=0.5. At step 8, objective=0.000053405762.

f=(λ₁x²+λ₂y²)/2; (xₙ₊₁,yₙ₊₁)=((1−αλ₁)xₙ,(1−αλ₂)yₙ).

What the result means

Each coordinate is multiplied by 1−αλ at every step, making stability transparent.

Where the model stops

The step interval is a guarantee for this positive quadratic. An individual special starting point may converge outside it; it does not describe arbitrary nonconvex objectives.
