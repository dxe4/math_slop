How do triangle angle sums differ across three geometries?

Make a prediction

Euclidean: 180°; the sphere octant: 270°; curvature −1 hyperbolic triangle: less than 180°.

What the result means

The sphere preset is an exact octant. Euclidean and Poincaré disk coordinates define right triangles. Hyperbolic side lengths use the disk metric; area equals angular defect for curvature −1.

Where the model stops

The sphere uses a fixed exact example. Hyperbolic angles and the drawn disk arc are numerical illustrations; the disk boundary lies infinitely far away. The area identity is invoked, not proved by the simulation.
