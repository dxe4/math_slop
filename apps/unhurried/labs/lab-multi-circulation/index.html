A hole inside the loop

Can a curl-free field circulate around a hole?

Make a prediction

The gradient field has zero circulation. The punctured-plane vortex has signed 2π around the origin and zero when the origin lies outside.

A worked setting

Controls: Vector field: Punctured-plane vortex; Circle center x: 0; Circle center y: 0; Radius: 1; Orientation: Counterclockwise

What the result means

The vortex F=(-y,x)/(x²+y²) is undefined at the origin. Its circle circulation depends on winding around that missing point.

Where the model stops

The midpoint integral is numerical. A path through the singularity has no circulation in this model; zero curl away from the hole alone gives no global potential.
