How does a 2×2 matrix move basis vectors and area?

Make a prediction

The two matrix columns are Ae₁ and Ae₂; the signed unit-square area is det A.

A worked setting

Controls: Matrix a₁₁: 1; Matrix a₁₂: 0; Matrix a₂₁: 0; Matrix a₂₂: 1

The unit square maps to signed area 1 and ordinary area 1.

A = [[1, 0], [0, 1]]; A(x,y) = xAe₁ + yAe₂; det A = ad − bc = 1.

What the result means

Linearity gives Av=xAe₁+yAe₂. The determinant gives the signed area of the parallelogram spanned by the columns.

Where the model stops

Finite samples and the drawing illustrate linearity; they do not prove it for every vector. A singular matrix collapses area, and a negative determinant reverses orientation.
