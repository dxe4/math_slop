Walk until an edge

What is the chance a token hits the right edge before the left?

Make a prediction

A small drift toward the right can change the right-edge chance substantially.

A worked setting

Controls: Right edge position N: 6; Starting position: 3; Chance of right step: 0.5; Sample paths: 100; Random seed: 17

Exact eventual chance of hitting right first: 0.5; left first: 0.5. For fair steps, the exact full mean absorption time is i(N−i) = 9 steps. In 100 seeded paths, 44 reached right, 56 left, and 0 were censored at 2000 steps. Sample mean of min(T,2000) ≈ 9.82 steps; this estimates the capped expectation, not necessarily E[T]. What the result means

A token on 0…N moves right with fixed chance p and left otherwise. Edges absorb. The finite recurrence is solved exactly; seeded paths merely check it.

Where the model stops

Paths are capped at 2000 steps for responsiveness. The displayed sample mean averages min(T,2000), not T. Censored paths are reported; the exact eventual hitting chance is independent of this cap.
