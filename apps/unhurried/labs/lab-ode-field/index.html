Exact curves and an approximate walk

How far can Euler steps drift from an exact decay curve?

Make a prediction

For y′=−ky, exact y=y₀e^(−kt) stays positive for y₀>0. Euler multiplies by 1−kh each step.

A worked setting

Controls: Initial amount y₀: 2; Positive rate k (per time): 1; Time step h: 0.5; Comparison focus: Exact and Euler

Exact and Euler focus. Exact y(5)=0.013475894; Euler=0.001953125. Euler stays nonnegative and decays.

Exact: y(t)=y₀e^(−kt). Euler: yⱼ=y₀(1−kh)ʲ, at tⱼ=jh.
