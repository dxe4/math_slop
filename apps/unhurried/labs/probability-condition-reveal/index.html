Reveal the denominator

After seeing a blue marble, which bag is likely?

Make a prediction

A blue marble favors the bag that produces blue more often, but the prior choice still matters.

A worked setting

Controls: Chance of choosing bag A: 0.5; Blue fraction in A: 0.8; Blue fraction in B: 0.2; Independent draws: 500; Random seed: 17

P(A | blue) = 0.8. Seeded sample: 197 of 256 blue observations came from A (0.769531).

P(A | blue) = P(A)P(blue | A) / [P(A)P(blue | A) + P(B)P(blue | B)]

What the result means

Multiply each prior bag chance by its blue-marble likelihood, then divide by the total blue chance. The counts are independent bag choices with replacement.

Where the model stops

Bag contents and prior choice are stipulated. A zero-probability clue has no conditional posterior; finite samples fluctuate.
