Clock orbits and inverses

When does multiplication rearrange every residue without collision?

Make a prediction

The map x↦ax mod m is a permutation exactly when gcd(a,m)=1.

A worked setting

Controls: Modulus m: 7; Multiplier a: 3; Orbit steps from 1: 6

gcd(3,7)=1; permutation; inverse of 3 mod 7: 5. Orbit: 1→3→2→6→4→5→1.

What the result means

Where the model stops

The table checks this one finite modulus. It illustrates, but does not prove, the criterion for all moduli.
