Build a fair or lopsided game

Can two games have a similar average but different surprises?

Make a prediction

The exact mean and variance come from the outcome weights; a short running average can miss the exact mean.

A worked setting

Controls: Score A: -2; Score B: 1; Score C: 6; Weight A: 3; Weight B: 4; Weight C: 3; Trials: 500; Random seed: 17

Exact mean 1.6, variance 9.84, standard deviation 3.13688. Sample mean after 500 trials: 1.598. Running means: 10: 0; 50: 0.96; 100: 1.34; 500: 1.598.

P(i) = weight(i) / total weight; E[X] = Σ pᵢxᵢ; Var(X) = Σ pᵢ(xᵢ − E[X])²

What the result means

Weights are normalized into probabilities. Each seeded trial independently samples one of the three scores.

Where the model stops

A mean is a long-run model average, not a promised score; these three outcomes are chosen by the learner.
