Analytic comparison and limits

For x≠0x\ne0, g(x)=acosh⁡(1+x2)g(x)=\operatorname{acosh}(1+x^2) and g′(x)=2sign⁡(x)x2+2g'(x)=\frac{2\operatorname{sign}(x)}{\sqrt{x^2+2}}. At x=0x=0 one-sided slopes are −2 and 2-\sqrt2\text{ and }\sqrt2, so no derivative; a symmetric difference of zero is not its derivative. The equivalent evaluation 2asinh⁡(∣x∣/2)2\operatorname{asinh}(|x|/\sqrt2) avoids rounding 1+x21+x^2 to 1 near zero; acosh⁡(cosh⁡x)=∣x∣\operatorname{acosh}(\cosh x)=|x| uses the exact real branch identity. Non-finite numeric output raises an explicit precision error; null is reserved for a deliberately absent derivative.

  • Default x=0.5x=0.5: g=ln⁡2g=\ln2 and g′=43g'=\frac43.
  • Changed x=−0.5x=-0.5: g=ln⁡2g=\ln2 and g′=−43g'=-\frac43.
  • Changed x=1x=1: g=acosh⁡2≈1.316957897g=\operatorname{acosh}2\approx1.316957897 and g′=23≈1.154700538g'=\frac2{\sqrt3}\approx1.154700538.

Boundary and failure behavior

Boundary: at x=0x=0 the value is zero, but one-sided slopes −2 and 2-\sqrt2\text{ and }\sqrt2 differ. A centered difference of zero is not a derivative.
