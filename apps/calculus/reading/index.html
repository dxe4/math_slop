How to use the five problem types

Types describe the main mathematical output requested, not difficulty. A problem can combine several kinds of work.

Fluency
Apply a known rule, calculation, or theorem to given data.
Interpretation
Explain meaning, scope, domain, units, or what the evidence supports.
Proof
Establish or refute a claim under its conditions, including bounds and counterexamples.
Modeling
Build, adapt, or validate a contextual relationship, with relevant assumptions and units.
Challenge
Coordinate methods, cases, or restrictions where choosing and checking a strategy matters.
