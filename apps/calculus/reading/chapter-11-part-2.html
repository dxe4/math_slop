    • Continuous: A′A^{\prime} exists and equals f(0)f(0); changed point: A′A^{\prime} exists but differs; jump: A′A^{\prime} does not exist
    • All three derivatives exist and equal their assigned rates because the rates are integrable
    • Both discontinuous rates make their accumulations nondifferentiable

    Answer. Continuous: A′A^{\prime} exists and equals f(0)f(0); changed point: A′A^{\prime} exists but differs; jump: A′A^{\prime} does not exist. The ordered pairs of left and right shrinking-average limits are (2,2)(2,2), (2,2)(2,2), and (2,4)(2,4) for the three rates. The assigned point value is a separate question.

    Why the other choices fail.

    • All three derivatives exist and equal their assigned rates because the rates are integrable — Integrability makes accumulation continuous, but gives neither derivative existence at a jump nor equality at a changed point.
    • Both discontinuous rates make their accumulations nondifferentiable — The changed-point rate has the same integral as the continuous rate; its accumulation still has derivative 22.

Chapter synthesis

  • Continuous rates have differentiable accumulation functions whose derivatives recover the rates.
  • The evaluation theorem converts an integral into an antiderivative difference; initial data determine the remaining constant.
  • Moving endpoints require chain-rule factors, and definite substitutions require transformed bounds.

Common mistakes to check

  • “Integration and differentiation always cancel without conditions.” Continuity guarantees the accumulation derivative; a step rate produces a corner at its jump. Test with problem 10
  • “An antiderivative is unique.” It is determined only up to a constant on an interval; an initial value selects one member. Test with problem 12
  • “A definite integral gives the amount present.” It gives accumulated change when its integrand is a rate; the initial amount must be added. Test with problem 5

Choose one mistake that tempted you. Give a counterexample or identify the missing condition, then solve the exact problem linked beside that mistake again without a hint.
