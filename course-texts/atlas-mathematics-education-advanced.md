## 6. ED-APP-P001 — Complete primary model

**1. Citation and claim status.** Use the full publication citation and STUDY-DESIGN/RESULT locators above. The target is an external research finding about an assignment contrast on a specified measure; the arithmetic check is a mathematical reconstruction. It is not a theorem that a teaching method works for every learner, and it is not evidence from Euler.

**2. Construct and measurement.** Word-problem performance involves identifying relationships and computing a labelled answer; it is not interchangeable with timed arithmetic, unrestricted proof or general intelligence. A content-specific validity question is whether a correct answer reflects the combine/compare/change structure or an operation chosen from a keyword. Vocabulary familiarity and response format may add irrelevant demands for some intended uses. If language interpretation is part of the target, however, removing it changes the construct rather than simply removing noise. Use ED-TASK-MORE-UP/DOWN to show why the same surface word cannot define the operation.

**3. Design, estimand and assumptions.** The causal target is the average effect of assignment to the specified package versus control on the posttest outcome for the eligible at-risk trial population, under the study's implementation and analysis. Individual assignment and clustered outcome analysis are compatible; it would be wrong to rewrite the study as school-randomized because it uses a multilevel model. Two central requirements are preserved randomization/consistent assignment interpretation without relevant interference, and a defensible treatment of missing outcomes and outcome measurement. Eligibility and a model-adjusted contrast alone do not establish transport to excluded populations. The retained-data model does not by itself recover outcomes for everyone initially assigned. The packet lacks the raw records needed to verify all those requirements.

**4. Quantitative reconstruction.** The normal margin is 1.96×0.550=1.078, giving [5.338,7.494] in the outcome's score units. This is the declared course approximation, not a newly recovered author-reported interval. A positive pointwise interval supports a nonzero contrast under its statistical assumptions; it does not prove universal benefit, the isolated effect of language, persistence, or practical value in every classroom.

## 10. ED-APP-R001 — Complete return model

**1. Citation and claim status.** Use the full citation once, the stated Introduction phrase, Results mediation paragraph and the exact packet locators. The article's mechanism language is an external causal interpretation to appraise; the printed arithmetic and the proposed access revision have different statuses.

**2. Construct and measurement.** Distinguish the mediator measure, the mathematical performance outcome, and the instructional package. A measured association between mediator and outcome is not a randomized comparison of mediator values. Posttest-only measurement weakens an inference about changes or temporal ordering. Any extension to a group outside the stated eligibility needs its own content/response-process/access argument, not a presumption of generality.

**3. Design, estimand and assumptions.** The added-language assignment effect compares WP[L] with WP; that contrast is more specific than P's whole-package comparison. A causal indirect-effect interpretation additionally needs a defensible mediator–outcome identification argument: no relevant unmeasured mediator–outcome confounding under the stated covariates, no unaddressed treatment-induced confounding, and suitable temporal/model assumptions. Randomizing the package alone does not randomize the mediator. These stronger conditions are not verified by this packet, so retain a bounded model-based mediation interpretation rather than an unconditional mechanism theorem.
