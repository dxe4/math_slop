# Read mathematics through cultures and time

**Audience.** Curious learners who want a globally framed, source-aware history anchored in mathematical work.

**Goal.** Compare mathematical practices and developments without a lone-genius story or an inevitable single line of progress.

**Estimated time.** About 90 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.

## Stage 1: Read notation and evidence

**Milestone.** Reconstruct one historical procedure while separating the source, translation, and modern interpretation.

## Stage 2: Compare traditions and exchanges

**Milestone.** Compare two procedures in their own representational and institutional settings without treating either as a failed modern method.

- **Mathematics in Nile Valley and Mesopotamian societies** — Study surviving numerical, geometric, administrative, and scribal practices from ancient Nile Valley and Mesopotamian settings.
  - [Ways to Write Thirty-Seven: A Number-System Time Machine](../counting-across-cultures.md)
  - [Mathematics Across Worlds: Ideas, Practices, and Exchanges](#/courses/atlas-mathematics-across-worlds)
  - [Read the Procedure, Rebuild the Mathematics: Three Historical Algorithms](../reading-historical-algorithms.md)
  - [Plimpton 322](../history-plimpton-322.md)
- **Mathematics in South Asian traditions** — Trace selected developments in numeration, algorithms, geometry, algebra, trigonometry, astronomy, and commentary across South Asian settings.
  - [Mathematics Across Worlds: Ideas, Practices, and Exchanges](#/courses/atlas-mathematics-across-worlds)
- **Mathematics in East Asian traditions** — Examine procedure texts, counting tools, commentaries, geometry, equations, and scholarly practices across East Asian histories.
  - [Mathematics Across Worlds: Ideas, Practices, and Exchanges](#/courses/atlas-mathematics-across-worlds)
  - [Read the Procedure, Rebuild the Mathematics: Three Historical Algorithms](../reading-historical-algorithms.md)
- **Mathematics in Islamic societies** — Explore algebra, geometry, astronomy, instruments, inheritance calculation, translation, and scholarship across diverse Islamic societies.
  - [Mathematics Across Worlds: Ideas, Practices, and Exchanges](#/courses/atlas-mathematics-across-worlds)
  - [Read the Procedure, Rebuild the Mathematics: Three Historical Algorithms](../reading-historical-algorithms.md)
- **African mathematical traditions** — Investigate selected numerical, geometric, architectural, artistic, navigational, and scholarly practices across Africa without treating a continent as one tradition.
  - [Mathematics Across Worlds: Ideas, Practices, and Exchanges](#/courses/atlas-mathematics-across-worlds)
- **Indigenous mathematics of the Americas and Pacific** — Study selected numeration, calendars, navigation, measurement, pattern, and knowledge practices with attention to living communities and colonial disruption.
  - [Ways to Write Thirty-Seven: A Number-System Time Machine](../counting-across-cultures.md)
  - [Mathematics Across Worlds: Ideas, Practices, and Exchanges](#/courses/atlas-mathematics-across-worlds)
