# Mathematics for graphics and visualization

**Audience.** Creative coders, game developers, designers, and scientific visualization builders.

**Goal.** Turn geometry, linear algebra, calculus, optics, and sampling into expressive interactive visuals.

**Estimated time.** About 220 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.
