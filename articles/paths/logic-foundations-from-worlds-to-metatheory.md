# Logic and foundations from tiny worlds to metatheory

**Audience.** Curious beginners and proof-ready learners seeking a coherent route from concrete truth conditions to models, axioms, computation, and constructive foundations.

**Goal.** Separate syntax, semantics, and proof; reason exactly about sets and cardinality; and state metatheorems, independence, and proof–program correspondences with their necessary hypotheses.

**Estimated time.** About 300 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.

## Capstone

Formalize one mathematical claim in a named language and theory; test finite models; supply a derivation or countermodel; implement a checked fragment; and report separately the object-level result, metatheorem used, constructive content, and unresolved boundary.
