# Applied modeling from measurements to defensible decisions

**Audience.** Curious beginners through graduate learners who want a connected route across scale, inverse reasoning, networks, operations, biology, epidemics, finance, and signals.

**Goal.** Formulate, analyze, compute, validate, and communicate conditional models while keeping units, uncertainty, identifiability, decision values, causal effects, equilibrium, and financial valuation distinct.

**Estimated time.** About 430 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.

## Capstone

Deliver a reproducible model-to-decision dossier for one scientific or operational system: use contract, units and scales, mechanism and observation maps, theorem or certificate, tested dependency-free computation, uncertainty and sensitivity, identifiability, validation, causal nonclaim or identification argument, accessible numerical alternative, and a decision boundary that stops where evidence stops.
