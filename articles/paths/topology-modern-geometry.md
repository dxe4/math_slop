# Topology and modern geometry from shape to schemes

**Audience.** Learners with proof fluency who want a connected route from intuitive shape through topology, manifolds, algebraic invariants, knots, and algebraic geometry.

**Goal.** Move from open-set and deformation reasoning to exact invariants, smooth and metric geometry, low-dimensional topology, and a prerequisite-honest schemes orientation.

**Estimated time.** About 380 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.

## Capstone

Build a finite topological case study with a triangulation, boundary matrices, homology, Euler characteristic, a map or covering, and a geometric interpretation; then compare what changes after adding smooth, metric, knot-complement, or scheme structure.
