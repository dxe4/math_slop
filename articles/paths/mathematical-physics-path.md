# Road to mathematical physics

**Audience.** Mathematics or physics learners seeking a unified structural route.

**Goal.** Develop the analysis, geometry, dynamics, and probability behind modern physical theories.

**Estimated time.** About 800 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.
