# Mathematics for computer science

**Audience.** Software developers and computer-science students building theoretical depth.

**Goal.** Master discrete proof, algorithms, information, computation, and mathematical program reasoning.

**Estimated time.** About 360 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.
