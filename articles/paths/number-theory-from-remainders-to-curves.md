# Number theory from remainders to curves

**Audience.** Curious beginners through proof-ready undergraduates seeking one coherent route into arithmetic, algorithms, cryptography, and modern number theory.

**Goal.** Move from visible remainder patterns to exact proofs, efficient algorithms, security definitions, number fields, prime distributions, and rational points while keeping every hypothesis and open boundary explicit.

**Estimated time.** About 360 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.
