# Teach mathematics for understanding, access, and agency

**Audience.** Teachers, tutors, curriculum writers, and mentors seeking mathematically serious education foundations.

**Goal.** Design, teach, assess, and revise mathematics using connected representations, interpretable evidence, proof, access, and research literacy.

**Estimated time.** About 120 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.
