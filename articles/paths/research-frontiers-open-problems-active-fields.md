# Research frontiers: open problems, active fields, and claim boundaries

**Audience.** Advanced learners entering research literature who want to distinguish a formal open problem from a broad research program, a conditional theorem, a finite computation, and an empirical model claim.

**Goal.** State each frontier's mathematical objects and quantifiers, reproduce one orienting calculation, place representative theorems in their exact scope, and audit what a new result does and does not establish.

**Estimated time.** About 86 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.

## Capstone

Produce a seven-frontier claim atlas: for each topic, give the object, exact target or research-program formulation, one known theorem with hypotheses, one computation or model and its limit, an authoritative dated status source where relevant, and a claim that the available evidence explicitly does not permit.
