# Differential equations from accountable models to long-time dynamics

**Audience.** Learners with calculus and linear algebra who want a connected route through ODEs, PDEs, dynamics, control, computation, chaos, and ergodic reasoning.

**Goal.** Build, analyze, control, approximate, and audit evolving systems while keeping model, theorem, numerical, and long-time claims distinct.

**Estimated time.** About 460 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.

## Capstone

Deliver an accessible evolution-system audit containing equations and units, well-posedness scope, phase or energy structure, feedback and measurement boundaries where relevant, a tested numerical refinement packet, and a long-time claim tied to a named invariant measure or explicitly labeled as finite numerical evidence.
