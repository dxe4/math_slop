# Graduate algebra and geometry

**Audience.** Advanced learners preparing for algebra, topology, geometry, or number theory research.

**Goal.** Build the categorical, algebraic, topological, and geometric language shared by modern pure mathematics.

**Estimated time.** About 900 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.
