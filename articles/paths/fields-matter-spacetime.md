# Fields, matter, and spacetime with mathematical accountability

**Audience.** Proof-ready mathematics or physics learners seeking a connected route through classical, continuum, relativistic, quantum, statistical, and gauge field theories.

**Goal.** Derive and compute physical models while keeping units, sign conventions, state spaces, initial and boundary data, constitutive closure, observables, and approximation status explicit.

**Estimated time.** About 540 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.

## Capstone

Produce a two-formulation study of one physical system with state and observables, units and conventions, domain plus compatible data, balance and closure, exact limiting case, dependency-free computation, residual and uncertainty audits, and an explicit model-breakdown boundary.
