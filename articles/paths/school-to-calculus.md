# School mathematics to calculus

**Audience.** Learners preparing for university science, economics, or engineering.

**Goal.** Build the algebra, functions, trigonometry, and limiting ideas needed for conceptual calculus.

**Estimated time.** About 120 hours.

> Use the named course for the current chapter sequence. The earlier path and its original study records remain available separately. See the [course](#/courses/course-calculus-native-1).
