# Mathematics education from learner evidence to responsible improvement

**Audience.** Educators, coaches, designers, and graduate learners who want to connect mathematical task analysis, technology choices, classroom inquiry, and research claims without outrunning the evidence.

**Goal.** Observe mathematical reasoning at an appropriate grain, audit tools and representations by their affordances, choose a research design that matches an inference, and run a collaborative improvement cycle with explicit ethical and claim boundaries.

**Estimated time.** About 42 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.

## Capstone

Create an ethical improvement dossier for one bounded mathematical problem of practice: include a content map, learner-evidence task, technology affordance audit, study-design and measurement argument, research-synthesis protocol, lesson-study conjecture table, de-identified evidence plan, and a final claim-boundary matrix.
