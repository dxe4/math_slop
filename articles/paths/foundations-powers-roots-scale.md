# Powers, roots, and scale bridge

**Audience.** Learners who know fractions and signed arithmetic and want a focused bridge into algebraic powers, radicals, scientific notation, and exponential models.

**Goal.** Interpret integer and rational exponents with their domain conditions, reverse powers using principal roots, calculate across powers of ten, and check scale claims with bounds and units.

**Estimated time.** About 8 hours.

## Capstone

Create a one-page scale audit for three quantities spanning at least six orders of magnitude: express each in scientific notation, compare their ratios, include one area or volume rescaling, and justify every rounded radical with a numerical bracket.
