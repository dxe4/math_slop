# Research on-ramp

**Audience.** Graduate-level learners choosing a field or beginning literature study.

**Goal.** Learn how to enter a modern topic through examples, surveys, prerequisite audits, and reproducible exploration.

**Estimated time.** About 180 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.
