# Geometry from construction to curvature

**Audience.** Learners moving from hands-on shape and secondary trigonometry to proof, computation, spatial geometry, perspective, and curved model spaces.

**Goal.** Construct, prove, compute, measure, and visualize geometric claims while naming the incidence, metric, curvature, and error assumptions that make each conclusion valid.

**Estimated time.** About 410 hours.

> This is a reading and exploration path across selected resources; it does not supply one primary-book course syllabus. Its existing activities and study identities remain available.

## Capstone

Create an accessible geometry case study joining a physical or specified construction, proof dependency map, coordinate computation, conic or solid element, shareable numerical visualization, and flat-versus-curved comparison; report theorem, rendering, measurement, approximation, and unresolved model error separately.
