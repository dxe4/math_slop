# Sources for reporting window consistency

1. [Enzo Orsingher and Bruno Toaldo, Counting processes with Bernštein intertimes and random jumps](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1312.1498), section 4.3, equations 4.22–4.25, 4.29 and 4.30. These give a Gamma-subordinated Poisson process, its negative-binomial marginal law, positive-size batch jumps, and its conditional two-window beta-binomial allocation. Their displayed unit Gamma-subordinator rate and shape-time parameterization are rescaled here to shape $\alpha t$ and rate $\beta$, with an independent unit-rate Poisson process. Our $\operatorname{NB}(r,p)$ convention places $p^r(1-p)^k$ in the PMF, matching the convention discussed in their equation 4.28.
2. [P. Vellaisamy and Aditya Maheshwari, Fractional Negative Binomial and Polya Processes](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1306.2493), section 5.1, equation 5.1, and Remark 5.2. These distinguish the shared random Gamma-rate Pólya process from the independent-increment negative-binomial process. Their Gamma notation orders rate before shape, and their NB probability parameter is the count-side factor $1-p$ in our notation. The lesson states its own conventions before deriving each joint law.
