# Extract torsion from a reversing mapping-torus relation

*Medium · [Topology problems](index.md)*

The mapping torus of reflection of S¹ has presentation $\langle a,b\mid aba^{-1}=b^{-1}\rangle$, where a follows the mapping-torus direction and b the fiber. Return \[rank of its abelianization, order of its nonzero torsion cyclic factor\]. Use integral abelianization.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Make the generators commute while retaining the displayed relation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The relation constrains b but imposes no nonzero multiple relation on a.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Orientation reversal in the fiber produces torsion when the loop relation is abelianized.

1. In additive abelian notation the relation reads a+b−a=−b, hence 2b=0.
2. There is no relation on a and no mixed relation left. Therefore the abelianization is Z generated by a direct sum Z/2 generated by b.
3. The free rank is 1 and the torsion factor has order 2, so the vector is \[1,2\].

</details>
