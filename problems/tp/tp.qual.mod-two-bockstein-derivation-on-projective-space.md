# Compute a Bockstein derivation on the projective cohomology ring

*Hard · [Topology problems](index.md)*

For the coefficient sequence $0\to\mathbb F_2\xrightarrow{1\mapsto2}\mathbb Z/4\to\mathbb F_2\to0$, prove the cohomology Bockstein β is a derivation over F₂. Then compute β(tᵏ) for every k≥0 in $H^*(\mathbb{RP}^{\infty};\mathbb F_2)=\mathbb F_2[t]$, \|t\|=1. Derive β(t)=t² using the cellular cochain differential or the coefficient exact sequence.

Lift cocycle representatives to Z/4 cochains, apply the cup Leibniz identity and divide their even coboundaries by 2.

The integral cellular boundary in dimension 2 is multiplication by 2, so the degree-one lifted cochain has nonzero divided coboundary modulo 2.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Model proof</summary>

The connecting map respects products through a degree-one derivation law and detects the first square.

1. For mod-two cocycles a,b choose Z/4 lifts A,B. Their coboundaries are twice cochains, and β is represented by those coboundaries divided by 2 and reduced modulo 2. The identity d(A∪B)=dA∪B+(−1)^{\|a\|}A∪dB gives β(ab)=β(a)b+aβ(b), since signs disappear modulo 2. Changing lifts or representatives changes only a coboundary.
2. In the standard projective cellular structure, d₂ is multiplication by 2. The degree-one mod-two generator has lift the value 1 on the one-cell; its Z/4 coboundary has value 2 on the two-cell. Dividing by 2 gives the nonzero degree-two mod-two class. Since that group is generated by t², β(t)=t².
3. Inducting with the derivation identity yields β(tᵏ)=k t^{k−1}β(t)=k t^{k+1}, with the coefficient k reduced modulo 2. Therefore it is zero for even k and t^{k+1} for odd k; β(1)=0.
4. This is the mod-two-valued connecting map from the Z/4 coefficient sequence. It is not the integral-valued Bockstein from 0→Z→Z→F₂→0, although reducing the latter gives the same operation in this example.
