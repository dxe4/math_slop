# Count cyclic vectors through a polynomial local quotient

*Medium · [Linear algebra problems](index.md)*

Over $\mathbb F_2$, let $p=t^2+t+1$ and let T be multiplication by t on $V=\mathbb F_2[t]/(p^2)$. Use cyclic modules and polynomial ideals as preparation. How many vectors v satisfy $\operatorname{span}(v,Tv,T^2v,T^3v)=V$?

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":12,"checker_scope":"Only the requested exact invariant or rational coordinate object is checked. Canonical-form reasoning and graduate preparation are not automatically certified."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The vectors generated from v are the ideal generated by its residue class.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

A residue generates the whole quotient exactly when it is not divisible by the sole irreducible factor p.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Cyclicity is a unit condition in the operator module.

1. The quotient has dimension four over $\mathbb F_2$, hence sixteen elements. Its cyclic T-submodule generated by v is its principal ideal in this quotient.
2. It equals the whole module exactly when v is a unit, equivalently when its polynomial representative is coprime to p.
3. The nonunits are the ideal $(p)/(p^2)$, with four elements corresponding to multipliers modulo p. Thus the cyclic-vector count is $16-4=12$.

</details>
