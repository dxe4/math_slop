# Prove complete positivity, Choi positivity and Kraus length equivalence

*Hard · [Linear algebra problems](index.md)*

For a complex-linear map $\Phi:M_n(\mathbb C)\to M_m(\mathbb C)$, let $C_\Phi=\sum_{i,j}E_{ij}\otimes\Phi(E_{ij})$. Prove Phi is completely positive, meaning every $\operatorname{id}_k\otimes\Phi$ preserves positive semidefinite matrices, iff $C_\Phi\ge0$. Construct $\Phi(X)=\sum_a K_aXK_a^*$ and prove the minimum number of K terms is $\operatorname{rank}C_\Phi$. Also identify the trace-preserving condition. Preparation: tensor coordinates, Gram factorization and positive congruence.

<summary>Model proof</summary>

A positive block coefficient matrix is exactly a sum of conjugation operators.

1. If Phi is completely positive, then $C_\Phi=(\operatorname{id}_n\otimes\Phi)(\Omega\Omega^*)\ge0$, by expanding the rank-one input in matrix units.
2. Conversely factor $C_\Phi=\sum_{a=1}^r v_av_a^*$ with $r=\operatorname{rank}C_\Phi$. Write $v_a=\sum_i e_i\otimes K_ae_i$. Comparing the (i,j) blocks gives $\Phi(E_{ij})=\sum_aK_aE_{ij}K_a^*$, hence the Kraus formula by linearity.
3. Every amplification of that formula is a sum of maps $X\mapsto(I_k\otimes K_a)X(I_k\otimes K_a)^*$ and therefore preserves positivity. This proves complete positivity.
4. Any Kraus expression with s terms gives the Choi matrix as s rank-one outer products of reshaped K vectors, so its rank is at most s. The rank factorization above attains r, including the zero map with zero terms.
5. Cyclic trace gives $\operatorname{tr}\Phi(X)=\operatorname{tr}((\sum_aK_a^*K_a)X)$. Equality with $\operatorname{tr}X$ for all matrix units, hence all X, is equivalent to $\sum_aK_a^*K_a=I_n$.
