# Identify a common-cause explanation

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

In an observational study, musicians who practice longer also score higher. Prior training may cause both longer practice and higher scores. Which variable is the proposed confounder? Select the mathematically correct statement from the four alternatives.

**Options**

1. The arithmetic sign of the score difference.
2. The random-assignment device, which was not used.
3. Prior training.
4. The total number of musicians in the study.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A common cause can generate association without isolating an exposure’s effect.

1. Practice time is the exposure and score is the outcome.
2. Prior training may influence both, making it the stated possible confounder; the statement “Prior training.” matches that role.
