# Finite population mean

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

The complete finite population consists of values $2,4,6,8$. What is its arithmetic population mean?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The entire population is given, so no sampling correction is needed.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Divide the total of all listed outcomes by their number.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The population mean averages the outcomes of every population unit.

1. The population total is $2+4+6+8=20$.
2. There are $N=4$ population units.
3. The population mean is $20/4=5$. This is the known finite-population target rather than an estimate from a subset.

</details>
