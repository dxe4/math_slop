# Identify the random object in a sampling distribution

*Medium · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

A simulation repeatedly takes samples of $10$ measurements, records each sample mean, and plots those means. What does the plot approximate? Select the mathematically correct statement from the four alternatives.

**Options**

1. The distribution of all individual measurements in a single sample.
2. The sampling distribution of the mean for samples of size $10$ under that simulation model.
3. The distribution of population sizes.
4. The distribution of the ten largest individual measurements in each sample.

The plotted values are functions of whole samples.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A sampling distribution names both a statistic and a sampling mechanism.

1. Every plotted value is a mean computed from one ten-measurement sample.
2. Across repetitions those values approximate the sampling distribution of the mean at the stated fixed sample size.

</details>
