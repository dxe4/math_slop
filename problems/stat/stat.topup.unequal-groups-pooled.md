# Pool groups using their sizes

*Medium · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

Of $10$ people in A, $8$ answer yes. Of $90$ people in B, $18$ answer yes. What fraction of all one hundred people answer yes?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Pool counts before taking a proportion.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

An unweighted average of the two group percentages treats unequal groups as equal.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Counts weight the two groups correctly.

1. The yes count is $8+18=26$.
2. The total count is $10+90=100$.
3. The pooled proportion is $26/100=13/50$, rather than the unweighted mean of the two percentages.

</details>
