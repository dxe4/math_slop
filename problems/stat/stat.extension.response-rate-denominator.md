# Response rate denominator

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

Of $20$ invited people, $15$ are known to be eligible and $12$ eligible people respond. What fraction of eligible people respond? Give a reduced rational number.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The target group in the question determines the denominator.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Compare responses with the eligible count, excluding known ineligible invitees.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The eligible-response fraction uses eligible invitees as its denominator.

1. The denominator is the $15$ eligible people, not all $20$ invitees.
2. The numerator is the $12$ eligible respondents, giving $12/15$.
3. Reducing by the common factor $3$ gives $4/5$. A response fraction alone does not establish absence of nonresponse bias.

</details>
