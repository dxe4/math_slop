# Standardize below the model mean

*Medium · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

An assumed normal reference has mean $50$ and standard deviation 8. Find the standardized z-score of observation 38, defined as (observation-mean)/standard deviation.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Subtract the reference mean before dividing.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

An observation below the mean has a negative z-score.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Standardization expresses displacement in standard-deviation units.

1. The displacement is $38-50=-12$.
2. Dividing by eight gives $-12/8=-3/2$.
3. The negative score indicates one and a half standard deviations below the reference mean.

</details>
