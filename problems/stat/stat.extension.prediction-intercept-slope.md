# Prediction intercept slope

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

A fitted mean-response line is $\widehat y=2+3x$. At $x=4$, what is the fitted mean response?

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Evaluate the fitted line.

1. At $x=4$ the slope contribution is $3\cdot4=12$.
2. Including the intercept gives $\widehat y=2+12=14$.
3. This is the fitted mean response, rather than a claim that every new observed response at this predictor equals $14$.

</details>
