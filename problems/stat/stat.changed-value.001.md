# Compare the sensitivity of two summaries

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

The five observations are $1,2,2,5,10$. Find their mean and median. Replace $10$ by $20$ and find the new mean and median.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A large change to one extreme value can change the mean while leaving the median unchanged.

1. The original total is $20$, so the mean is $4$; the middle value is $2$.
2. The new total is $30$, so the mean is $6$; the middle value remains $2$.
