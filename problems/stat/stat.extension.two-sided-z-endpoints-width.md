# Half-width of a symmetric interval

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

A symmetric confidence interval has endpoints $[3,9]$. What is its half-width?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Find the length from the lower endpoint to the upper endpoint.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The half-width is half the full length; no normal critical value is needed.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Read the geometric half-width from the two endpoints.

1. The full width is $9-3=6$.
2. The midpoint is $(3+9)/2=6$.
3. The distance from the midpoint to either endpoint is $6/2=3$. The endpoints alone do not specify the coverage level.

</details>
