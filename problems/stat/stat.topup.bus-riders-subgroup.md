# Use the conditioned subgroup denominator

*Medium · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

Group A contains $8$ cyclists, $12$ bus riders and $10$ walkers. Group B contains $15$ cyclists, $5$ bus riders and $10$ walkers. Among bus riders only, what proportion belong to A?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Keep only people meeting the bus-rider condition.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The denominator contains bus riders from both groups.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Conditioning selects a new population for the fraction.

1. There are twelve bus riders in A and five in B.
2. The conditioned group therefore has seventeen people.
3. Twelve of those seventeen belong to A, giving $12/17$.

</details>
