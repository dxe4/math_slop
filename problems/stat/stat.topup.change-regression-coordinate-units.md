# Transform both predictor and outcome coordinates

*Medium · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

A supplied prediction is $\widehat y=4+2x$. Define new coordinates Xprime=x-10 and Yprime=y/2. What is the predicted Yprime at Xprime=3?

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Coordinate changes affect the interpretation of the same predictive relation.

1. Xprime equal to three means x equals thirteen.
2. The original prediction is $4+2(13)=30$.
3. Dividing by two gives predicted Yprime equal to fifteen.
