# Identify a failed conditional mean form

*Medium · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

For a supplied straight-line regression, the conditional average residual is negative at low and high x but positive in the middle. This pattern is stable in a large dataset. Which concern is most direct? Select the mathematically correct statement from the four alternatives.

**Options**

1. The straight-line conditional mean form may be inadequate.
2. Predictor values must be exactly normally distributed.
3. The pattern proves that all errors are independent.
4. The regression slope must be zero because the middle residuals are positive.
