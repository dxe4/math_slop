# Summarize an unsorted list

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

The five observations are $3, 8, 4, 3, 7$. Find the mean and median of this complete list. Enter exact values in the mean and median fields.

| Observation number | Value |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 |
| 2 | 8 |
| 3 | 4 |
| 4 | 3 |
| 5 | 7 |

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

For the mean, add every observation, including repeated values, then divide by five.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

For the median, sort the list and identify the observation in the middle position.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The mean uses the total; the median uses positions after sorting.

1. The sum is 3 + 8 + 4 + 3 + 7 = 25, so the mean is $25/5 = 5$.
2. The sorted list is $3, 3, 4, 7, 8$. Its third value, 4, is the median.

</details>
