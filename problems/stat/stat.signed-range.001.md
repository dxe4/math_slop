# Find a range across zero

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

Four temperature observations, in degrees Celsius, are $-4, 3, 7$ and $-1$. The range is the largest value minus the smallest value. What is this dataset’s range in degrees Celsius? Enter the numeric value only.

| Observation number | Temperature (°C) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | -4 |
| 2 | 3 |
| 3 | 7 |
| 4 | -1 |

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Range measures the distance between the two extreme values, including when they lie on opposite sides of zero.

1. The smallest observation is −4 and the largest is $7$.
2. The range is $7 - (-4) = 11$ degrees Celsius.

</details>
