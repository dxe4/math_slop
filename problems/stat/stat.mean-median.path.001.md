# Compute two different summaries

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

Find the mean and median of the five observations $0,1,3,6,10$. Give exact values in mean and median.

| observations | \[0,1,3,6,10\] |

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The two summaries use different information from the same list.

1. The total is 20, so the mean is $20/5=4$.
2. The third observation in sorted order is $3$, giving median $3$.
