# Compare spread when centers agree

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

Dataset A is $2, 4, 6$. Dataset B is $0, 4, 8$. Each dataset has mean $4$. Calculate the range of A and the range of B using maximum minus minimum. Enter the two numeric ranges in range\_A and range\_B.

| Dataset | Observations | Supplied mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 2, 4, 6 | 4 |
| B | 0, 4, 8 | 4 |


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

An equal mean does not require equal extremes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Find the smallest and largest values separately for each dataset.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A measure of center does not determine a measure of spread.

1. For A, maximum $6$ minus minimum $2$ gives range\_A = $4$.
2. For B, maximum $8$ minus minimum $0$ gives range\_B = $8$.
3. B has the larger range, even though the two datasets have the same mean.

</details>
