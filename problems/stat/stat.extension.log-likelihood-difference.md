# Log likelihood difference

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

Two candidate models have log likelihoods $-5$ and $-8$, respectively, on the same data. What is the first minus the second log likelihood?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Keep the requested order of subtraction.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtracting a negative number adds its positive magnitude.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Compute the signed difference of the two log likelihoods.

1. The requested quantity is $\ell_1-\ell_2=(-5)-(-8)$.
2. It equals $-5+8=3$.
3. This is a log-likelihood difference, not the likelihood ratio itself. If natural logarithms are used, the likelihood ratio is $e^3$.

</details>
