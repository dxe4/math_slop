# Compare ranges at an equal mean

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

Dataset A is $4,5,6$ and dataset B is $1,5,9$. Both have mean $5$. Find range\_A and range\_B.

| Given information | Value |
| --- | --- |
| A | \[4,5,6\] |
| B | \[1,5,9\] |
| supplied mean | 5 |

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"record_of_rationals","expected":{"range_A":"2","range_B":"8"},"checker_scope":"Checks the requested exact quantities under the stated conventions and model. It does not certify an inferential assumption or assess an open explanation."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The equal means do not determine the extremes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Calculate maximum minus minimum separately for each list.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Center and spread describe different features.

1. A has range $6-4=2$.
2. B has range 9−1=8, so its extreme-to-extreme distance is larger despite the equal means.

</details>
