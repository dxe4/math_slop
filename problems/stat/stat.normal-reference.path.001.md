# Standardize a value under a stated model

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

A stated normal model has mean $50$ and standard deviation $10$. Find the z-score of $x=70$.

| Given information | Value |
| --- | --- |
| mean | 50 |
| standard deviation | 10 |
| x | 70 |

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"rational","expected":{"numerator":2,"denominator":1},"checker_scope":"Checks the requested exact quantities under the stated conventions and model. It does not certify an inferential assumption or assess an open explanation."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A z-score records displacement from the mean in standard-deviation units.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use($70-50$)/$10$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Standardization expresses a displacement on the reference scale.

1. The displacement from the model mean is $70-50=20$.
2. Dividing by $10$ gives $z=2$.

</details>
