# Use a summary to complete a dataset

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

Five observations have mean $9$. Four of them are $4, 7, 9$ and $12$. Find the remaining observation.

| Given quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Number of observations | 5 |
| Mean | 9 |
| Known observations | 4, 7, 9, 12 |

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":13,"checker_scope":"Exact numeric fields for the stated data. This check does not assess a written justification or a population inference."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

All five observations must add to five times their mean.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The known observations add to $32$. Compare this with the required total.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A mean specifies an exact total once the number of observations is known.

1. The five-value total must be $5 \times  9 = 45$.
2. The known total is 4 + 7 + 9 + 12 = 32, so the missing value is $45 - 32 = 13$.

</details>
