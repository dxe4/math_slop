# Recover a complementary category before dividing

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

Thirty people answer exactly yes or no to one question. Eleven answer no. What proportion answer yes?

| Given information | Value |
| --- | --- |
| total | 30 |
| excluded | 11 |

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The yes and no categories exhaust all $30$ responses.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Find the yes count by subtracting the no count.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An exhaustive two-category split lets a total recover a missing frequency.

1. The yes count is $30-11=19$.
2. The yes proportion is $19/30$.

</details>
