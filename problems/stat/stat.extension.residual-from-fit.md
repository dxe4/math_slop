# Residual from fit

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

An observed response is $y=7$ and its fitted value is $\widehat y=5$. What is the residual under the convention $e=y-\widehat y$?

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":"2","checker_scope":"Checks only the requested exact finite answer under the stated model; it does not certify the reasoning or modeling assumptions."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Keep the order in the stated sign convention.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

A response above its fitted value has a positive residual.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Subtract fitted from observed.

1. The definition is $e=y-\widehat y$.
2. In this case $e=7-5=2$.
3. The positive sign means the fitted response is below the observation by $2$ response units.

</details>
