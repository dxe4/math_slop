# Recover a predictor from an outcome and residual

*Medium · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

The supplied line is $\widehat y=5-2x$. An observation has y=4 and residual 3, where residual=y-prediction. Find x.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The residual equation determines the fitted value before inverse prediction.

1. The residual equation gives prediction $4-3=1$.
2. Solving $1=5-2x$ gives $2x=4$.
3. Thus x equals two.
