# Choose the denominator in a book table

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

A shelf contains $10$ fiction books, $6$ nonfiction books and $4$ art books. Each book belongs to exactly one of these categories, and there are no other books on the shelf. What proportion of the books are fiction? Give an exact fraction.

| Book category | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Fiction | 10 |
| Nonfiction | 6 |
| Art | 4 |

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The denominator counts all books on the shelf, not just the books outside the requested category.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Add the three category counts, then divide the fiction count by that total.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A proportion compares the requested part with the whole group named in the question.

1. The total count is $10 + 6 + 4 = 20$ books.
2. The fiction proportion is $10/20 = 1/2$.

</details>
