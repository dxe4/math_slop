# Recover one value from a mean

*Easy · [Statistics and probability](index.md)*

Four observations have mean $6$. Three of the observations are $3, 5$ and $8$. What is the fourth observation?

| Given quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Number of observations | 4 |
| Mean | 6 |
| Known observations | 3, 5, 8 |

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The mean multiplied by the number of observations gives the total.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtract the sum of the three known observations from the required total.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The definition of mean also lets you recover a missing observation from a required total.

1. A mean of $6$ across four observations requires total $4 \times  6 = 24$.
2. The known observations total $3 + 5 + 8 = 16$, leaving $24 - 16 = 8$.

</details>
