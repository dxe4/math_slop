# Solve a linear complex equation

*Medium · [Complex analysis problems](index.md)*

Solve $(1+i)z=2-2i$ for $z$, in $a+bi$ form.

<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The nonzero coefficient can be divided out.

<details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Division by a nonzero complex coefficient gives a unique solution.

1. $z=(2-2i)/(1+i)$. Multiply numerator and denominator by $1-i$.
2. The numerator becomes $-4i$ and the denominator becomes $2$.
3. Thus $z=-2i$, and substitution gives $(1+i)(-2i)=2-2i$.

</details>
