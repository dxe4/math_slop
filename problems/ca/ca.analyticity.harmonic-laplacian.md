# Check a harmonic polynomial

*Easy · [Complex analysis problems](index.md)*

For $u(x,y)=x^2-y^2$, compute its Laplacian $u_{xx}+u_{yy}$.

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Take the second derivative in each real coordinate separately.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Retain the sign of the second squared term.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The two second derivatives cancel.

1. $u_{xx}=2$.
2. $u_{yy}=-2$.
3. The Laplacian is $2-2=0$.

</details>
