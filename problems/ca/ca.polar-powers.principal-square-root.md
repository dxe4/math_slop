<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The declared argument convention resolves the two square-root possibilities.

1. The principal polar data for $-4$ are modulus $4$ and argument $\pi$.
2. The selected square root has modulus $2$ and argument $\pi/2$.
3. It is $2i$; its square is $-4$.
