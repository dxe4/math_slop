# Add real and imaginary components

*Easy · [Complex analysis problems](index.md)*

Compute $(2+3i)+(1-5i)$ and enter the result in $a+bi$ form.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Complex addition is coordinatewise addition.

1. The real part is $2+1=3$.
2. The imaginary coefficient is $3-5=-2$.
3. The sum is $3-2i$.

</details>
