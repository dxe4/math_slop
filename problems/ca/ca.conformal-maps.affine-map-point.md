# Evaluate a rotation-dilation and translation

*Easy · [Complex analysis problems](index.md)*

Find the image of $z=1-i$ under $T(z)=(1+i)z+2$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Apply the complex multiplication before the translation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The two factors in the product are conjugates.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The affine map combines a complex scale with a translation.

1. $(1+i)(1-i)=1-i^2=2$.
2. Adding the translation gives $2+2=4$.
3. The image is the real complex value $4$.

</details>
