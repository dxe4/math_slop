# Find a geometric power-series radius

*Easy · [Complex analysis problems](index.md)*

Find the radius of convergence about zero of $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}z^n/2^n$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Identify the geometric ratio as a complex number.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Convergence requires its modulus to be strictly less than one.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The modulus of the geometric ratio specifies the convergence disk.

1. The ratio is $z/2$.
2. The geometric series converges for $|z/2|<1$, or $|z|<2$.
3. The radius is $2$.

</details>
