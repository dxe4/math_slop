# Evaluate a Cayley transformation

*Medium · [Complex analysis problems](index.md)*

For $T(z)=(z-i)/(z+i)$, find $T(1)$ in $a+bi$ form.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A Mobius map evaluation reduces to exact complex division.

1. $T(1)=(1-i)/(1+i)$.
2. Multiplying by $1-i$ in numerator and denominator gives $(1-i)^2/2=-2i/2$.
3. The image is $-i$.

</details>
