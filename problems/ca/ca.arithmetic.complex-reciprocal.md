# Find a complex reciprocal

*Easy · [Complex analysis problems](index.md)*

Find $1/(2-i)$ in $a+bi$ form.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Use the conjugate of the denominator.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Divide both resulting coefficients by the same real denominator.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The reciprocal equals conjugate divided by squared modulus.

1. The conjugate of $2-i$ is $2+i$.
2. The squared modulus is $2^2+1^2=5$.
3. The reciprocal is $(2+i)/5=2/5+i/5$.

</details>
