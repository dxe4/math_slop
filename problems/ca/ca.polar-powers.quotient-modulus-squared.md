# Use multiplicativity of the modulus

*Easy · [Complex analysis problems](index.md)*

Find $\left|(3+4i)/(1-2i)\right|^2$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Squared moduli multiply and divide just as moduli do.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Compute the two real sums of coordinate squares.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The quotient norm is a ratio of squared moduli.

1. The numerator has squared modulus $3^2+4^2=25$.
2. The denominator has squared modulus $1^2+(-2)^2=5$.
3. The requested squared modulus is $25/5=5$.

</details>
