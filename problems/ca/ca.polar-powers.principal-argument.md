# Choose the principal argument

*Easy · [Complex analysis problems](index.md)*

Use the principal argument convention $\operatorname{Arg}z\in(-\pi,\pi]$. Find $\operatorname{Arg}(-1-i)/\pi$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Locate the point in its quadrant and use equal absolute coordinates.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Choose the representative angle in the declared principal interval.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The principal interval selects one angle from the usual full-turn ambiguity.

1. The point has both coordinates negative and lies on the diagonal of the third quadrant.
2. An argument is $5\pi/4$, whose representative in $(-\pi,\pi]$ is $-3\pi/4$.
3. The requested ratio is $-3/4$.

</details>
