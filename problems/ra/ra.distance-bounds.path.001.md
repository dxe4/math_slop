# Find endpoints around a negative center

*Easy · [Real analysis](index.md)*

For real x, write $|x+3/2|<5/4$ as an open interval. Give the exact lower and upper endpoints in lower and upper; both endpoints are excluded.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A distance interval extends the same radius on each side of its center.

1. The center is −3/2=−6/4.
2. Subtracting and adding $5/4$ gives $-11/4<x<-1/4$.
