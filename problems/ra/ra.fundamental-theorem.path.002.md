# Evaluate a definite integral from an antiderivative

*Easy · [Real analysis](index.md)*

Evaluate ∫ from $1$ to $3$ of $2x$ dx. You may use the antiderivative $G(x)=x^{2}$ and continuity of the integrand.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An antiderivative converts accumulation into an endpoint difference.

1. The Fundamental Theorem gives the integral as $G(3)-G(1)$.
2. This equals $9-1=8$.
