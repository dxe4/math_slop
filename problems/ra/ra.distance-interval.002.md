# Locate a closed distance interval

*Easy · [Real analysis](index.md)*

For real x, rewrite $|x+2|\leq 3/4$ as a closed interval. Give the exact lower and upper endpoints in the fields lower and upper. Both endpoints are included.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Write $x+2$ as $x-(-2)$: the center is −2.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtract and add $3/4$ to the center $-2$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The sign in $x+2$ places the center at −2, not at $2$.

1. The condition is −3/4≤x+2≤3/4.
2. Subtract $2$ throughout to obtain $-11/4\leq x\leq -5/4$.
3. The nonstrict inequalities include both endpoints.

</details>
