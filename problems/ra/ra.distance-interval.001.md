# Turn a distance condition into endpoints

*Easy · [Real analysis](index.md)*

For real x, the condition $|x-3|<2/5$ describes an open interval. Give its lower and upper endpoints as exact numbers in the fields lower and upper. The endpoints themselves are excluded.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Distance less than r from c means $c-r<x<c+r$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Write $3$ with denominator $5$ before adding or subtracting $2/5$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A distance ball on the real line is an interval centered at the reference point.

1. The distance condition is equivalent to −2/5&lt;x−3&lt;2/5.
2. Add $3$ throughout: $13/5<x<17/5$.
3. Thus lower=$13/5$ and upper=$17/5$, both excluded.

</details>
