# Find sharp bounds from two magnitudes

*Medium · [Real analysis](index.md)*

For real u,v with \|u\|=7/3 and \|v\|=4/3, give the sharp lower and upper bounds of \|u−v\| over all sign choices.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Both triangle estimates are attained by suitable sign choices.

1. The lower bound is $|7/3-4/3|=1$, attained when the signs agree.
2. The upper bound is $7/3+4/3=11/3$, attained when the signs differ.
