# Congruence and remainders: congruence as divisibility

*Easy · [Number theory](index.md)*

Find the integer k such that $-25 - 3 = 7k$, witnessing $-25$ ≡ $3$ (mod 7).

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Congruence asks for a multiple of the modulus in the difference.

1. $-25 - 3 = -28$.
2. Since $-28 = 7 \times  (-4)$, the witness is $k = -4$.
