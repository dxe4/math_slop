# Normalize unequal outcome weights

*Easy · [Probability problems](index.md)*

A spinner has labels $1,2,3,4$ with probabilities proportional to weights $1,2,3,4$. Find the probability of a label at least $3$.

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Weights must be normalized by their total.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Add the weights of the desired labels before dividing.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Unequal weights replace counts in the finite-model ratio.

1. The total weight is $1+2+3+4=10$.
2. The desired labels have combined weight $3+4=7$.
3. The required probability is $7/10$.

</details>
