# Separate all bridge coordinates from the endpoint

*Medium · [Probability problems](index.md)*

Let $B$ be standard Brownian motion on $[0,1]$ and $U_t=B_t-tB_1$. Prove $\sigma(U_t:0\le t\le1)$ is independent of $B_1$. You may use Gaussian zero-covariance independence and the pi-lambda theorem.

<summary>Model proof</summary>

Finite Gaussian orthogonality gives the process independence after a sigma-field extension.

1. For any $t$, $\operatorname{Cov}(U_t,B_1)=\min(t,1)-t\operatorname{Var}(B_1)=t-t=0$.
2. Each vector $(U_{t_1},\ldots,U_{t_k},B_1)$ is jointly Gaussian, being a linear transformation of a Brownian finite-dimensional vector. Its zero cross-covariances imply independence of the bridge vector and $B_1$.
3. Finite-coordinate cylinder events form a pi-system generating $\sigma(U_t:0\le t\le1)$. For every endpoint event, the independence identity holds on this pi-system and extends by the pi-lambda theorem to the generated sigma-field.
