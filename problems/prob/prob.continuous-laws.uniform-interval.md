# Probability inside a uniform interval

*Easy · [Probability problems](index.md)*

A random variable is uniform on $[2,8]$. Find $P(3\le X\le5)$.

<summary>Hint 1</summary>

For a uniform continuous distribution, compare interval lengths.

<details>

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Uniform density converts interval length into probability.

1. The total support has length $8-2=6$, giving density $1/6$.
2. The requested interval has length $5-3=2$.
3. The probability is $2/6=1/3$.

</details>
