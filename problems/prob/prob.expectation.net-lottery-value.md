# Expected net return

*Easy · [Probability problems](index.md)*

A ticket costs $2$ units. It pays $8$ units with probability $1/5$ and pays nothing otherwise. Find the expected net return, including the ticket cost.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Distinguish the prize from the net return.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The ticket cost is paid in every outcome.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Subtract the fixed cost from the expected prize.

1. The expected prize is $(1/5)8+(4/5)0=8/5$.
2. The expected net return is $8/5-2$.
3. This equals $-2/5$ units.

</details>
