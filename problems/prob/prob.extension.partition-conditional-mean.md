# Conditional mean on a partition cell

*Easy · [Probability problems](index.md)*

On the uniform space $\Omega=\{1,2,3,4\}$, let $X(1)=0$, $X(2)=2$, $X(3)=4$, $X(4)=8$. Let $\mathcal G$ be generated by the partition $\{1,2\},\{3,4\}$. Find the value of $E[X\mid\mathcal G]$ at outcome $4$.

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":"6","checker_scope":"Checks the conditional-mean value at the specified positive-probability outcome only."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The observed information identifies a partition cell, not the exact outcome.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Average $X$ over the cell containing the specified point, with its conditional probability weights.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Conditional expectation is constant on each observed cell.

1. Outcome four lies in the cell $\{3,4\}$, which has probability $1/2$.
2. Uniform original masses make the two outcomes conditionally equally likely within that cell.
3. Their $X$ values are four and eight, so the cell mean and hence the requested value is $(4+8)/2=6$. It is not the realised value $X(4)=8$.

The conditional mean there is $(4+8)/2=6$.

</details>
