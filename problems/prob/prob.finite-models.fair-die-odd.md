# An event in a uniform finite model

*Easy · [Probability problems](index.md)*

A fair six-sided die has faces $1,\ldots,6$. Find the probability that its result is odd.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Fairness makes all individual faces equally likely.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Count favorable faces and divide by the full outcome count.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A uniform finite probability is a ratio of outcome counts.

1. The odd faces are $1,3,5$, giving three favorable outcomes.
2. There are six equally likely outcomes in total.
3. The probability is $3/6=1/2$.

</details>
