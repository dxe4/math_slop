# Evaluate the conserved quartic energy

*Easy · [Differential equations problems](index.md)*

For $x'=v$, v'=-x^3, the conserved energy is H=v^2/2+x^4/4. Find H on the trajectory through (x,v)=(1,2).

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An orbit's energy level is set by its state data.

1. The kinetic term at the point is 2^2/2=2.
2. The potential term is 1^4/4=1/4.
3. Hence H=2+1/4=9/4.
