# Check infinitesimal area change in a nonlinear oscillator

*Easy · [Differential equations problems](index.md)*

For the planar vector field $x'=v$, v'=-x-x^3, find its divergence in the coordinates (x,v).


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Differentiate each component with respect to its own state coordinate.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Cross derivatives do not enter divergence.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Nonlinear acceleration does not by itself imply area contraction.

1. The first component v has x derivative zero.
2. The second component -x-x^3 has v derivative zero.
3. The divergence is therefore zero.

</details>
