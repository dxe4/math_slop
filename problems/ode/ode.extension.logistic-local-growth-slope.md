# Evaluate a stated density-dependent growth law

*Easy · [Differential equations problems](index.md)*

A population satisfies $y'=3y(1-y/6)$. Find its instantaneous derivative when y=2.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Substitute the current population into both appearances of y.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The carrying capacity is six, not the current population.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A density-dependent rate is evaluated at the current state.

1. At y=2, the factor 1-y/6 is 1-1/3=2/3.
2. The other factor is 3y=6.
3. Thus yprime=6\*(2/3)=4.

</details>
