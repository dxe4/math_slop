<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Interior equilibria come from the non-coordinate factors.

1. At an equilibrium with x,y&gt;0, the first equation gives 2-y=0.
2. The second then requires x-3=0.
3. The equilibrium is \[3,2\].
