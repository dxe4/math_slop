# Reciprocal quartic minimum

*Medium · [Original Putnam-style training](index.md)*

For $x>0$, find the minimum of $x^2+4/x^2$.

<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Apply AM–GM to the two positive summands.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The positive choice $x=\sqrt2$ is allowed and attains the value $4$.

1. AM–GM gives $x^2+4/x^2\ge2\sqrt{x^2(4/x^2)}=4$.
2. Equality holds when $x^2=4/x^2$, or $x^4=4$.
3. The positive choice $x=\sqrt2$ is allowed and attains the value $4$.

</details>
