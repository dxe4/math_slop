<details>
<summary>Model proof</summary>

Cross-multiplication yields $PA\cdot PB=PC\cdot PD$.

1. Angles $\angle PAC$ and $\angle PDB$ intercept the same arc $CB$, and angles $\angle PCA$ and $\angle PBD$ intercept the same arc $AD$; hence triangles $PAC$ and $PDB$ are similar.
2. Correspondence gives $PA/PD=PC/PB$.
3. Cross-multiplication yields $PA\cdot PB=PC\cdot PD$.

**Self-assessment rubric**

- Identify both matching angle pairs from equal intercepted arcs.
- Establish the correct correspondence of triangles PAC and PDB.
- Cross-multiply the ratio to obtain PA·PB=PC·PD.

</details>
