<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The geometry associated with a reduced fraction depends on its denominator scale.

1. The fraction three eighths is reduced, so q=8.
2. The radius denominator is 2 times 8 squared, or 128.
3. The radius is one hundred twenty-eighth.
