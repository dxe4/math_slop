# Count equal rows

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

There are seven rows of tiles, with nine tiles in every row. How many tiles are there altogether?

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Every row contributes the same count.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Add nine seven times, or use multiplication.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Multiplication is a compact way to count repeated equal groups.

1. Seven equal groups of nine give the product $7 \times  9$.
2. The total is $63$ tiles.

</details>
