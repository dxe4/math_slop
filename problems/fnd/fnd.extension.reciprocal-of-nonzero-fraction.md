# Find a multiplicative reciprocal

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Find the reciprocal of $7/9$, meaning the rational number whose product with 7/9 is 1.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A reciprocal reverses numerator and denominator when neither is zero.

</details>

Check by multiplying the proposed number by 7/9.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The reciprocal is defined through a product equal to one.

1. Both 7 and 9 are nonzero.
2. The candidate 9/7 gives (7/9)(9/7)=63/63.
3. This product is 1, so the reciprocal is 9/7.

</details>
