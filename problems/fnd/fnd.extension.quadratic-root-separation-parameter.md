# Use the separation of two roots to determine a constant

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

The real roots of $x^2-6x+k=0$ are 4 units apart. Find k.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Root sum and separation determine the unordered pair.

1. Write the roots as r≤s. Their sum is r+s=6 and their difference is s−r=4.
2. Adding gives 2s=10, so s=5 and r=1.
3. Their product equals the constant k, hence k=5.

</details>
