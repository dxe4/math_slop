# Apply a percentage to its base

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A class contains $80$ students. Exactly $25$% take a bus. How many students take a bus?

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Percent means per hundred.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Convert $25$% to a fraction and multiply by eighty.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A percentage is a multiplicative ratio applied to an explicit base.

1. $25$% = $25/100 = 1/4$.
2. One quarter of $80$ is $80/4 = 20$ students.

</details>
