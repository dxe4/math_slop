# Track a stock after two changes

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A box initially holds $18$ pencils. Seven pencils are added, then nine are removed. How many pencils remain?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Track the changes in the stated order.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

First add seven to the original eighteen; subtract nine from that subtotal.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A change in a quantity can be modeled by addition for an increase and subtraction for a decrease.

1. After the addition there are $18 + 7 = 25$ pencils.
2. After the removal there are $25 - 9 = 16$ pencils.

</details>
