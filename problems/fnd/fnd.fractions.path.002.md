# Take a fractional portion

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A vessel contains $7/9$ litre. Two-sevenths of its contents are used. How many litres are used? Give an exact fraction.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The portion applies to the current contents.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Multiply $2/7$ by $7/9$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A portion scales its specified base quantity.

1. The used amount is $(2/7) \times  (7/9) = 14/63$ litre.
2. Reducing gives $2/9$ litre.

</details>
