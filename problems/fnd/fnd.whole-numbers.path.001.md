# Update a collection

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A drawer holds $32$ buttons. Eleven more are added and seven are used. How many buttons remain?

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":36,"checker_scope":"Checks only the exact stated structured result; explanation quality and general proof mastery are not assessed."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Account for the addition and removal separately.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Calculate the new subtotal before subtracting the used buttons.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The action in the story determines whether a count increases or decreases.

1. After the addition there are $32 + 11 = 43$ buttons.
2. Using seven leaves $43 - 7 = 36$ buttons.

</details>
