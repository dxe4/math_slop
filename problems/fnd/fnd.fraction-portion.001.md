# Take a fraction of an existing fraction

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A jug contains $3/4$ litre of juice. Two-thirds of the juice in the jug is poured out. How many litres are poured out? Give an exact fraction of a litre.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The question asks for part of the available amount, rather than adding two amounts.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Multiply the available quantity $3/4$ by the portion $2/3$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A fraction of a quantity is modeled by multiplication.

1. The amount poured out is $(2/3) \times  (3/4) = 6/12$ litre.
2. Reduce $6/12$ to $1/2$ litre. This is smaller than the available $3/4$ litre.

</details>
