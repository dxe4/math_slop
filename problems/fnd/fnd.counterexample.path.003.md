# Make the hypothesis true and conclusion false

Among the integers $4,8,6$, enter a counterexample to “If an integer is divisible by two, then it is divisible by four.”

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Check both halves of an implication when constructing a counterexample.

1. All three listed integers are divisible by $2$.
2. The integers $4$ and $8$ are also divisible by $4$.
3. The integer $6$ has remainder $2$ on division by $4$, so it satisfies the hypothesis and refutes the conclusion.
