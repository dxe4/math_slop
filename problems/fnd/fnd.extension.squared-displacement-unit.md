# Compute a squared planar displacement

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A displacement has perpendicular horizontal and vertical components 5 m and 12 m. Using $d^2=x^2+y^2$, find d² in square metres, not d in metres.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A numerical square and a length are different requested quantities.

1. The component squares are 25 m² and 144 m².
2. Adding gives d²=25+144=169 m².
3. The requested squared displacement is 169, even though d itself would be 13 m.
