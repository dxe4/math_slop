# Compare portions when their wholes differ

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Compare $3/4$ of $20$ metres with $2/3$ of $27$ metres. By how many metres does the larger resulting length exceed the smaller?


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Compute each portion using its own whole.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Then compare the actual lengths in metres.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Ordering bare fractions does not order portions of different wholes.

1. The first length is (3/4)×20=15 metres.
2. The second is (2/3)×27=18 metres.
3. Their difference is 18−15=3 metres.

</details>
