# Find a horizontal line slope

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

What is the slope dy/dx of the straight horizontal line $y=7$? Interpret this as the ratio of coordinate changes on two distinct points, with no calculus required.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The y-coordinate does not change along this line.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Divide that zero change by a nonzero horizontal change.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Slope measures change of height per horizontal change.

1. Choose points (0,7) and (1,7).
2. Their vertical change is zero and horizontal change is one.
3. The slope is 0/1=0.

</details>
