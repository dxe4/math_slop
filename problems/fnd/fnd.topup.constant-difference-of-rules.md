# Find a difference independent of the input

*Hard · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Let $A=3(2x-5)-2(x+1)$ and $B=4(x-4)$. Their difference A-B has the same value for every real x. Find that value.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The variable terms cancel after correct distribution and subtraction.

1. Expanding gives $A=6x-15-2x-2=4x-17$.
2. Also $B=4x-16$.
3. Thus $A-B=(4x-17)-(4x-16)=-1$ for every real x.
