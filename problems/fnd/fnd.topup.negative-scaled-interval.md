<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The closed endpoint is retained when dividing by a negative coefficient.

1. Expand to get $-6x+3\leq9$, hence $-6x\leq6$.
2. Dividing by -6 gives $x\geq-1$.
3. The solution interval is $[-1,\infty)$.

</details>
