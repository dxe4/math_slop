# Move left from a negative point

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A marker at −3 moves six units left on a number line. What is its final coordinate?

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A leftward move is negative.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Add the displacement $-6$ to the starting coordinate $-3$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The final position is the initial position plus signed displacement.

1. The calculation is −3 + (−6).
2. Both moves are left of zero, giving a final coordinate of −9.

</details>
