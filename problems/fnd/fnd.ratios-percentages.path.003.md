# Recover a part from a ratio

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Red and blue beads have ratio $3$:$5$. There are forty beads altogether. How many are red?

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The ratio compares two parts of the collection.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

There are eight total ratio parts, of which three are red.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A part-to-part ratio becomes a fraction of the total by adding its entries.

1. Each ratio part contains $40/(3 + 5) = 5$ beads.
2. Red occupies three parts, so there are $3 \times  5 = 15$ red beads.

</details>
