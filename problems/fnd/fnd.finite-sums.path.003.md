# Combine a supplied sum and two remaining terms

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Suppose $\sum _{k=1}^3$ $a_{k}=8, a_{4}=-2$ and $a_{5}=6$. Find $\sum _{k=1}^5$ $a_{k}$.

1. Split the full sum as $\sum _{k=1}^3$ $a_{k}+a_{4}+a_{5}$.
2. The value is $8+(-2)+6=12$.
