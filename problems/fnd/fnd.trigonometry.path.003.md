# Read a clockwise quarter turn

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

The unit-circle point at angle $-\pi /2$ is $(0,-1)$. Find $\sin (-\pi /2)$ and $\cos (-\pi /2)$; return sin and cos.

<details>

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The negative angle indicates a clockwise turn.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Signed angles can give negative coordinate values.

1. At the supplied point $(0,-1)$, the vertical coordinate is −1 and the horizontal coordinate is $0$.
2. Therefore sin=$-1$ and cos=$0$.

</details>
