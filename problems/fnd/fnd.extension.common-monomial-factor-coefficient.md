# Read the coefficient of a common monomial factor

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Factor $6x^2y+9xy^2$ as $cxy(2x+3y)$ for all real x,y. Find the positive integer c.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Compare the first coefficient with 2c.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Check the second coefficient with 3c.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

One common scalar must reproduce both terms.

1. Expanding cxy(2x+3y) gives 2cx²y+3cxy².
2. Matching 2c=6 gives c=3.
3. The other coefficient is then 3c=9 as required.

</details>
