# Choose total time as the averaging denominator

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A cyclist travels 6 km at $30\,\mathrm{km/h}$ and another $6$ km at $60\,\mathrm{km/h}$, with no stops. Find the average speed for the complete trip in km/h.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Find the duration of each equal-distance leg.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Average speed is total distance divided by total time, not the mean of the two speeds.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Equal distances generally require unequal times.

1. The leg times are 6/30=1/5 hour and 6/60=1/10 hour.
2. The complete trip covers 12 km in 3/10 hour.
3. Average speed is 12÷(3/10)=40 km/h.

</details>
