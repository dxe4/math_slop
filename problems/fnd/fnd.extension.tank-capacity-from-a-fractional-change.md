# Recover the unit whole from a change of fractions

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A tank rises from $2/5$ full to $7/10$ full when exactly 18 litres are added. No liquid escapes. Find its capacity in litres.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Find the fraction of the whole represented by the added liquid.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Divide the added volume by that fraction.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The fraction change must refer to the same tank capacity.

1. The increase is 7/10−2/5=3/10 of the capacity.
2. Thus 3/10 of the full capacity equals 18 litres.
3. The capacity is 18÷(3/10)=60 litres.

</details>
