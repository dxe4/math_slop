# Compute an exact unit price

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Exactly $1.2\,\mathrm{kg}$ of flour costs £3. With a proportional price, find the price per kilogram in pounds.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A price per kilogram normalizes the mass to one kilogram.

1. The unit price is 3÷1.2 pounds per kilogram.
2. This is 3÷(6/5)=3×5/6.
3. The price is 5/2 pounds per kilogram.
