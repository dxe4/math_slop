# Use a reciprocal power

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Find log₃$(1/9)$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A positive base greater than one produces a reciprocal when its exponent is negative.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Express one-ninth as a power of three.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Negative exponents describe reciprocals of positive powers.

1. Since $3^{2}=9$, the reciprocal is $1/9=3^{-2}$.
2. Thus log₃$(1/9)=-2$.

</details>
