<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Inverse operations recover a positive logarithm argument.

1. The value log₂7 is the real exponent whose power of 2 equals 7.
2. Raising 2 to that exponent returns the logarithm’s argument.
3. Thus the expression equals 7.
