<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An absolute-distance bound describes all points within a radius of its centre.

1. The distance condition is equivalent to $-3\leq x-2\leq3$.
2. Adding $2$ throughout gives $-1\leq x\leq5$.
3. Both endpoints are included, so the answer is $[-1,5]$.

</details>
