# Combine repeated small volumes with a litre amount

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Three cups each contain exactly $250\,\mathrm{mL}$, and a jug contains 0.8 L. All liquid is combined without loss. Find the total volume in litres as a fraction.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Repeated quantities and units must both be handled before combining.

1. The cups hold 3×250=750 mL=3/4 litre.
2. The jug holds 0.8=4/5 litre.
3. The total is 3/4+4/5=31/20 litres.
