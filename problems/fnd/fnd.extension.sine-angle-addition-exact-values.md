# Combine two angles through their signed coordinates

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Given sinα=3/5, cosα=4/5, sinβ=5/13 and cosβ=12/13, find $\sin(\alpha+\beta)$ exactly.

Use sin(α+β)=sinα cosβ+cosα sinβ.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Multiply cross-angle pairs before adding.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Angle addition combines coordinate information from two angles.

1. The first product is (3/5)(12/13)=36/65.
2. The second is (4/5)(5/13)=20/65.
3. Their sum is 56/65.

</details>
