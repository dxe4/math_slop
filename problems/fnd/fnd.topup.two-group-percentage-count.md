# Use the right base within each group

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A group has $40$ people. Exactly $60\%$ cycle. Half of the cyclists and one-quarter of the others wear hats. How many people wear hats?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Split the original group before applying the two different hat fractions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The noncyclists are the complement of the cyclists, not another forty-person base.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Each percentage or fraction uses its own stated group as the base.

1. There are $40\cdot60/100=24$ cyclists and $40-24=16$ others.
2. Hat wearers number $24/2=12$ among cyclists and $16/4=4$ among the others.
3. The total is $12+4=16$.

</details>
