# Prove a divisibility consequence directly

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Let a,b be arbitrary integers with $a=3r+1$ and $b=3s+2$ for some integers r,s. Prove that $ab+1$ is divisible by three.

<details>
<summary>Model proof</summary>

Divisibility follows by constructing its required integer quotient.

1. Substitution gives $ab+1=(3r+1)(3s+2)+1$.
2. Expanding yields $9rs+6r+3s+3=3(3rs+2r+s+1)$.
3. The expression in parentheses is an integer, so ab+1 is divisible by three for every permitted a,b.
