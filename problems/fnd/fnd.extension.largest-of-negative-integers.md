Find the largest integer in $\{-11,-2,-5\}$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A smaller negative magnitude means a larger negative number.

1. The ordering is −11&lt;−5&lt;−2.
2. The point −2 is farthest right among the three.
3. Thus the largest listed integer is −2.
