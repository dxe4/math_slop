# Coordinate two sign conventions

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Evaluate $\sqrt{(-7)^2}-(-2)^3$ over the real numbers, using the principal square root.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The square root is nonnegative even when the number being squared is negative.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The cube retains its negative sign, and subtracting it changes that sign once more.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Principal-root and odd-power conventions must be applied separately.

1. The principal root is $\sqrt{49}=7$.
2. The parenthesized cube is $(-2)^3=-8$.
3. The expression is $7-(-8)=15$.

</details>
