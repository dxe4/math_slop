# Read place value across zero digits

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

In the base-ten integer $50704$, what value does the digit 7 contribute?

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Zero digits preserve positions even when they contribute no value.

1. Reading from the right gives units 4, tens 0 and hundreds 7.
2. The 7 therefore contributes 7×100.
3. Its value is 700, rather than 7 or 7000.

</details>
