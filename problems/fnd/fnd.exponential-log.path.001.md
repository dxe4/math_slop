# Use repeated doubling

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A quantity starts at $3$ units and doubles once per step. What is its value after four steps?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Apply four multiplications by two to the initial amount.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The step-zero amount is three; the first doubled amount is six.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Exponential growth counts the number of multiplicative steps.

1. The four-step multiplier is $2^{4} = 16$.
2. The final amount is $3 \times  16 = 48$ units.

</details>
