# Keep a fixed amount of work under a worker change

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Three identical workers complete a job in $8$ hours. At the same constant individual work rate, how many hours do five workers need? Assume work divides perfectly with no overhead.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Worker count and completion time vary inversely for the stated fixed-work model.

1. The job requires 3×8=24 worker-hours.
2. Five workers provide five worker-hours per elapsed hour.
3. The time is 24/5 hours.
