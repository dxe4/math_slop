# Weight concentrations by their actual volumes

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Mix 2 L of a $20\%$ solution with 3 L of a 50% solution. Assume additive volumes and no loss. What fraction of the final volume is solute?

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Concentrations average by volume weights, not by taking an unweighted midpoint.

1. The solute volumes are 2×1/5=2/5 L and 3×1/2=3/2 L.
2. Their sum is 19/10 L, and total solution volume is 5 L.
3. The fraction is (19/10)/5=19/50.

</details>
