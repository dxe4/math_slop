# Solve a reciprocal inequality without unsafe cross multiplication

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Solve $1/x>2$ over real x, excluding x=0. Give the full solution interval.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The denominator’s sign determines whether clearing it preserves an inequality.

1. For x&lt;0 the left side is negative and cannot exceed 2.
2. For x&gt;0 multiplication by x gives 1&gt;2x, hence x&lt;1/2.
3. The solution is (0,1/2), with both endpoints excluded.

</details>
