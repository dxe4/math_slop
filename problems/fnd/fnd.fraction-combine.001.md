# Add lengths measured in different fractions

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Two pieces of cord have lengths $1/4$ metre and $2/3$ metre. What is their total length as an exact fraction of a metre?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The denominator describes the size of each piece, so first make the piece sizes match.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Both quarters and thirds can be expressed in twelfths.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Addition of fractions requires a common unit before adding numerators.

1. Rewrite $1/4$ as $3/12$ and $2/3$ as $8/12$.
2. Add like-sized pieces: $3/12 + 8/12 = 11/12$ metre.

</details>
