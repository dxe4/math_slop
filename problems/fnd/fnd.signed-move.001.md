# Move right from a negative position

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A marker is at −4 on a number line. It moves nine units to the right. What is its final coordinate?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A move to the right increases the coordinate.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use four of the nine units to reach zero; then account for the remaining units.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An initial coordinate plus a signed displacement gives the final coordinate.

1. Four units to the right take the marker from $-4$ to $0$.
2. Five of the nine units remain, so the final coordinate is $5$. Equivalently, $-4 + 9 = 5$.

</details>
