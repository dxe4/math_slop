# Raise a negative base to an even power

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Evaluate $(-3)^4$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The parentheses make $-3$ the base of every factor.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Pair negative factors before multiplying the positive products.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An even number of negative factors has a positive product.

1. $(-3)^4 = (-3) (-3) (-3) (-3)$.
2. Each pair multiplies to nine, and $9 \times  9 = 81$.

</details>
