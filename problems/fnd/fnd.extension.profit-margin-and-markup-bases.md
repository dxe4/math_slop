# Use different bases for margin and markup

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

An item costs £$80$ and sells for £$100$. Return \[profit margin percent, markup percent\], where margin divides profit by selling price and markup divides profit by cost. Enter percentage numbers, not fractions of one.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The same monetary gain gives different percentages under different reference bases.

1. The profit is 100−80=20 pounds.
2. Margin is 20/100=20%, whereas markup is 20/80=25%.
3. The requested vector is \[20,25\].
