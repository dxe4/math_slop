# Use a fraction of a remaining ribbon

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A roll has $5/8$ metre of ribbon left. A craft uses three-fifths of that remaining ribbon. How many metres does the craft use? Give an exact fraction.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Three-fifths refers to the ribbon currently left, not to an original full metre.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Write the requested quantity as $(3/5)$ times $(5/8)$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Identify the base quantity before applying a fractional portion.

1. Multiply the fractions: $(3/5) \times  (5/8) = 15/40$ metre.
2. Divide numerator and denominator by five to obtain $3/8$ metre.

</details>
