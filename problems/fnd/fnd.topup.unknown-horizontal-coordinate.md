<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The slope definition can be solved backward for a coordinate.

1. The slope condition is $3=(8-2)/(4-a)$ with a not equal to four.
2. Cross-multiplying gives $3(4-a)=6$, hence $12-3a=6$.
3. Thus a=2, which gives distinct points and the required slope.

</details>
