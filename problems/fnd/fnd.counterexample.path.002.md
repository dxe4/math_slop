# Refute a strict universal inequality

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

The claim is “For every real $x$, $x^2>x$.” Among the supplied candidates $2,0,-1$, enter the value that refutes it. You may use $2^2=4$, $0^2=0$, and $(-1)^2=1$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Refute a universal strict inequality with one permitted input.

1. At $x=2$ the comparison $4>2$ is true.
2. At $x=-1$ the comparison $1>-1$ is true.
3. At $x=0$ the comparison would be $0>0$, which is false, giving the required counterexample.
