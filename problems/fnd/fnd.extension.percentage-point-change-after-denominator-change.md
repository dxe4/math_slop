# Distinguish percentage points from a relative percent change

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A jar has $30$ red and $20$ blue counters. Ten blue counters are added. By how many percentage points does the red percentage decrease?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Compute the red fraction using each total separately.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtract the two percentages; a percentage-point change is their ordinary numerical difference.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The red count stays fixed while its reference total changes.

1. Originally the red fraction is 30/50=60%.
2. After the addition it is 30/60=50%.
3. The decrease is 60−50=10 percentage points, not 10% of the old percentage.

</details>
