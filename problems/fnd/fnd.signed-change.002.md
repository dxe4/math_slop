# Calculate a downward height change

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Relative to a chosen reference level, a lift moves from height $5$ metres to height $-3$ metres. Define signed height change as final minus initial height. What is its signed change, in metres?

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A downward change has a negative sign when increasing height is positive.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtract the starting height $5$ from the final height $-3$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A signed change and the nonnegative distance traveled answer different questions.

1. Compute final minus initial: $-3 - 5$.
2. The result is −8 metres; the magnitude eight gives the vertical separation between the endpoints, while the negative sign describes the downward change.

</details>
