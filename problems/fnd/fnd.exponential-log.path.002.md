# Read a logarithm as an exponent

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Find log₂$(32)$.

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":5,"checker_scope":"Checks only the exact stated structured result; explanation quality and general proof mastery are not assessed."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Look for the exponent, not the value of another power.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

List powers of two until the output is thirty-two.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A logarithm inverts exponentiation at its stated base.

1. The powers are $2^{1}=2, 2^{2}=4, 2^{3}=8, 2^{4}=16, 2^{5}=32$.
2. Therefore log₂$(32)=5$.

</details>
