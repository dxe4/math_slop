<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A branch restriction makes the quadratic invertible.

1. The equation (x−3)²+1=10 gives (x−3)²=9.
2. The algebraic candidates are x=0 and x=6.
3. Only 6 lies in \[3,∞), so f⁻¹(10)=6.
