# Calculate a slope from two points

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Find the slope of the line through $(-1,2)$ and $(3,10)$.

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Slope is vertical change divided by horizontal change.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtract the first point from the second in both changes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A slope compares changes rather than individual coordinate values.

1. The changes are $10 - 2 = 8$ vertically and $3 - (-1) = 4$ horizontally.
2. The slope is $8/4 = 2$.

</details>
