# Advance a recurrence the right number of times

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A sequence has $a_{1}=3$ and $a_{n+1}=a_{n}+2$ for integers $n\geq 1$. Find $a_{4}$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A recurrence needs its initial index as well as its update rule.

1. Starting at $a_{1}=3$, the next terms are $a_{2}=5$ and $a_{3}=7$.
2. One more update gives $a_{4}=9$.
