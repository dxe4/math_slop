<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A logarithm need not be an integer.

1. If y=log₄8, then 4ʸ=8.
2. Using 4=2² and 8=2³ gives 2^{2y}=2³, hence 2y=3.
3. Therefore y=3/2.
