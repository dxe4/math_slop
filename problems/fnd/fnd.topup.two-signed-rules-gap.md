# Compare results after removing double negatives

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Compute $A=-(-4)-7$ and $B=-(-7)-4$. What is the nonnegative distance between A and B on the number line?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Remove each double negative before subtracting.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The distance is the larger result minus the smaller one.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Computing the signed endpoints first separates signs from geometric distance.

1. The first value is $A=4-7=-3$.
2. The second is $B=7-4=3$.
3. The distance is $3-(-3)=6$.

</details>
