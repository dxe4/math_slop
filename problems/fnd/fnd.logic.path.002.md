# Negate a conjunction

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Which statement is the logical negation of “P and Q”? Select the mathematically correct statement from the four alternatives.

**Options**

1. Not P and not Q.
2. Not P or not Q.
3. P or Q.
4. P and not Q.

<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The conjunction fails if either requirement fails.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Its negation must also allow the case where exactly one of P and Q is false.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Negating an all-requirements connective changes it to an either-failure connective.

1. P and Q requires both statements to hold.
2. Its negation says that at least one does not hold: not P or not Q. Select the statement “Not P or not Q.”.

</details>
