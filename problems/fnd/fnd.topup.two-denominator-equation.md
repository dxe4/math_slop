<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

One nonzero common multiplier preserves the equation and removes both fractions.

1. Multiply by six to get $2(x-2)+3(x+1)=24$.
2. Expanding and collecting gives $5x-1=24$.
3. Thus $x=5$, and the original left side is one plus three, equal to four.
