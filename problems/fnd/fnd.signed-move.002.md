# Move left through zero

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A marker is at $3$ on a number line. It moves eight units to the left. What is its final coordinate?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A move to the left is a negative displacement.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Three of the eight units reach zero; continue left by the unused amount.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Crossing zero changes the sign of a coordinate without changing the meaning of the direction.

1. Moving three units left takes $3$ to $0$.
2. The remaining five units take the marker to −5; $3 + (-8) = -5$.

</details>
