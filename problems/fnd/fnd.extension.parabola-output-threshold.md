<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A parabola threshold can be expressed as a bounded distance from its axis.

1. The condition is −2(x−3)²+5≥−3, hence −2(x−3)²≥−8.
2. Dividing by −2 gives (x−3)²≤4, equivalent to \|x−3\|≤2.
3. Therefore 1≤x≤5 and the interval is \[1,5\].
