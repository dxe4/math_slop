# Recover the base of a percentage decrease

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

After a $25\%$ discount, a price is £90. Find the original price in pounds.

<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Divide by 3/4 rather than adding 25% of 90.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A reverse percentage calculation uses the original reference whole.

1. A 25% reduction leaves the factor 1−1/4=3/4.
2. Thus (3/4)P=90 for the original price P.
3. Solving gives P=90×4/3=120 pounds.

</details>
