<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A zero numerator may be included, but a zero denominator never is.

1. The critical inputs are −1, where the expression is undefined, and 2, where it is zero.
2. Both factors are negative for x&lt;−1, have opposite signs on (−1,2), and are positive for x&gt;2.
3. The solution is (−∞,−1)∪\[2,∞), including 2 and excluding −1.
