<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Positive denominators preserve comparison when a common denominator is introduced.

1. The common denominator 54 gives 5/6=45/54.
2. The other fraction is 7/9=42/54.
3. Since 45&gt;42, the larger value is 5/6.
