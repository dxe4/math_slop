# Subtract a smaller length from a strip

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A strip is $4/5$ metre long. A piece of length $1/6$ metre is cut off. What length remains? Give an exact fraction of a metre.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The remaining length is the original length minus the removed length.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Express both lengths as numbers of thirtieths.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The same common-unit method works for subtraction as for addition.

1. The original length is $24/30$ metre and the removed length is $5/30$ metre.
2. Subtract: $24/30 - 5/30 = 19/30$ metre.

</details>
