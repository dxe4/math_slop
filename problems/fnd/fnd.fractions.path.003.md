<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Equal positive denominators turn the comparison into an integer comparison.

1. We have $5/8=15/24$ and $2/3=16/24$.
2. The denominators agree and $16>15$.
3. Therefore the greater number is $2/3$.
