# Recover a component of a weighted mean

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Two exam scores have weights $2$ and $3$. Their weighted mean is 74, and the weight-2 score is 80. Find the other score, with no rounding.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A weighted mean divides the weighted sum by the total weight.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Multiply by five before solving for the missing score.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Weights multiply scores before averaging.

1. Let the missing score be s. Then (2×80+3s)/5=74.
2. Multiplying gives 160+3s=370, so 3s=210.
3. Thus s=70.

</details>
