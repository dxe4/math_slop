# Scale a ratio from a supplied component

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A paint mix has red:blue volume ratio 2:7. It contains exactly $12$ litres of red paint. How many litres of blue paint are required?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Match the two red ratio parts to 12 litres.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use the same litres-per-part for the blue component.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The ratio parts share a common scale.

1. Two parts correspond to 12 litres, so one part is 6 litres.
2. Blue paint has seven parts.
3. Its volume is 7×6=42 litres.

</details>
