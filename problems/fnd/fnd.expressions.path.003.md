# Factor a common multiplier

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Complete the factorisation identity $6x+9=3(2x+c)$ for every real $x$. Enter the exact integer $c$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Factoring removes the common factor from every term.

1. The right-hand side expands to $6x+3c$.
2. Equality for every real $x$ requires $3c=9$.
3. Hence $c=3$, giving $6x+9=3(2x+3)$.

</details>
