# Use the cube volume rule in consistent units

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A cube has edge length $2$ cm. Using V=s³, find its volume in cubic centimetres.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The three length factors produce a cubic unit.

1. Substitute s=2 cm into V=s³.
2. The numerical product is 2×2×2=8.
3. The volume is 8 cm³.
