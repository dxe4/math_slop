# Use radians in an arc-length formula

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

An arc lies on a circle of radius 3 cm and subtends $2$ radians. Using s=rθ for θ in radians, find its length in centimetres.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The angle is already in the unit required by the formula.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Multiply the radius by the numerical radian angle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The radian measure is the arc-to-radius ratio.

1. Substitute r=3 cm and θ=2.
2. The formula gives s=3×2 cm.
3. The arc length is 6 cm.

</details>
