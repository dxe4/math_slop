# Convert an area unit by squaring the length factor

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

How many square centimetres equal $1\text{ m}^2$? Use 1 m=100 cm.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A square metre is a square with two one-metre side lengths.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Convert each side before multiplying.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Area conversion uses the square of the length conversion factor.

1. Each side becomes 100 cm.
2. The area is 100×100 square centimetres.
3. Therefore 1 m²=10000 cm².

</details>
