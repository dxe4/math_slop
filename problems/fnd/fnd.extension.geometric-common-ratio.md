# Read a multiplicative sequence step

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A geometric sequence begins 5,15,45. Find its common ratio r, defined by $a_{n+1}=ra_n$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Divide a term by the preceding nonzero term.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Check the same ratio on the next pair.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A geometric rule has a constant multiplier rather than a constant difference.

1. The first ratio is 15/5=3.
2. The next ratio is 45/15=3 as well.
3. Thus the common ratio is 3.

</details>
