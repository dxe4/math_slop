# Recover an intercept through a signed slope

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A line passes through $(-2,7)$ and $(4,-5)$. Find its vertical-axis intercept, meaning its y-coordinate when x=0.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The two-point data determine both the slope and the intercept.

1. The slope is $(-5-7)/(4-(-2))=-12/6=-2$.
2. Using (-2,7) gives $7=(-2)(-2)+b=4+b$.
3. The intercept is $b=3$.

</details>
