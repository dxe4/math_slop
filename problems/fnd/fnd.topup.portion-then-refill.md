# Combine a fractional portion with a refill

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A jug contains $3/4$ litre of juice. You pour out $2/3$ of that juice, then add $1/6$ litre. How much juice is in the jug now, in litres?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The poured fraction refers to the juice initially present, not to a whole litre.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

After removing that portion, combine the remaining amount with the stated refill.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A fraction of a quantity is multiplied before the later addition.

1. The poured amount is $(2/3)(3/4)=1/2$ litre.
2. The amount left before refilling is $3/4-1/2=1/4$ litre.
3. After the refill it is $1/4+1/6=5/12$ litre.

</details>
