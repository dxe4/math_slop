# Convert a compound duration to seconds

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A timer shows a duration of $2$ minutes $35$ seconds. How many seconds is this? Use 60 seconds per minute.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Convert only the minute part first.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Add the already-second part afterward.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Both parts must use the requested unit.

1. Two minutes are 2×60=120 seconds.
2. The extra 35 seconds already have the target unit.
3. The duration is 120+35=155 seconds.

</details>
