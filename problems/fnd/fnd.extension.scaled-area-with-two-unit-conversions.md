# Apply an area scale and report square metres

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A plan has length scale 1:50. A region occupies $2\text{ cm}^2$ on the plan. Find the actual area in square metres, assuming the same scale in both directions.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Convert square centimetres using 10000 cm² per m².

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Area grows by the square of a uniform length factor.

1. The area scale is 50²=2500.
2. The actual area is 2×2500=5000 cm².
3. Since 10000 cm²=1 m², this is 1/2 m².

</details>
