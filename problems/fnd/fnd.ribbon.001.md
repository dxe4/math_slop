# Subtract lengths in different units of a fraction

*Easy · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

A strip is $7/8$ metre long. You cut off $1/3$ metre. Give the remaining length as an exact fraction of a metre.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Fractions can be subtracted after expressing them in a common unit.

1. $7/8=21/24$ and $1/3=8/24$.
2. Subtract equal units: $21/24-8/24=13/24$ metre.
