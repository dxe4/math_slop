# Keep a common whole when joining two remainders

*Medium · [Foundations bridge](index.md)*

Two strips have the same original length. On one strip $3/5$ is painted, and on the other $4/7$ is painted. Join the two unpainted pieces. Their total length is what fraction of one original strip?

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Complements are added in units of one original strip, not of the combined pair.

1. The unpainted pieces have lengths $1-3/5=2/5$ and $1-4/7=3/7$ of a strip.
2. Adding with denominator thirty-five gives $14/35+15/35$.
3. The joined length is $29/35$ of one original strip.

</details>
