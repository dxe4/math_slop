# Geometry from proof to curvature audit studio

Solved exercises spanning every geometry and trigonometry topic while separating Euclidean hypotheses, coordinate predicates, physical measurement, projective invariants, and curved metrics.

**Objectives**

- Prove metric and incidence claims with their parallel, convexity, and nondegeneracy assumptions visible
- Compute planar, spatial, spherical, hyperbolic, and projective quantities with correct units and domains
- Audit ambiguous construction, floating-point boundary, and physical measurement claims
