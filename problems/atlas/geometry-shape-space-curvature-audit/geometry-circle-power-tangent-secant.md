# Cross-check tangent length by circle power

*Difficulty 3 of 5 · [Geometry from proof to curvature audit studio](index.md) · exercise 5 of 12*

A circle is centered at the origin with radius 5, and $P=(13,0)$. Find the tangent length from P. Then use the x-axis secant intersections to verify the same power product.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Power is |P| squared minus radius squared

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The x-axis meets the circle at $x=5$ and $x=-5$

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 3</summary>

External and whole secant lengths are 8 and 18

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Power is $13^2-5^2=169-25=144$. For a tangent point T, $PT^2=144$, so the nonnegative tangent length is 12. Along the x-axis, the nearer intersection is (5,0), distance 8 from P, and the farther is (-5,0), distance 18. Their product is $8\cdot 18=144$, matching the same power. Using only the internal chord length 10 would be the wrong secant quantity.

**Answer.** Tangent length is 12; the secant audit gives $8\cdot 18=144=12^2$.

</details>
