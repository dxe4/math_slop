# Build a seven-column claim boundary

*Difficulty 5 of 5 · [Research frontiers claim-boundary studio](index.md) · exercise 12 of 12*

Classify these headlines and repair each in one sentence: `Energy decay proves smooth fluids`; `Ten trillion checked zeros prove RH`; `Brute force is exponential, so P differs from NP`; `Swapped Hodge numbers prove every mirror conjecture`; `A long barcode is biological truth`; `Few measurements recover any signal`; `One ensemble spread is total climate uncertainty`. Then give the shared logical failure.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

For each headline restore the missing solution class, quantifier, computational model, formulation, metric, signal model, or uncertainty source

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Look for a partial or conditional result being promoted to a universal conclusion

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Repairs: energy decay is an L2 estimate and does not control all derivatives of every three-dimensional solution; a rigorous zero check proves RH only in its finite height range; exponential brute force lower-bounds one algorithm rather than every polynomial-time algorithm; Hodge-number exchange is a numerical mirror consistency test rather than every enumerative, categorical, or geometric correspondence; a long bar is persistent in a chosen metric/filtration and needs sampling and scientific inference; undersampled recovery needs sparsity and compatible measurement conditions; ensemble spread is conditional on scenarios and construction and omits or entangles several uncertainties. The shared failure is quantifier and model-scope inflation: a theorem, computation, or representation valid under stated conditions is promoted beyond its object, hypotheses, range, or inferential design.

**Answer.** Each headline inflates a scoped result into a universal claim by deleting a class, condition, finite range, model, or uncertainty layer.

</details>
