# Research frontiers claim-boundary studio

Solved audits in which the main task is to preserve hypotheses, quantifiers, solution classes, finite ranges, probability sources, model layers, and excluded stronger conclusions.

**Objectives**

- Restate a frontier claim with its exact mathematical object and quantifiers
- Distinguish a partial theorem, finite computation, or conditional model guarantee from a universal conclusion
- Identify the missing logical bridge in an overclaimed result
- Use authoritative dated status and primary theorem sources at their stated scope
