# An enormous finite height remains finite

*Difficulty 1 of 5 · [Research frontiers claim-boundary studio](index.md) · exercise 3 of 12*

A rigorous interval computation proves that every nontrivial zeta zero with imaginary part between zero and `3*10^12` lies on the critical line. Write the strongest justified statement and explain in one logical sentence why it is not the Riemann hypothesis.

<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Retain the height qualifier

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Compare a bounded universal quantifier with an unbounded one

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The justified statement is: every nontrivial zero `beta+i gamma` in the rigorously covered range `0<gamma<=3*10^12` has `beta=1/2` (and any additional certified properties should be quoted exactly). The Riemann hypothesis quantifies over every nontrivial zero with no upper height. A counterexample could logically occur above the completed finite range, so the bounded theorem does not imply the unbounded one.

**Answer.** RH is verified in the stated finite range only; its universal quantifier has no height cutoff.

</details>
