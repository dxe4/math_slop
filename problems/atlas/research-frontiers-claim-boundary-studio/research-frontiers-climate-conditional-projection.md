# Read a projection conditionally

*Difficulty 4 of 5 · [Research frontiers claim-boundary studio](index.md) · exercise 11 of 12*

A model ensemble produces warming ranges under two forcing scenarios. A summary selects the higher range and calls it `the temperature prediction`, treating the ensemble spread as total uncertainty. Rewrite the claim and build an uncertainty ledger.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A scenario is a condition, not a forecast that the pathway will occur

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Ensemble members can share structure and omit uncertainty sources

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Report each range as a projection conditional on its stated forcing scenario, model versions, initialization, period, baseline, and summary statistic. Do not collapse scenarios unless a probability model for scenario occurrence is separately justified. The ledger includes scenario/forcing uncertainty, internal variability, parameter uncertainty, structural and parameterization discrepancy, observation and calibration uncertainty, numerical approximation, ensemble sampling, and dependence from shared code or ancestry. Ensemble spread may represent only part of this ledger and members may not be probability draws. Validation and hindcast skill must match the target; a global mean does not describe every region or event.

**Answer.** State scenario-conditional ranges and separately record scenario, variability, parameter, structural, observational, numerical, sampling, and dependence uncertainties.

</details>
