# Mathematical physics audit studio

Solved derivations across mechanics, fields, spacetime, continua, quantum theory, ensembles, gauge geometry, and quantum-field-theory boundaries.

**Objectives**

- Derive physical equations with units and sign conventions visible
- Supply compatible initial, boundary, domain, or ensemble data
- Distinguish invariant observables from coordinate or gauge descriptions
- Classify approximation and construction claims without overstating them
