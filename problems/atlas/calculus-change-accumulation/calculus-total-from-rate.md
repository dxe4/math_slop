# Recover a total from a rate

*Difficulty 2 of 5 · [Change and accumulation](index.md) · exercise 3 of 5*

Water enters a basin at rate $q(t)=3+t^2$ liters per minute for $0 \le t \le 2$. How much water enters, and what is the average inflow rate?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Integrate the rate over time

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Average value is total divided by interval length

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The total inflow is $\int_0^2 (3+t^2)\,dt = \left[3t+\frac{t^3}{3}\right]_0^2 = 6+\frac{8}{3} = \frac{26}{3}$ liters. The interval lasts $2$ minutes, so the average rate is $\frac{26/3}{2} = \frac{13}{3}$ liters per minute. Multiplying this average by $2$ recovers the total.

**Answer.** $\frac{26}{3}$ liters enter; the average rate is $\frac{13}{3}$ liters per minute.

</details>
