# A cyclic subgroup and its cosets

*Difficulty 2 of 5 · [Groups as symmetry and composition](index.md) · exercise 3 of 4*

In the additive group $\mathbb{Z}/12\mathbb{Z}$, list the subgroup generated by $8$, its order, all distinct cosets, and its index.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Keep adding 8 modulo 12 until returning to 0

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Cosets partition all 12 residues into equal-sized sets

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The multiples of $8$ modulo $12$ are $0,8,16\equiv4,24\equiv0$, so $H=\langle8\rangle=\{0,4,8\}$ and $|H|=3$. Its distinct cosets are $H=\{0,4,8\}$, $1+H=\{1,5,9\}$, $2+H=\{2,6,10\}$, and $3+H=\{3,7,11\}$. Further translates repeat one of these. There are four cosets, so the index is $[\mathbb{Z}/12\mathbb{Z}:H]=4$, consistent with $12/3=4$.

**Answer.** $H=\{0,4,8\}$, order $3$; four cosets $H$, $1+H$, $2+H$, $3+H$; index $4$.

</details>
