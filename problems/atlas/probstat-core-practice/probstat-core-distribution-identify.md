# Identify a simple probability distribution

*Difficulty 1 of 5 · [Probability and statistics core practice](index.md) · exercise 2 of 12*

A fair six-sided die is rolled once. Let $X$ be the outcome. Name the probability distribution of $X$, state its parameters, and compute $P(X \ge 4)$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A fair die has equally likely outcomes

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Count the favorable outcomes out of six

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The random variable $X$ follows a discrete uniform distribution on $\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$, often written as $\mathrm{Uniform}\{1,6\}$. Each outcome has probability $1/6$. The event $X \ge 4$ corresponds to outcomes $4$, $5$, and $6$, so $P(X \ge 4)=3/6=1/2$.

**Answer.** Discrete uniform on $\{1,\dots,6\}$; $P(X \ge 4)=1/2$.

</details>
