# Probability and statistics core practice

Solved exercises bridging probability models with statistical reasoning: distributions, estimation, testing, regression design, and common misconceptions.

**Objectives**

- Connect probability calculations to statistical conclusions
- Diagnose design and modeling assumptions
- Interpret estimates, intervals, and tests with appropriate caution
