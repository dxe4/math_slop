# Analysis and linear algebra core practice

Solved exercises across limits, multivariable and vector calculus, measure theory, complex analysis, and the spectral side of linear algebra, mixing exact computation with proof and counterexample work.

**Objectives**

- Carry out exact computations in calculus and linear algebra with full justification
- Prove convergence and continuity claims from definitions
- Distinguish theorem directions and construct counterexamples
- Use spectral and contour methods to replace iteration with closed forms
