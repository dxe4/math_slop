# Transferable proof strategies

Problems that reuse invariants, structural induction, pigeonhole reasoning, and witness construction across mathematical domains.

**Objectives**

- Recognize a proof pattern outside its first context
- Name the invariant or witness that carries the argument
- Check edge cases and quantify conclusions precisely
