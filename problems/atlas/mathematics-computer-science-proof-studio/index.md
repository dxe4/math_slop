# Mathematics for computer science proof and counterexample studio

Solved exercises connecting algorithm contracts, data-structure invariants, automata and languages, information and coding, cryptography, randomization, geometry and graphics, verification, cellular systems, and quantum computation.

**Objectives**

- Turn implementations and finite models into explicit contracts, invariants, variants, and checkable certificates
- Distinguish testing, exhaustive finite proof, randomized evidence, approximation, and deployment claims
- Construct exact counterexamples at empty, degenerate, numerical, adversarial, and model-boundary cases
- Compute small language, information, code, geometry, graphics, verification, and quantum examples with every hypothesis visible
