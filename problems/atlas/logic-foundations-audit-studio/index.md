# Logic and foundations audit studio

Solved exercises that keep object language, semantic models, proof calculi, axioms, metatheorems, computation, and constructive content in their proper layers.

**Objectives**

- Evaluate formulas with scopes, assignments, witnesses, and counterexamples made explicit
- Distinguish mathematical statements from metatheoretic claims about models and proofs
- State foundational theorems with the language, theory, consistency, and choice hypotheses they require
- Extract computational or witness content without overstating Curry–Howard or constructive scope
