# Describe a transformation sequence

*Difficulty 3 of 5 · [Algebra & Functions Core Practice](index.md) · exercise 7 of 12*

Starting from the parent parabola $y=x^2$, describe in words how to obtain the graph of $y=-2(x-1)^2+3$. State the vertex and whether the parabola opens upward or downward.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Work from the inside out: $x-1$ shifts horizontally.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The coefficient $-2$ reflects across the $x$-axis and stretches vertically.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Begin with $y=x^2$. The inside change $x-1$ shifts the graph right by $1$ unit. The coefficient $2$ stretches vertically by a factor of $2$, making the parabola narrower. The negative sign reflects the graph across the $x$-axis, so it now opens downward. Finally, $+3$ shifts the graph up by $3$ units. The vertex form $y=a(x-h)^2+k$ has vertex $(h,k)$, so the vertex is $(1,3)$. Because $a=-2<0$, the parabola opens downward.

**Answer.** Shift right $1$, stretch vertically by $2$, reflect across the $x$-axis, shift up $3$; vertex $(1,3)$; opens downward.

</details>
