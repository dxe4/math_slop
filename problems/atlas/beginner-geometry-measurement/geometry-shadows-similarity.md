# Measure a tree by its shadow

*Difficulty 2 of 5 · [Geometry through measurement](index.md) · exercise 3 of 4*

At the same moment, a 1.5 m person casts a 2 m shadow and a vertical tree casts a 12 m shadow. Assuming level ground, find the tree's height and name the geometric assumption.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Sun rays give the same acute angle in both triangles

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Set height/shadow equal for the two similar triangles

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The person's height and shadow form a right triangle, as do the tree and its shadow. Parallel sunlight gives them the same other acute angle, so the triangles are similar. Therefore height/shadow is constant: $1.5/2 = H/12$. Solving gives $H = 12 \times 1.5/2 = 9$ m. The conclusion depends on vertical objects, level ground, and effectively parallel sun rays.

**Answer.** $9$ m, using similarity of the two shadow triangles.

</details>
