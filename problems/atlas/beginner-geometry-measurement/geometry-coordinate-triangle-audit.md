# Audit a triangle using coordinates

*Difficulty 2 of 5 · [Geometry through measurement](index.md) · exercise 4 of 4*

The points $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, and $C=(4,3)$ form a triangle. Find its three side lengths, perimeter, and area, and explain how the coordinates reveal its type.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

$AB$ is horizontal and $BC$ is vertical

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use the distance formula for $AC$

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

$AB$ has length $4$ and $BC$ has length $3$. Because a horizontal segment is perpendicular to a vertical one, the angle at $B$ is right. The distance $AC$ is $\sqrt{(4-0)^2+(3-0)^2}=\sqrt{25}=5$. Thus the perimeter is $3+4+5=12$, and the area is one half times the perpendicular legs: $(1/2)\cdot4\cdot3=6$ square units. It is a $3$-$4$-$5$ right triangle.

**Answer.** Side lengths $3, 4, 5$; perimeter $12$; area $6$ square units; a right triangle.

</details>
