# Geometry through measurement

Accessible geometry problems combining area, right triangles, similarity, coordinates, and checks using units.

**Objectives**

- Decompose geometric measurements into known formulas
- Use right triangles and similarity to infer missing lengths
- Verify coordinate results with geometric structure

## Exercises

1. [Area of a border](geometry-garden-border-area.md) — difficulty 1 of 5
2. [A ladder and a right triangle](geometry-ladder-right-triangle.md) — difficulty 1 of 5
3. [Measure a tree by its shadow](geometry-shadows-similarity.md) — difficulty 2 of 5
4. [Audit a triangle using coordinates](geometry-coordinate-triangle-audit.md) — difficulty 2 of 5
