# Area of a border

*Difficulty 1 of 5 · [Geometry through measurement](index.md) · exercise 1 of 4*

A rectangular garden is 8 m by 5 m. A path 1 m wide is built all the way around the outside. What is the area of the path alone?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The outside rectangle gains 1 m on both ends of each dimension

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtract the garden area from the outside area

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The path adds $1$ m on the left and right, so the outside length is $10$ m. It likewise makes the outside width $7$ m. The total outside area is $10 \times 7 = 70$ m$^2$, while the garden area is $8 \times 5 = 40$ m$^2$. Subtracting gives a path area of $70 - 40 = 30$ m$^2$.

**Answer.** $30$ m$^2$.

</details>
