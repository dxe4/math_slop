# A ladder and a right triangle

*Difficulty 1 of 5 · [Geometry through measurement](index.md) · exercise 2 of 4*

A 13 m ladder leans against a vertical wall with its foot 5 m from the wall. Assuming level ground, how high up the wall does it reach?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The ladder is the hypotenuse

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use $h^2 + 5^2 = 13^2$

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The wall and level ground form a right angle, so the Pythagorean theorem gives $h^2 + 5^2 = 13^2$. Thus $h^2 = 169 - 25 = 144$. Height is nonnegative, so $h = \sqrt{144} = 12$ m. The negative square root is not a physical height in this setting.

**Answer.** $12$ m.

</details>
