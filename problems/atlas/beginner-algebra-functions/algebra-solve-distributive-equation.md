# Solve through the distributive law

*Difficulty 2 of 5 · [Algebra and functions from first principles](index.md) · exercise 2 of 4*

Solve $3(2x - 5) + 4 = 2(x + 7)$, and verify the result by substituting it into both sides.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Expand both sides first

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

After collecting terms, the equation is $4x = 25$

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Expanding gives $6x - 15 + 4 = 2x + 14$, hence $6x - 11 = 2x + 14$. Subtracting $2x$ and adding $11$ gives $4x = 25$, so $x = 25/4$. For verification, the left side is $3(25/2 - 5) + 4 = 3(15/2) + 4 = 53/2$. The right side is $2(25/4 + 7) = 2(53/4) = 53/2$, so the solution checks.

**Answer.** $x = 25/4$.

</details>
