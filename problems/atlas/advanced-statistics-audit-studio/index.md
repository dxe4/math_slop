# Advanced statistics design and dependence studio

Solved audits for unequal-probability samples, generalized responses, temporal and multivariate dependence, rank and smoothing procedures, censored event times, and spatial prediction.

**Objectives**

- Match estimators and uncertainty to the actual sampling, censoring, temporal, or spatial mechanism
- Interpret link, hazard, covariance, rank, and smoothing quantities on their correct scales
- Compute exact small examples while naming every identification and asymptotic boundary
- Separate descriptive, predictive, population-generalization, and causal claims
