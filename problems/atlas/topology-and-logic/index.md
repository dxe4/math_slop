# Logic, sets, and topology

Exercises moving from elementwise set logic and quantifiers to open sets, closed sets, and metric-space examples.

**Objectives**

- Translate between symbolic and elementwise arguments
- Negate nested quantifiers without changing their order incorrectly
- Test topological definitions on concrete spaces

## Exercises

1. [Prove a set identity elementwise](logic-elementwise-de-morgan.md) — difficulty 2 of 5
2. [Order nested quantifiers](logic-negate-student-problem.md) — difficulty 2 of 5
3. [A set that is neither open nor closed](topology-half-open-interval.md) — difficulty 3 of 5
4. [Open balls in the discrete metric](topology-discrete-metric-balls.md) — difficulty 3 of 5
