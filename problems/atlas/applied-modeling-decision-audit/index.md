# Applied modeling and decision audit studio

Solved derivations, certificates, computations, and claim-boundary audits across scale, inverse reasoning, networks, operations, biology, epidemics, finance, and signals.

**Objectives**

- Derive and certify model conclusions with units and hypotheses visible
- Separate sensitivity, identifiability, numerical stability, and validation
- Formulate operational decisions with feasibility and optimality certificates
- Keep prediction, causal effect, equilibrium, and financial valuation claims distinct
