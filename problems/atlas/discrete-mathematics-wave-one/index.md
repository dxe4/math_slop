# Discrete mathematics: structure, counting, extremal reasoning, and matroids

Fully reasoned exercises spanning the complete discrete-mathematics domain, including proofs, exact computation, counterexamples, interpretation, and synthesis.

**Objectives**

- State definitions and hypotheses before selecting a discrete proof method
- Move among bijections, recurrences, generating functions, and graph representations
- Construct and audit extremal, probabilistic, game, and matroid certificates
- Distinguish exact finite computation from unbounded mathematical proof
