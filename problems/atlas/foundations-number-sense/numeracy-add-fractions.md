# Combine two fractional amounts

*Difficulty 1 of 5 · [Number sense in everyday quantities](index.md) · exercise 2 of 4*

A recipe uses $3/4$ cup of oats and $2/3$ cup of nuts. How many cups is that altogether? Give both an improper fraction and a mixed number.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Use $12$ as a common denominator

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Convert $17/12$ into wholes and twelfths

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Rewrite $3/4$ as $9/12$ and $2/3$ as $8/12$. Their sum is $9/12 + 8/12 = 17/12$ cups. Since $17 = 12 + 5$, this is $1\frac{5}{12}$ cups. Adding numerators before finding a common denominator would be invalid because quarters and thirds are differently sized pieces.

**Answer.** $17/12$ cups, or $1\frac{5}{12}$ cups.

</details>
