# A journey with consistent units

*Difficulty 2 of 5 · [Number sense in everyday quantities](index.md) · exercise 4 of 4*

A cyclist travels 180 km in 2 hours 15 minutes at a constant average speed. Find the speed in km/h and the distance traveled in 50 minutes at that speed.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Convert $15$ minutes to $0.25$ hour

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Convert $50$ minutes to $5/6$ hour

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The total time is $2.25$ hours, so the average speed is $180/2.25 = 80$ km/h. Fifty minutes is $50/60 = 5/6$ hour. At the same speed the distance is $80 \times 5/6 = 200/3$ km, or $66\frac{2}{3}$ km. Keeping time in hours makes the units cancel correctly.

**Answer.** $80$ km/h, and $200/3$ km ($66\frac{2}{3}$ km) in $50$ minutes.

</details>
