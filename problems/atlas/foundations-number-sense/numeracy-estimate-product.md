# Estimate, calculate, and audit a product

*Difficulty 1 of 5 · [Number sense in everyday quantities](index.md) · exercise 1 of 4*

Estimate $398 \times 51$ using nearby round numbers, then calculate the exact product and find the percentage by which your estimate differs from the exact answer.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Round $398$ to $400$ and $51$ to $50$

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use $398 \times (50 + 1)$ for the exact calculation

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 3</summary>

Percentage difference is absolute difference divided by the exact value

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The rounded calculation is $400 \times 50 = 20{,}000$. Exactly, $398 \times 51 = 398 \times 50 + 398 = 19{,}900 + 398 = 20{,}298$. The estimate is low by $298$. Its percentage difference from the exact value is $\frac{298}{20{,}298} \times 100\%$, which is approximately $1.47\%$. The small relative difference confirms that $20{,}000$ was a sensible estimate.

**Answer.** Estimate $20{,}000$; exact product $20{,}298$; the estimate is about $1.47\%$ low.

</details>
