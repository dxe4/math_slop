# Design an ethical improvement dossier

*Difficulty 5 of 5 · [Mathematics education evidence and design studio](index.md) · exercise 12 of 12*

A department wants to record a research lesson, use an AI transcript service, compare pre/post exit tickets, and publish selected learner quotations as evidence that its new proof-discussion routine works. Produce a compact redesign covering mathematics, data governance, evidence, analysis, revision, and the maximum warranted claim.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Separate instructional improvement from personnel evaluation and research/publication authority

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

A pre/post change without a counterfactual does not isolate the routine

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

First bound the mathematical target, such as coordinating example, counterexample, and quantifier in one proof explanation, and predict observable strategies. Establish institutional review, consent or assent as applicable, a noncoercive participation route, observer roles, and separation from employment or grades. Minimize recording; document what the AI vendor receives, retention and deletion, access, accuracy checks, cross-border or account constraints, and a no-upload alternative. Collect timestamped low-inference observations and independent artifacts with an explicit sample and missingness log. Score pre/post tickets with a versioned rubric and report paired changes and uncertainty, but do not attribute change causally without a defensible counterfactual. Use the lesson discussion to compare predictions, negative cases, and implementation, then revise one conjectured mechanism and document changed conditions. Publish quotations only within consent and deductive-identification protections. The maximum initial claim is local: what strategy patterns and score changes were observed under this implementation, how the design was revised, and what remains unknown. It cannot establish that the routine works generally.

**Answer.** Bound the proof construct, obtain proper authority and consent, minimize and govern vendor data, collect traceable mixed evidence, report descriptive change, revise one mechanism, and make only a local observed-pattern claim.

</details>
