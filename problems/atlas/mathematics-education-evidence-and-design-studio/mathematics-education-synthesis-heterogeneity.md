# Synthesize without manufacturing one effect

*Difficulty 4 of 5 · [Mathematics education evidence and design studio](index.md) · exercise 10 of 12*

A review finds five reports labeled `technology-supported fractions instruction`. Two reports contain different outcomes from the same randomized trial; one report is a single-case design, one compares historical cohorts, and one randomized schools. The reviewer takes the unweighted mean of all reported standardized effects and calls it `the effect of technology`. Diagnose the synthesis and give a defensible redesign.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Reports, studies, and effect estimates are different units, and estimates from one sample are dependent

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Standardizing a score does not make interventions, comparisons, constructs, or designs share one estimand

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The calculation treats dependent estimates from one trial as independent, gives precise and imprecise estimates equal weight, mixes designs with different warrant, and assumes that unlike interventions, comparisons, outcomes, populations, and timings estimate one generic technology effect. A defensible review begins with a protocol-defined question and eligibility criteria, reconstructs links among studies and reports, documents searches and exclusions, codes intervention and mathematical constructs, assesses design-specific bias, and records uncertainty. It selects comparable estimates and handles within-study dependence under a stated meta-analytic model only where a common target is defensible. Otherwise it gives a structured narrative synthesis preserving magnitude, precision, context, contradictions, heterogeneity, and missing/selectively reported evidence. Any pooled result must retain its narrower estimand and transport limits.

**Answer.** Do not average the five reports. Reconstruct study units, define comparable estimands, model dependence and uncertainty, examine heterogeneity and selective reporting, and use narrative synthesis where pooling is unjustified.

</details>
