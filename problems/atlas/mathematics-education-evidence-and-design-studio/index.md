# Mathematics education evidence and design studio

Solved cases that require a learner to distinguish observation from interpretation, affordance from efficacy, estimate from causal claim, and local improvement from universal prescription.

**Objectives**

- Design low-stakes mathematical probes that isolate a relation without labeling a learner
- Trace a technology from mathematical target through action, feedback, access, and independent checking
- Match descriptive, interpretive, causal, and improvement claims to designs that can warrant them
- Use uncertainty, ethics, and explicit excluded claims in research and lesson-study reports
