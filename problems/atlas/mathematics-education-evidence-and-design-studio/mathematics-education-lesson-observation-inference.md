# Rewrite evaluation as evidence

*Difficulty 4 of 5 · [Mathematics education evidence and design studio](index.md) · exercise 11 of 12*

A lesson-study observer writes, `The teacher confused the class, and most students did not understand fractions.` Rewrite this as an observation record and a separately labeled inference. Then give one follow-up evidence source.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Use timestamped actions or exact words instead of judgments

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Do not infer `most` unless the observation plan sampled the class accordingly

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A defensible record might be: `At 10:18, after the unequal-whole diagram was displayed, three of the four pairs assigned to this observer compared numerators; two said that 5/8 was larger because 5 is larger than 3. The fourth pair's discussion was inaudible.` A separately labeled inference is: `The unequal-whole display or preceding prompt may not yet have foregrounded the common-whole condition for these recorded pairs.` This avoids evaluating the teacher and does not generalize to unobserved learners. A follow-up source is each learner's independent exit explanation or artifact, linked to the observation zone and collected before the lesson summary.

**Answer.** Record sampled, timestamped learner actions and exact words; label the possible design mechanism as an inference and check it against independent artifacts.

</details>
