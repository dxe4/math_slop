# Data and mathematical learning audit studio

Solved calculations that separate data provenance, empirical fitting, differentiation, probabilistic conditioning, geometry, sequential control, and transport from claims their hypotheses do not support.

**Objectives**

- Compute learning quantities with dimensions, marginal constraints, and train-only transformations checked
- Distinguish optimization output and fixed-sample evidence from population, causal, or control guarantees
- Diagnose singularity, dependence, leakage, inadequate sampling, and exploration assumptions
