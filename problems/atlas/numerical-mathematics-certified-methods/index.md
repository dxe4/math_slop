# Numerical mathematics and certified optimization

Solved exercises in finite arithmetic, approximation, linear and differential solvers, continuous optimization, automatic differentiation, and bounded integer certificates.

**Objectives**

- Separate conditioning, approximation, rounding, and implementation error
- Derive convergence or stability evidence under explicit hypotheses
- Use residuals, dual bounds, relaxations, and finite search as certificates
- Diagnose boundary cases rather than reporting unsupported decimals
