# Operations across orders of magnitude

*Difficulty 2 of 5 · [Powers, roots, and scale solved studio](index.md) · exercise 7 of 12*

Let `A=6.0*10^7`, `B=2.4*10^-5`, and `C=7.5*10^6`. Compute `AB` and `A+C` in normalized scientific notation. Then state approximately how many orders of magnitude separate `A` and `B` by examining their ratio.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Multiply coefficients and add exponents for a product

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Align powers of ten before adding

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

For the product, `AB=(6.0*2.4)*10^(7-5)=14.4*10^2=1.44*10^3`. For the sum, rewrite `C=0.75*10^7`; then `A+C=(6.0+0.75)*10^7=6.75*10^7`. For the scale comparison, `A/B=(6.0/2.4)*10^(7-(-5))=2.5*10^12`. Thus A is about twelve orders of magnitude larger than B. The coefficient 2.5 refines the ratio, while the exponent difference captures its scale.

**Answer.** `AB=1.44*10^3`, `A+C=6.75*10^7`, and `A/B=2.5*10^12`, about twelve orders of magnitude.

</details>
