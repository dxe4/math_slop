# Powers, roots, and scale solved studio

Short, fully reasoned exercises that expose the domain checks and scale interpretations hidden by compact exponent notation.

**Objectives**

- Identify the complete base before evaluating a power
- Use integer and rational exponent laws only where the expressions are defined
- Distinguish a principal root from all solutions of a power equation
- Compute and audit quantities across powers of ten and geometric scales
