# Abstract algebra through structure-preserving maps

Proof-focused exercises joining quotient groups and rings, finite fields, module exactness, localization, derived functors, and naturality.

**Objectives**

- Verify that quotient and localization constructions are well defined
- Compute structural invariants from exact sequences and resolutions
- State the hypotheses that make field, module, and categorical arguments valid
