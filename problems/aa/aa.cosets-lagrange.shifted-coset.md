# List a shifted additive coset

*Easy · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

In $\mathbb Z/12\mathbb Z$, list the coset $5+\langle4\rangle$ using representatives from $0$ to $11$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A coset is a translate of the entire subgroup.

1. The subgroup generated by $4$ is $\{0,4,8\}$.
2. Adding $5$ gives residues $5,9,13$, with $13\equiv1\pmod{12}$.
3. The coset is $\{1,5,9\}$.

</details>
