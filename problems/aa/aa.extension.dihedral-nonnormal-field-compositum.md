# Use two reflection subgroups rather than cyclic degree arithmetic

*Medium · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

Let $L/K$ be Galois with $G=\langle r,s\mid r^4=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle$ of order $8$. Put $E=L^{\langle s\rangle}$ and $F=L^{\langle rs\rangle}$. Return $[[E\cap F:K],[EF:K]]$ and explain the subgroup operations.

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"vector","expected":["1","8"],"checker_scope":"Only the requested exact invariant is checked; no written proof is automatically assessed."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Intersection of fields corresponds to the subgroup generated by both fixing groups.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Compositum corresponds to intersection of fixing groups, even when the subgroups are not normal.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Generated and intersected subgroups reverse the field operations.

1. The elements $s$ and $rs$ generate $r=(rs)s$ and hence all of $G$. Thus $E\cap F=L^G=K$.
2. The distinct order-two subgroups $\langle s\rangle$ and $\langle rs\rangle$ have trivial intersection.
3. Therefore $EF=L$, of degree $8$ over $K$. The requested pair is $[1,8]$.

</details>
