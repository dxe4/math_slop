# Intersect two cyclic subgroups

*Medium · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

In the additive group $\mathbb Z/24\mathbb Z$, find $\langle4\rangle\cap\langle6\rangle$, using canonical residue representatives.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Membership must hold in both generated subgroups simultaneously.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

List the multiples of each generator within one complete residue range.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Intersect the complete finite subgroup rosters.

1. $\langle4\rangle=\{0,4,8,12,16,20\}$ and $\langle6\rangle=\{0,6,12,18\}$.
2. The only canonical residues appearing in both lists are $0$ and $12$.
3. Thus the intersection is $\{0,12\}$. It is itself the subgroup generated by $12$.

</details>
