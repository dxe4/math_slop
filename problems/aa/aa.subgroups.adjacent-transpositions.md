# Generate a symmetric group

*Medium · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

What is the order of the subgroup of $S_3$ generated by $(1\ 2)$ and $(2\ 3)$? Permutations compose with the rightmost map first.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Products of the generators produce cycles as well as the original transpositions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Account for each possible cycle type in this small symmetric group.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Two adjacent transpositions generate every permutation on three letters.

1. Let $s=(1\ 2)$ and $t=(2\ 3)$. Their products give $st=(1\ 2\ 3)$ and $ts=(1\ 3\ 2)$.
2. The conjugate $sts=(1\ 3)$ also lies in the subgroup, along with $e,s,t$.
3. These are six distinct permutations, exhausting $S_3$, so the subgroup has order $6$.

</details>
