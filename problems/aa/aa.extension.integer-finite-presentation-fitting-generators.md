# Use two different sizes of presentation minors

*Easy · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

A Z-module has presentation matrix diag(2,6). Its zeroth Fitting ideal is generated by the two-by-two minors and its first by the entries. Enter the positive generators of $Fitt_0$ and Fitt\_1 in that order.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The first ideal uses the full determinant.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The second ideal uses the gcd of all one-by-one minors, including zero entries.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Different Fitting indices use different minor sizes.

1. The only full-size minor is determinant2\*6=12, so Fitt\_0=(12).
2. The entries generate(2,6)=(2), with zero entries contributing nothing.
3. Thus the ordered generators are\[12,2\].

</details>
