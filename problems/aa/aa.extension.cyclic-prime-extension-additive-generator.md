# Recover an Artin–Schreier generator from a trace-one element

*Hard · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

Let $L/K$ be a cyclic Galois extension of prime degree $p$ in characteristic $p$, generated by $\sigma$. You may use that the trace map of a finite separable extension is nonzero. Construct $u\in L$ with $\sigma(u)=u+1$ and deduce $L=K(u)$ with $u^p-u\in K$. Prove conversely that an irreducible $X^p-X-a\in K[X]$ defines a cyclic Galois extension.

<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The difference under $sigma$ of $sum isigma^i(b)$ is minus the trace; use the orbit of the resulting element.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Model proof</summary>

Trace weights turn the cyclic action into translation.

1. Choose $b$ of trace $1$. Let $u=-\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}i\sigma^i(b)$, with coefficients in the prime field. Reindexing gives $\sigma(u)-u=\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}\sigma^i(b)=1$.
2. The orbit of $u$ consists of the distinct $u+j$ for $j\in\mathbf F_p$, so $[K(u):K]=p$ and $L=K(u)$. Also $\sigma(u^p-u)=u^p-u$, hence this element lies in $K$.
3. Conversely an irreducible $X^p-X-a$ has distinct roots $u+j$ all in $K(u)$ because its derivative is $-1$. Its splitting field thus has degree $p$, and translation by $1$ generates its cyclic Galois group.

**Self-assessment rubric**

- Construct a trace-one element and check the weighted conjugate difference.
- Prove the translation orbit has degree p and its additive polynomial parameter is fixed.
- Prove the converse polynomial splits separably with a cyclic translation group.

</details>
