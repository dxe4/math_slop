# Separate content from polynomial degree

*Easy · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

The content of a nonzero integer polynomial is the positive gcd of its coefficients. Find the content of $3x^2+6x+9$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Content isolates the scalar arithmetic of an integer polynomial.

1. The coefficients are3,6,9.
2. Their greatest common divisor is three.
3. Thus the positive content is three.

</details>
