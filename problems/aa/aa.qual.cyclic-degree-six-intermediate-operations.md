# Translate subgroup intersections and generated subgroups into field operations

*Medium · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

Let L/K be a cyclic Galois extension of degree $6$, generated by sigma. Put E=L^(&lt;sigma^2&gt;) and F=L^(&lt;sigma^3&gt;). Return the vector (\[E:K\],\[F:K\],\[E intersection F:K\],\[EF:K\]).

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Fixed-field degrees are subgroup indices.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Intersection of fixed fields corresponds to the generated subgroup, while compositum corresponds to subgroup intersection.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Order-reversing Galois correspondence distinguishes the two field operations.

1. The subgroups generated by sigma squared and sigma cubed have orders three and two, giving fixed-field degrees two and three.
2. Together they generate the full cyclic group because two and three are coprime, so E intersection F is K and has degree one.
3. Their subgroup intersection is trivial, so the compositum is L with degree six. The requested vector is (2,3,1,6).

</details>
