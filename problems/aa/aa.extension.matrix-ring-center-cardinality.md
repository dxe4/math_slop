# Use the scalar-center description of a full matrix ring

*Easy · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

For a field F, the center of $M_2(F)$ consists of scalar matrices. Find the number of central elements of M2(F2).

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":2,"checker_scope":"Exact requested finite ring invariant only; no radical or classification proof is automatically assessed."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Each central matrix is determined by one field scalar.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The zero scalar is included.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The full matrix ring need not be commutative even though its center is a field copy.

1. A scalar matrix has form a\*I.
2. There are exactly two choices a=0,1 in F2.
3. Thus the center has two elements.

</details>
