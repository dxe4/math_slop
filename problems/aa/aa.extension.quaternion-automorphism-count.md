# Count permissible generator images with all quaternion relations

*Medium · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

For $Q_8=\{\pm1,\pm i,\pm j,\pm k\}$ with i^2=j^2=k^2=-1 and ij=k=-ji, find the number of group automorphisms.

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":24,"checker_scope":"Exact requested finite structural quantity only; no group-theoretic proof is automatically assessed."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

An automorphism sends i to one of the six elements of order four.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The image of j must lie outside the cyclic subgroup generated by the image of i.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Independent quaternion directions determine an automorphism exactly.

1. The image of i may be any of the six order-four elements. Its cyclic subgroup has four elements, leaving four possible images of j outside it.
2. Any such pair squares to -1 and anticommutes, hence satisfies the defining relations and generates Q8. It therefore defines a surjective endomorphism, which is an automorphism of this finite group.
3. Different ordered image pairs give different maps, so the count is 6\*4=24.

</details>
