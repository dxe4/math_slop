# Use the order of a Frobenius subgroup to recover its fixed field

*Medium · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

Let sigma be the Frobenius x-&gt;x^3 on $L=\mathbb F_{3^{12}}$. Find the number of elements fixed by the subgroup generated by sigma^8.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A subgroup generated by a Frobenius power is controlled by a gcd, not by that exponent alone.

1. The full cyclic Galois group has order twelve. The element sigma^8 has order 12/gcd(12,8)=3.
2. Its fixed field therefore has degree twelve divided by three, or four, over F3.
3. The fixed field has 3^4=81 elements.

</details>
