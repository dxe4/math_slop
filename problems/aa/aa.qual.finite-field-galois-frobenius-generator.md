# Prove the full finite-field Galois group is cyclic

*Hard · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

For a prime power q and integer d&gt;=1, prove $\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb F_{q^d}/\mathbb F_q)$ is cyclic of order d, generated by x-&gt;x^q. Verify the order rather than only that the dth power is the identity.

<summary>Hint 1</summary>

All elements of the larger field are distinct roots of X^(q^d)-X.

If a smaller positive Frobenius power fixed the whole field, compare the number of roots with the polynomial degree.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Model proof</summary>

A splitting polynomial proves Galoisness and a root-count bound makes the Frobenius order exact.

1. The polynomial X^(q^d)-X splits into all field elements and has derivative negative one, so the extension is normal and separable of degree d.
2. The q-power map fixes Fq and its dth power is the identity. If its rth power were the identity for 0&lt;r&lt;d, every one of the q^d elements would solve X^(q^r)-X, impossible for a polynomial of degree q^r.
3. Thus the Frobenius has order d. A degree-d extension has at most d base-field automorphisms, so its d Frobenius powers exhaust the Galois group.

**Self-assessment rubric**

- Use the splitting polynomial and its nonzero derivative to establish the field-extension hypotheses.
- Exclude every smaller positive Frobenius order using a root-count argument.
- Compare with the extension-degree automorphism bound and conclude cyclicity.

</details>
