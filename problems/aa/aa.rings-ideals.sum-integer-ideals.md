# Add two principal ideals of the integers

*Easy · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

Find the positive generator of the ideal $(12)+(18)$ in $\mathbb Z$.

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":6,"checker_scope":"Exact stated finite group, polynomial or field answer only; optional personal reasoning is not graded."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Every member is an integer linear combination of the two given generators.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use the greatest common divisor and a Bezout representation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The ideal generated by two integers is generated by their gcd.

1. Every integer combination of $12$ and $18$ is divisible by their gcd $6$.
2. The integer $6=18-12$ is itself in the sum ideal, so all multiples of $6$ occur.
3. The positive generator is $6$.

</details>
