# Identify a finite abelian lattice quotient through Smith invariants

*Medium · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

Let H be generated by $(4,2)$ and (2,4) in the additive group Z^2. Find the ordered nontrivial invariant factors of Z^2/H as a vector.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A rank-two integer presentation determines its finite abelian quotient.

1. The presentation matrix with the two generators as columns has determinant twelve and entry gcd two.
2. For a full-rank two-by-two matrix the Smith factors satisfy d1=gcd(entries) and d1 d2=absolute determinant, so d1=2 and d2=6.
3. They satisfy divisibility 2 divides 6, giving quotient C2 times C6 and vector (2,6).

</details>
