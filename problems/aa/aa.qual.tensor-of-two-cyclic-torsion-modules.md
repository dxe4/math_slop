<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A bilinear witness proves the exact order rather than only an annihilation bound.

1. The tensor generator z=1 tensor 1 is killed by twelve and eighteen, hence by their gcd six. All elementary tensors are integer multiples of z, so the product has at most six elements.
2. The map from residue pairs to their ordinary product modulo six is well-defined and Z-bilinear, since six divides both moduli.
3. By the tensor universal property it gives a homomorphism onto Z/6 sending z to one. Thus z has order at least six and the tensor has exactly six elements.

</details>
