# Count conjugates of a transposition subgroup

*Medium · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

How many distinct conjugate subgroups does $\langle(1\ 2)\rangle$ have inside $S_3$?

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":3,"checker_scope":"Exact stated finite group, polynomial or field answer only; optional personal reasoning is not graded."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Conjugation relabels a transposition.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Each subgroup generated by a transposition is determined by its sole nonidentity element.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Count the possible relabelled transpositions and distinguish their generated subgroups.

1. Conjugating $(1\ 2)$ by a permutation gives the transposition of the two corresponding images.
2. The possible conjugate transpositions are $(1\ 2)$, $(1\ 3)$ and $(2\ 3)$.
3. Their two-element generated subgroups are different, giving exactly $3$ conjugate subgroups. In particular the original subgroup is not normal.

</details>
