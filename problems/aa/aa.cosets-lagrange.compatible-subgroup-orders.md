# Find the smallest possible ambient group

*Medium · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

Find the smallest possible order of a finite group having a subgroup of order $6$ and a subgroup of order $10$. The subgroups may be different.

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":30,"checker_scope":"Exact stated finite group or ring answer only; optional personal reasoning is not graded."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Both subgroup orders must divide the ambient group order.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

A divisibility lower bound must be attained by an actual group before it is the answer.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use Lagrange for a lower bound and a cyclic group for attainment.

1. By Lagrange, the group order is divisible by both $6$ and $10$, hence by their least common multiple $30$.
2. The cyclic group of order $30$ has subgroups of orders $6$ and $10$, generated by the residues $5$ and $3$ respectively.
3. Thus the lower bound is achieved, and the smallest possible order is $30$.

</details>
