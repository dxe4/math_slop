# Prove the module structure of homomorphisms between cyclic torsion modules

*Medium · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

For positive integers m,n, prove $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(\mathbb Z/m,\mathbb Z/n)$ is a cyclic group of order gcd(m,n). Identify it through the image of one.

Its allowed image is the kernel of multiplication by m in Z/n.

<details>
<summary>Model proof</summary>

A generator-image condition identifies the entire Hom group, including its addition law.

1. A homomorphism is determined by y=phi(1), and it is well-defined exactly when my=0 in Z/n. Evaluation is additive and bijects Hom with this kernel subgroup.
2. Writing d=gcd(m,n), the congruence my=0 mod n is equivalent to n/d dividing an integer representative of y.
3. The allowed residues are multiples of n/d, forming the cyclic subgroup generated by that residue, of order d. Thus evaluation gives the claimed group isomorphism.
