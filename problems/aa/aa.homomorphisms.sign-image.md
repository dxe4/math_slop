# Find the image of the sign homomorphism

*Easy · [Abstract algebra problems](index.md)*

Find the image of the sign homomorphism $\operatorname{sgn}:S_3\to\{1,-1\}$, where the codomain uses multiplication.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

To establish an image, show that every listed value is attained.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Compare the identity permutation with a transposition.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An even permutation and an odd permutation realize the two possible signs.

1. The identity has sign $1$.
2. A transposition such as $(1\ 2)$ has sign $-1$.
3. Both codomain values occur, so the image is $\{-1,1\}$.

</details>
