# Find an omitted angle around a point

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Nonoverlapping angles around a point fill one full turn. All but one have total measure $200^\circ$. Find the remaining angle in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Account for all sectors of one turn.

1. The complete collection has measure $360^\circ$.
2. The unknown sector therefore satisfies $x+200=360$.
3. Hence $x=160^\circ$; it is not the reflex sector of $200^\circ$.

</details>
