# Identify a point fixed by a reflection

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Reflection in line $y=2x$ fixes which point? Options: p: $(1,1)$; q: $(2,4)$; r: $(0,1)$; s: $(-1,3)$. Enter p, q, r or s.

**Options**

1. (1,1)
2. (2,4)
3. (0,1)
4. (-1,3)

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A reflection fixes every point on its mirror line and no others.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Check the equation $y=2x$ for the four points.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the fixed-point locus of a reflection.

1. The point $(2,4)$ satisfies $4=2\cdot2$.
2. The other ordinates differ from twice their abscissae.
3. Only q lies on the mirror and is therefore fixed.

</details>
