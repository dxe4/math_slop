# Recover a square area from its diagonal

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A square has diagonal $10$. Use $A=d^2/2$ to find its area.

<details>

The requested area formula is supplied directly in terms of the diagonal.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Apply the diagonal form of the square-area formula.

1. The two equal square sides satisfy $2s^2=d^2$ by Pythagoras.
2. Thus $A=s^2=10^2/2$.
3. The area is $50$.

</details>
