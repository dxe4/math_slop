# Complete a right angle

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A ray inside a right angle makes $37^\circ$ with one boundary ray. Find its angle with the other boundary ray, in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The two parts partition $90^\circ$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The unknown is the amount left after removing $37$ from $90$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the right-angle partition.

1. Because the ray is inside, the two adjacent angles add to $90^\circ$.
2. Thus $37+x=90$, so $x=53$.
3. The resulting acute angle fits inside the given right angle.

</details>
