# Use the extended sine relation

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Side $a=10$ is opposite angle $A$ of a triangle, with $\sin A=5/6$. Using $a=2R\sin A$, find its circumradius $R$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The side and angle are an opposite pair.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Divide $10$ by twice the given sine.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Isolate the radius in the supplied identity.

1. The identity gives $10=2R(5/6)$.
2. Thus $10=(5/3)R$.
3. Multiplying by $3/5$ gives $R=6$.

</details>
