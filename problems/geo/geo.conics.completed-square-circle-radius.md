# Complete squares in a circle equation

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Standard circle form isolates the positive squared radius.

1. Rearrange to $x^2-6x+y^2+4y=12$.
2. Complete squares to obtain $(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=25$.
3. The positive radius is $\sqrt{25}=5$.

</details>
