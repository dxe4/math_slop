# Measure a sphere surface

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A sphere has radius $6$. Its surface area is $q\pi$. Using $A=4\pi r^2$, find $q$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The supplied formula concerns area, not volume.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Apply the sphere area formula.

1. The radius square is $36$.
2. The area is $4\pi\cdot36=144\pi$.
3. Therefore $q=144$.

</details>
