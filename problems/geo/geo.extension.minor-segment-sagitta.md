# Measure a minor segment sagitta

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A circle has radius $10$ cm. A chord lies at perpendicular distance $6$ cm from the center. Along that perpendicular, find the distance from the chord to the nearer circular boundary, in centimetres. This is the minor segment sagitta.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The chord foot and nearer boundary lie on the same center ray.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The radius is split into the center-to-chord distance and the requested sagitta.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Subtract collinear distances on the radius.

1. Let $M$ be the perpendicular chord foot and $T$ the nearer intersection of ray $OM$ with the circle.
2. The points have order $O,M,T$ because $0<OM=6<10=OT$.
3. The sagitta is $MT=OT-OM=10-6=4$ cm.

</details>
