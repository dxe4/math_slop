# Scale two perpendicular directions differently

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A planar region of area $5$ is mapped by $(x,y)\mapsto(2x,3y)$. Use the area factor $|2\cdot3|$ to find the image area.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The two directions have different length factors.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The supplied area factor multiplies them, rather than squaring either one alone.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the supplied product of independent scales.

1. The area factor is $|2\cdot3|=6$.
2. It applies to the whole region because the map is linear.
3. The image area is $6\cdot5=30$.

</details>
