# Read a parabola directrix

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

The parabola $y^2=4px$ has directrix $x=-p$. For $y^2=12x$, find the constant $d$ in its directrix equation $x=d$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Match the coefficient $4p$ with $12$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The directrix uses the negative of $p$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the supplied standard-form relation.

1. The coefficient comparison gives $4p=12$, so $p=3$.
2. The given directrix formula is $x=-p$.
3. Thus $d=-3$.
