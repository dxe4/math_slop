# Read the orientation effect of one reflection

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A triangle has signed area $9$ with counterclockwise orientation positive. Reflect it in any line. Using the reflection signed-area factor $-1$, find its image signed area.

Apply the orientation-reversing area factor.

1. Reflection preserves ordinary area but reverses boundary orientation.
2. The signed-area multiplier is $-1$.
3. The image signed area is $-9$.
