# Find a perpendicular line slope

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A nonvertical line has slope $2$. Find the slope of any line perpendicular to it. Enter a rational number.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The product of finite slopes of perpendicular lines is $-1$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Solve $2m=-1$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the negative reciprocal slope.

1. The given slope is finite and nonzero, so the perpendicular slope is also finite.
2. Perpendicularity gives $2m=-1$.
3. Thus $m=-1/2$.

</details>
