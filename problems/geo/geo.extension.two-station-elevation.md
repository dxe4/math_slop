# Combine two exact elevation ratios

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A vertical pole stands on level ground. Two observation points lie on the same straight ground ray from its base, the farther point $6$ m beyond the nearer. The elevation tangents of the pole top are $1/2$ at the nearer point and $1/3$ at the farther point. Find the pole height in metres.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

If the nearer distance is $x>0$ and height is $h$, write $h/x=1/2$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The second distance is $x+6$, giving a second equation for the same height.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use both right triangles to remove the unknown ground distance.

1. The nearer observation gives $h=x/2$.
2. The farther gives $h=(x+6)/3$. Equating yields $3x=2x+12$, so $x=12$.
3. Thus $h=6$ m; both positive distances satisfy the stated observation geometry.

</details>
