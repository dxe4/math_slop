# Measure internally tangent center separation

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A circle of radius $4$ lies inside a circle of radius $10$ and is internally tangent to it. Find the distance between their centers.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The contact point lies on the same ray from both centers.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The larger radius equals the center distance plus the smaller radius.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the internal tangency difference.

1. The centers and contact point are collinear, with the smaller center between the larger center and contact point.
2. Thus $10=d+4$.
3. The center separation is $d=6$.

</details>
