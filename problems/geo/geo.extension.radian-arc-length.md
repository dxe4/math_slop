# Use a radian arc measure

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A circular arc has radius $12$ cm and central angle $5/6$ radians. Using $s=r\theta$ for angles in radians, find its length in centimetres.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Apply the radian definition of arc length.

1. Here $r=12$ and $\theta=5/6$.
2. The formula gives $s=12(5/6)=10$.
3. The angle is dimensionless, so the resulting unit is centimetres.
