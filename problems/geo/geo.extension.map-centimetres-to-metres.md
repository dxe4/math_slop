# Interpret a map scale

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

On a map, $1$ cm represents $250$ m on the ground. A straight mapped segment is $3$ cm long. Find its ground length in metres.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The represented length is proportional to map length.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Three centimetres correspond to three copies of $250$ metres.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Apply the stated linear scale.

1. The scale is $250$ ground metres per map centimetre.
2. The mapped length contains three units of one centimetre.
3. The ground length is $3\cdot250=750$ m.

</details>
