# Use vertically opposite angles

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Two distinct straight lines meet. One angle at their intersection is $146^\circ$. Find its vertically opposite angle in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Each given or opposite angle forms a straight angle with the same adjacent angle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Write the two sums equal to $180^\circ$ and subtract.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Compare two supplements of the same angle.

1. Let an adjacent angle be $t$. Then $146+t=180$.
2. If the vertically opposite angle is $x$, its straight-angle relation is $x+t=180$.
3. Subtracting these equations gives $x=146$.

</details>
