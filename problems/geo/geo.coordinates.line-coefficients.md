<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Two distinct x-coordinates determine a unique slope-intercept line.

1. The slope is $(7-1)/(5-2)=2$.
2. Using $(2,1)$ gives $1=2\cdot2+b$, so $b=-3$.
3. The required pair is $(2,-3)$.
