# Compensate a base change by its altitude

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A triangle is replaced by a triangle of the same area whose base is three times the original base. If the original perpendicular altitude is $12$, find the new perpendicular altitude.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Equal triangle areas mean equal base-altitude products.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Tripling one factor requires dividing the other by three.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use inverse proportionality for a fixed area.

1. Write the original positive base as $b$. Its area is $b\cdot12/2$.
2. For new height $h$, equality gives $3bh/2=12b/2$.
3. Cancel the positive base to obtain $3h=12$, so $h=4$.

</details>
