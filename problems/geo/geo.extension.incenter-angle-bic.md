# Find an angle at the incenter

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$I$ is the incenter of triangle $ABC$, and $\angle A=50^\circ$. Use $\angle BIC=90^\circ+\angle A/2$ to find $\angle BIC$ in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Substitute in the incenter identity.

1. The angles at $B,C$ in triangle $BIC$ are $B/2,C/2$.
2. Hence its third angle is $180-(B+C)/2=90+A/2$.
3. This is $90+25=115^\circ$.

</details>
