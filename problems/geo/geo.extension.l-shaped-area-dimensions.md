# Decompose an L-shaped region

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

From a rectangle of width $8$ and height $7$, remove the upper-right rectangular corner of width $3$ and height $4$. Find the area of the remaining L-shaped region.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The missing corner is wholly inside the outer rectangle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Compute the two rectangle areas before subtracting.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Subtract one geometrically specified rectangle from another.

1. The outer rectangle has area $8\cdot7=56$.
2. The removed corner has area $3\cdot4=12$.
3. The remainder has area $56-12=44$.

</details>
