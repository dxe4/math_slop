# Keep the two radii in a sector boundary

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A sector has radius $6$ and angle $120^\circ$. Write its entire boundary length as $a+b\pi$ and enter $[a,b]$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The boundary includes the circular arc and two radii.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The arc is $120/360$ of circumference $2\pi r$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Add straight and circular boundary contributions.

1. The two radii contribute $2\cdot6=12$.
2. The arc length is $(120/360)\cdot12\pi=4\pi$.
3. Thus the total is $12+4\pi$, giving $[12,4]$.

</details>
