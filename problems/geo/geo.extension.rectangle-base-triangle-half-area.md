# Move a triangle vertex along an opposite side

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A rectangle has area $50$. A triangle uses one complete side of the rectangle as its base and has its third vertex anywhere on the opposite side. Find the triangle area.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The triangle altitude equals the rectangle height for every allowed vertex.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use half the product that gives the rectangle area.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Compare base and altitude with the containing rectangle.

1. Write the rectangle dimensions as $b,h$, so $bh=50$.
2. The triangle has base $b$ and perpendicular height $h$, regardless of the position along the opposite side.
3. Its area is $bh/2=25$.

</details>
