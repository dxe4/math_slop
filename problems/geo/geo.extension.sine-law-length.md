# Use an opposite-side sine ratio

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

In a triangle, side $a=6$ is opposite angle $A$ and side $b$ is opposite angle $B$. Given $\sin A=1/2$, $\sin B=3/4$, use $a/\sin A=b/\sin B$ to find $b$.

<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the supplied sine-law proportion.

1. The common ratio is $6/(1/2)=12$.
2. Therefore $b=12\sin B=12(3/4)$.
3. Thus $b=9$.
