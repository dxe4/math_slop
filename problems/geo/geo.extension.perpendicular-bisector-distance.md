# Read an equidistance condition

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$A\ne B$, and $P$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of segment $AB$. If $PA=9$ cm, find $PB$ in centimetres.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Let $M$ be the midpoint of $AB$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The right triangles with common leg $PM$ have equal other legs $MA=MB$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A perpendicular bisector consists of equidistant points.

1. The midpoint satisfies $MA=MB$ and $PM$ is perpendicular to $AB$.
2. Pythagoras gives $PA^2=PM^2+MA^2=PM^2+MB^2=PB^2$.
3. Lengths are nonnegative, so $PB=PA=9$ cm.

</details>
