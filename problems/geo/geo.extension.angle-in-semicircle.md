# Recognize a semicircle angle

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$AB$ is a diameter of a circle and $C$ is a point on the circle distinct from $A,B$. Find $\angle ACB$ in degrees.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The arc $AB$ opposite $C$ is a semicircle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

An inscribed angle is half its intercepted arc measure.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Apply the semicircle inscribed-angle theorem.

1. The intercepted semicircle measures $180^\circ$.
2. The angle at $C$ is half that measure.
3. Thus $\angle ACB=90^\circ$.

</details>
