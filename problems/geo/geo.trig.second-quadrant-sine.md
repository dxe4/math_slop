# Respect the quadrant sign

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

An angle $\theta$ lies strictly in quadrant II and has $\cos\theta=-5/13$. Find $\sin\theta$.

<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The sine-cosine square identity determines the magnitude.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Sine is positive in the specified quadrant.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An identity supplies the magnitude while the quadrant supplies the sign.

1. The identity gives $\sin^2\theta=1-25/169=144/169$.
2. Quadrant II requires positive sine.
3. Hence $\sin\theta=12/13$.

</details>
