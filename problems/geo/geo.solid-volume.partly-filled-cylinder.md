# Use a volume fraction in a uniform cylinder

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A vertical right circular cylinder has radius $3$ and height $10$. It is filled to two-fifths of its capacity. Find the liquid volume divided by $\pi$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A uniform cylinder has proportional filled height and filled volume.

1. The full volume is $\pi\cdot3^2\cdot10=90\pi$.
2. Two-fifths of that volume is $36\pi$.
3. The requested normalized volume is $36$.

</details>
