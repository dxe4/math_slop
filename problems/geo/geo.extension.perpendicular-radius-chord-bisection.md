# Use a perpendicular radius to bisect a chord

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A line through the center of a circle meets a chord $AB$ perpendicularly at $M$. Given $AB=14$, find $AM$.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The perpendicular from the center bisects a chord.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The two chord pieces therefore share its length equally.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the chord-bisection theorem.

1. The center-to-chord perpendicular gives $AM=MB$.
2. The pieces sum to $14$, so $2AM=14$.
3. Thus $AM=7$.

</details>
