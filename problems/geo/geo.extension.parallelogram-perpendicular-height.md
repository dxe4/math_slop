# Use a parallelogram altitude

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A parallelogram has base $7$ and perpendicular height $6$. Find its area.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The perpendicular height is supplied, not an oblique side.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use base times perpendicular height.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the parallelogram area formula.

1. The base and its corresponding altitude are $7,6$.
2. The area is $bh=7\cdot6$.
3. Thus it equals $42$ square units.

</details>
