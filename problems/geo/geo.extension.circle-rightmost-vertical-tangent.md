# Write a tangent at a horizontal radius endpoint

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A circle has center $(1,2)$ and radius $5$. Its tangent at the rightmost point is $x=c$. Find $c$.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The rightmost point lies five units to the right of the center.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The radius there is horizontal, so the tangent is vertical.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use a horizontal radius and its perpendicular tangent.

1. The rightmost point is $(1+5,2)=(6,2)$.
2. The tangent is perpendicular to its horizontal radius.
3. It is therefore $x=6$, giving $c=6$.

</details>
