# Compute a directed displacement

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$A=(2,5)$ and $B=(-1,7)$. Enter $\overrightarrow{AB}$ as $[x,y]$.

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"vector","expected":["-3","2"],"checker_scope":"The exact requested finite result only; a correct entry does not certify a geometric argument or award reviewed evidence."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Displacement is endpoint minus starting point.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtract the horizontal and vertical coordinates in the same order.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Subtract position vectors.

1. $\overrightarrow{AB}=b-a$.
2. The components are $-1-2=-3$ and $7-5=2$.
3. Thus the directed displacement is $[-3,2]$; reversing the order would negate both components.

</details>
