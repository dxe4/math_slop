# Locate the largest triangle angle

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

In triangle $ABC$, $AB=7$, $BC=9$, $CA=12$. Which angle is largest? Options: angle-a: angle $A$; angle-b: angle $B$; angle-c: angle $C$; equal: all three equal. Enter angle-a, angle-b, angle-c or equal.

**Options**

1. Angle A
2. Angle B
3. Angle C
4. All three equal

<details>

The largest angle lies opposite that side.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use side-angle order.

1. The longest side is $CA=12$.
2. The vertex opposite $CA$ is $B$.
3. Therefore angle $B$ is largest, with no tie because the sides are distinct.

</details>
