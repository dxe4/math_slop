# Express an arc as a full-circle fraction

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A minor arc has central angle $72^\circ$. What fraction of the full circumference does it occupy? Enter a rational number.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Arc length is proportional to central angle in a fixed circle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Compare $72^\circ$ to a full $360^\circ$ turn.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Normalize the angle by a full turn.

1. The arc-to-circumference ratio equals $72/360$.
2. Divide numerator and denominator by $72$.
3. The fraction is $1/5$.

</details>
