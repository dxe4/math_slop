# Choose the coefficient for an affine combination

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Coefficients $2,-3,t$ are to define an affine combination of three points. Using the defining condition that the coefficients sum to one, find $t$.

<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Affine coefficients may be negative.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use the supplied sum-one condition, not a sum-zero condition.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Enforce translation-independent point combination coefficients.

1. The affine condition is $2-3+t=1$.
2. This simplifies to $t-1=1$.
3. Thus $t=2$; positivity is not required for an affine combination.

</details>
