# Perform a half-turn about a specified center

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Rotate $P=(4,6)$ by $180^\circ$ about $C=(1,2)$. Enter the image as $[x,y]$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The center is the midpoint of a point and its half-turn image.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use image $2C-P$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use central symmetry about the rotation center.

1. The displacement from $C$ to $P$ is $(3,4)$.
2. A half-turn negates this displacement to $(-3,-4)$.
3. Adding $C$ gives image $(-2,-2)$.

</details>
