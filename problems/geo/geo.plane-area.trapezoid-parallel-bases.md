# Average a trapezoid pair of bases

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A trapezoid has parallel bases of lengths $8$ and $14$ and perpendicular distance $5$ between them. Find its area.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The area uses the average of the two parallel base lengths.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Multiply the average base by their perpendicular separation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Two congruent copies form a parallelogram with the sum of the base lengths.

1. The average base length is $(8+14)/2=11$.
2. The perpendicular height is $5$.
3. The area is $11\cdot5=55$.

</details>
