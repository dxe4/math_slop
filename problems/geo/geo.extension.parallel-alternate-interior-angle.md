# Match alternate interior angles

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Two distinct parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One interior angle is $123^\circ$. Find its alternate interior angle in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The two angles lie between the parallels on opposite sides of the transversal.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Alternate interior angles of parallel lines are equal.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the alternate-interior relation.

1. The two given lines are parallel, so the alternate-interior theorem applies.
2. The requested angle is the alternate partner of the angle measuring $123^\circ$.
3. The requested measure is $123^\circ$, rather than its supplement.

</details>
