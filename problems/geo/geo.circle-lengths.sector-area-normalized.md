# Find a sector area from its central angle

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A minor circular sector has radius $6$ and central angle $120^\circ$. Find its area divided by $\pi$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A sector has the same fraction of disk area as its angle has of a full turn.

1. The fraction is $120/360=1/3$.
2. The full disk area is $36\pi$, so the sector area is $12\pi$.
3. The requested value is $12$.

</details>
