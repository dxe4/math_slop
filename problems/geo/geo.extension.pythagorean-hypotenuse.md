# Find a right triangle hypotenuse

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A right triangle has perpendicular legs of lengths $5$ and $12$. Find its hypotenuse.

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Apply Pythagoras to the two legs.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Take the positive square root of $5^2+12^2$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Apply Pythagoras.

1. Writing the hypotenuse as $c$, $c^2=5^2+12^2$.
2. The sum is $25+144=169$.
3. The positive length is $c=13$.

</details>
