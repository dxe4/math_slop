# Intersect perpendicular bisectors

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, $C=(0,8)$. Find the circumcenter as $[x,y]$ using perpendicular bisectors.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The bisector of horizontal $AB$ is vertical through its midpoint.

The bisector of vertical $AC$ is horizontal through its midpoint.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Intersect two equal-distance loci.

1. The midpoint of $AB$ is $(3,0)$, giving perpendicular bisector $x=3$.
2. The midpoint of $AC$ is $(0,4)$, giving perpendicular bisector $y=4$.
3. Their intersection $[3,4]$ is equidistant from all vertices.

</details>
