# Find an equilateral area coefficient

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

An equilateral triangle has side $8$. Using $A=(\sqrt3/4)s^2$, write $A=q\sqrt3$ and find $q$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Separate the coefficient of the fixed radical.

1. Substitution gives $A=(\sqrt3/4)\cdot64$.
2. The rational factor is $64/4=16$.
3. Hence $q=16$.
