# Read a line normal vector

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Read the supplied coefficient-normal convention.

1. The equation has form $ax+by=c$ with $a=3,b=-4$.
2. A direction vector along the line has dot product zero with $(a,b)$.
3. The requested coefficient normal is $[3,-4]$.
