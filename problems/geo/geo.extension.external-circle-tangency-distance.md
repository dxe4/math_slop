# Measure externally tangent center separation

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Two circles of radii $3$ and $8$ are externally tangent. Find the distance between their centers.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The tangency point lies between the centers.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The center separation is the sum of the two radii.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the external tangency alignment.

1. At external tangency the two radii to the contact point lie on the center line in opposite directions.
2. The contact point partitions the center-to-center segment.
3. The distance is $3+8=11$.

</details>
