# Compare two tangents from one point

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

From an exterior point $P$, tangents touch a circle at $T,U$. Given $PT=11$, find $PU$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The tangent segments share exterior endpoint $P$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The two center-radius right triangles have equal hypotenuse and radius.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use equality of tangent lengths from a point.

1. Let $O$ be the center. Triangles $PTO,PUO$ are right with shared hypotenuse $PO$.
2. Their radii $OT,OU$ are equal, so the other legs are equal.
3. Thus $PU=PT=11$.

</details>
