# Convert degrees to a coefficient of pi

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Write $150^\circ=q\pi$ radians. Enter the rational number $q$.

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

$180^\circ$ corresponds to $\pi$ radians.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The requested coefficient is the fraction $150/180$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the degree-to-radian proportion.

1. The conversion factor is $\pi/180$ radians per degree.
2. Thus $150^\circ=(150/180)\pi$ radians.
3. Reducing by $30$ gives $q=5/6$.

</details>
