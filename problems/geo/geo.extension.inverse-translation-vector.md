# Undo a translation

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A translation adds vector $(4,-3)$ to every point. Enter the vector for its inverse translation.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The two displacements must sum to zero.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Negate both components.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the additive inverse displacement.

1. Composing translations adds their displacement vectors.
2. To obtain the identity, $(4,-3)+v=(0,0)$.
3. Thus the inverse displacement is $[-4,3]$.

</details>
