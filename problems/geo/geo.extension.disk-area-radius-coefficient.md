# Find the pi coefficient of disk area

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A disk has radius $7$ cm. Its area is $q\pi$ square centimetres. Find $q$.

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":"49","checker_scope":"The exact requested finite result only; a correct entry does not certify a geometric argument or award reviewed evidence."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Disk area is $\pi r^2$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The requested coefficient is the radius squared.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Separate the rational coefficient from pi.

1. Substitute $r=7$ in $A=\pi r^2$.
2. This gives $A=49\pi$ square centimetres.
3. Thus $q=49$.

</details>
