<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Compare with the standard form $y^2=4px$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The focus lies on the opening axis, not on the parabola itself.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The standard coefficient specifies the signed focal offset from the vertex.

1. Comparing $y^2=8x$ with $y^2=4px$ gives $p=2$.
2. The vertex is the origin and the axis points along positive x.
3. The focus is $(2,0)$.

</details>
