# Complete a straight angle

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Two adjacent angles form a straight angle. One is $128^\circ$. Find the other angle in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A straight angle measures $180^\circ$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Write $128+x=180$ before isolating $x$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Subtract the known part from the whole straight angle.

1. Adjacency and the straight outer rays give $128+x=180$.
2. Subtraction gives $x=180-128=52$.
3. This is positive and less than $180$, as required for the stated angle.

</details>
