# Compute a planar dot product

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

For $u=(3,4)$ and $v=(-2,5)$, find $u\cdot v$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The Euclidean dot product pairs matching coordinates.

1. The coordinate products are $3(-2)=-6$ and $4\cdot5=20$.
2. The dot product is their sum.
3. Thus $u\cdot v=-6+20=14$.
