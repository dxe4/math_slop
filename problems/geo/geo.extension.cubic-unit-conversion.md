# Convert a cubic unit

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Given $1$ dm $=10$ cm, express $2$ cubic decimetres in cubic centimetres.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The conversion applies in all three independent dimensions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

One cubic decimetre contains $10^3$ cubic centimetres.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Cube the length conversion.

1. A unit-decimetre cube is $10$ cm in each direction.
2. Its volume is $10\cdot10\cdot10=1000$ cubic centimetres.
3. Two such units give $2000$ cubic centimetres.

</details>
