# Relate two tangents to their radii

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Tangents from an exterior point $P$ touch a circle at $A$ and $B$. If the angle $APB$ between the tangent segments is $60^\circ$, find the minor central angle $AOB$ in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A radius is perpendicular to the tangent at its contact point.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use the angle sum in quadrilateral OAPB.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Two right angles relate the exterior angle to the minor central angle.

1. Angles $OAP$ and $OBP$ are each $90^\circ$.
2. The quadrilateral angle sum gives $\angle AOB+60+90+90=360$.
3. Thus $\angle AOB=120^\circ$.

</details>
