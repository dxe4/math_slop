# Find a vertex tangent segment

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$BC=13$, $CA=14$, $AB=15$. The incircle touches $AB$ at $T$. Find $AT$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Tangent segments from the same vertex are equal.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Write the sides as $x+y,y+z,z+x$ and eliminate $y,z$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Solve the tangent-length partition.

1. Let tangent lengths from $A,B,C$ be $x,y,z$.
2. Then $x+y=15$, $y+z=13$, $z+x=14$.
3. The first plus third minus second equation gives $2x=16$.
4. Therefore $AT=x=8$.

</details>
