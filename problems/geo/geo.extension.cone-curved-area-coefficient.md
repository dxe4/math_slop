# Use a cone slant height

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A right circular cone has radius $3$ and slant height $5$. Its curved surface area, excluding the base, is $q\pi$. Use $A=\pi r\ell$ to find $q$.

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The given $5$ is slant height, as required by the supplied formula.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Multiply the radius and slant height.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Apply the cone lateral-area formula.

1. Here $r=3$ and $\ell=5$.
2. The curved area is $\pi\cdot3\cdot5=15\pi$.
3. Thus $q=15$; adding a base would answer a different question.

</details>
