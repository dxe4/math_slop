# Find a horizontal-axis intercept

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Find the $x$-coordinate where $3x+2y=12$ meets the $x$-axis.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Every point of the $x$-axis has $y=0$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Substitute that coordinate in the line equation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Impose the axis condition.

1. At an $x$-axis intersection, $y=0$.
2. The equation becomes $3x=12$.
3. Thus $x=4$.

</details>
