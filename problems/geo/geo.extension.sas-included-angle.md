# Identify the SAS included angle

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Triangles $ABC,DEF$ have $AB=DE$ and $AC=DF$. Which equality directly supplies their SAS included-angle condition? Options: vertex: $\angle BAC=\angle EDF$; base: $\angle ABC=\angle DEF$; last: $\angle ACB=\angle DFE$; perimeter: equal perimeters. Enter vertex, base, last or perimeter.

**Options**

1. Angle BAC equals angle EDF
2. Angle ABC equals angle DEF
3. Angle ACB equals angle DFE
4. Equal perimeters

Match the angle enclosed by the known sides.

1. The specified sides meet at $A$ and $D$.
2. The included angles are $\angle BAC$ and $\angle EDF$.
3. The vertex option supplies exactly their equality.
