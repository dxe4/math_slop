<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The midpoint is the equal affine combination of the endpoints.

1. The midpoint x-coordinate is $(-3+5)/2=1$.
2. The y-coordinate is $(7-1)/2=3$.
3. The midpoint is $(1,3)$.
