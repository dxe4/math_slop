# Find a parabola tangent line

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Find the tangent to $y^2=8x$ at $(2,4)$. Write it as $y=mx+b$ and enter $[m,b]$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Differentiate the defining equation implicitly or use its standard tangent form.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

At the specified point, $2y\,dy/dx=8$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Find the slope and impose incidence.

1. The point satisfies $4^2=8\cdot2$.
2. Implicit differentiation gives $dy/dx=4/y$, which equals $1$ at $(2,4)$.
3. The line is $y-4=x-2$, so $[m,b]=[1,2]$.

</details>
