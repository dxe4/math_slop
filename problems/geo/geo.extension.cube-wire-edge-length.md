# Count every edge of a cube frame

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A wire frame uses each of the twelve edges of a cube exactly once and has no extra joining length. Every edge is $3$ cm. Find the total wire length in centimetres.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Sum twelve equal segment lengths.

1. The frame has twelve segments of length $3$ cm.
2. No segment is counted twice and no connecting allowance is added.
3. The total length is $12\cdot3=36$ cm.

</details>
