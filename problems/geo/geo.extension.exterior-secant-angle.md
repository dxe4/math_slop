# Use the difference of intercepted arcs

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Two secants from exterior point $P$ meet a circle in near points $A,C$ and far points $B,D$, respectively. The far intercepted arc $BD$ measures $140^\circ$ and the near intercepted arc $AC$ measures $60^\circ$. Find the smaller angle between the secant rays in degrees.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

An exterior secant angle uses a difference, unlike an interior chord angle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Take half the difference of the far and near intercepted arcs.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Apply the exterior-secant angle relation.

1. The arc order is specified by near and far intersections on the two rays.
2. The exterior angle is $(140-60)/2$.
3. Thus it measures $40^\circ$.

</details>
