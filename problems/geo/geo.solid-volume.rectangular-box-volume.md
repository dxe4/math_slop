<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

All three given dimensions are mutually perpendicular.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Multiply one face area by the remaining height.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A rectangular box is a stack of equal rectangular cross-sections.

1. A base with edges $3$ and $4$ has area $12$.
2. The perpendicular height is $5$.
3. The volume is $12\cdot5=60$.

</details>
