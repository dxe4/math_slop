# Compare distances to angle sides

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$P$ lies on the internal bisector of an angle strictly between $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$. Its perpendicular distance to one supporting side line is $3$ cm. Find its perpendicular distance to the other supporting side line in centimetres.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Drop perpendiculars from $P$ to both lines.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The resulting right triangles share a hypotenuse and equal angles at the original vertex.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use equidistance from the angle side lines.

1. The two perpendicular-foot triangles are right triangles with common vertex-to-$P$ hypotenuse.
2. The bisector makes their acute vertex angles equal, so the perpendicular legs are equal.
3. The second distance is therefore $3$ cm.

</details>
