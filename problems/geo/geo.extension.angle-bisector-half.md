# Read an internal angle bisector

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

An angle of $118^\circ$ is divided by its internal bisector. Find either resulting angle in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A bisector produces two equal angles.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Two copies of the unknown measure sum to $118$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Split the given angle equally.

1. By definition each part has measure $x$ for the same $x$.
2. The whole-angle relation is $2x=118$.
3. Dividing by two gives $x=59^\circ$.

</details>
