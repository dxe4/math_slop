# Reflect in an offset vertical mirror

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Reflect $(1,5)$ in line $x=3$. Enter the image as $[x,y]$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The vertical mirror leaves the ordinate unchanged.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The two abscissae have midpoint $3$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use equal horizontal distances from the mirror.

1. The point is two units to the left of $x=3$.
2. Its image is two units to the right, so the image abscissa is $5$.
3. The ordinate stays $5$, giving $[5,5]$.

</details>
