# Normalize a circumference by pi

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A circle has radius $7$. Find its circumference divided by $\pi$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The normalization removes the transcendental constant from the requested value.

1. The circumference is $2\pi r$.
2. With $r=7$, it is $14\pi$.
3. Dividing by $\pi$ gives $14$.

</details>
