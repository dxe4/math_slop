# Locate a hyperbola vertex

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

For $x^2/9-y^2/16=1$, find the positive $x$-coordinate of its vertex on the $x$-axis.

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A point on the $x$-axis has $y=0$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Choose the positive root after substitution.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Intersect the hyperbola with its transverse axis.

1. Setting $y=0$ gives $x^2/9=1$.
2. Thus $x=\pm3$.
3. The requested positive vertex has $x=3$.

</details>
