# Remove a complete cosine turn

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Find $\cos(2\pi+\pi/3)$ as a rational number.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Cosine has period $2\pi$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The remaining standard angle is $\pi/3$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use periodicity and a standard angle.

1. A full turn returns the unit-circle point to itself.
2. Thus $\cos(2\pi+\pi/3)=\cos(\pi/3)$.
3. The value is $1/2$.

</details>
