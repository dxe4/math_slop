# Match minor arcs of equal chords

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Equal chords $AB$ and $CD$ lie in the same circle. The minor arc $AB$ measures $84^\circ$. Find the minor arc $CD$ in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Equal chords have equal distances from the center.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

They subtend equal minor central angles, hence equal minor arcs.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the equal-chord arc relation.

1. Equal chord lengths in the same circle determine equal smaller central angles.
2. The minor arc measures are those central angle measures.
3. Therefore the minor arc $CD$ measures $84^\circ$.

</details>
