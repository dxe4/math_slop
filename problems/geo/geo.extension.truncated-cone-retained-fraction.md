# Remove a small similar cone

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A cone of perpendicular height $9$ is cut by a plane parallel to its base at distance $3$ from the apex. The smaller cone above the cut is removed. Find the fraction of the original volume that remains. Enter a rational number.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The removed cone is similar to the whole cone with length factor $1/3$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Volume uses the cube of that factor; subtract the removed fraction from one.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the three-dimensional similarity scale.

1. The height ratio and hence every corresponding length ratio is $3/9=1/3$.
2. The removed volume fraction is $(1/3)^3=1/27$.
3. The retained frustum fraction is $1-1/27=26/27$.

</details>
