# Recover an isosceles base angle

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$AB=AC$ in triangle $ABC$, and $\angle BAC=40^\circ$. Find $\angle ABC$ in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Equal sides have equal opposite angles at $B,C$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The base angles share the remaining $140^\circ$ equally.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the isosceles angle relation.

1. Let the two equal base angles be $x$.
2. The angle sum is $40+2x=180$.
3. Hence $x=70^\circ$.

</details>
