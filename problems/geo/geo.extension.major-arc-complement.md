# Recover a major arc measure

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

The minor arc between two distinct circle points has measure $110^\circ$. Find the corresponding major arc measure in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The two arcs together make the entire circle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtract the minor arc from $360^\circ$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Partition one circle into the two complementary arcs.

1. The minor and major arcs have total measure $360^\circ$.
2. The major arc therefore measures $360-110$.
3. This is $250^\circ$, greater than $180^\circ$ as required.

</details>
