# Find a regular exterior turn

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Traverse a convex regular $8$-gon once. At each vertex take the smaller exterior turning angle. Find that angle in degrees.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The exterior turns of a convex polygon sum to one full turn.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The eight turns are equal because the octagon is regular.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Divide the full turn among equal vertices.

1. The total turn is $360^\circ$.
2. Regularity gives eight equal exterior angles, so $8x=360$.
3. Consequently $x=45^\circ$.

</details>
