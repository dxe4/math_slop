# Evaluate an isosceles right-triangle tangent

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Find $\tan(\pi/4)$.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

$\pi/4$ is a $45^\circ$ angle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The two legs of its isosceles right triangle are equal.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the ratio of equal legs.

1. A right triangle with acute angle $45^\circ$ has the other acute angle $45^\circ$.
2. Its opposite and adjacent legs are equal.
3. Thus their ratio, the tangent, is $1$.

</details>
