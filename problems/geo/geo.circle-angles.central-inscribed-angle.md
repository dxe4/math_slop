# Relate a central and an inscribed angle

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Points $A,B,C$ lie on a circle with center $O$. The minor central angle $AOB$ is $110^\circ$, and $C$ lies on the major arc from $A$ to $B$. Find $\angle ACB$ in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The inscribed angle subtends the minor arc not containing its vertex.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Compare the central and inscribed angles subtending that same arc.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An inscribed angle is half the central angle on its intercepted arc.

1. The minor arc $AB$ has measure $110^\circ$.
2. Since $C$ is on the other arc, $\angle ACB$ intercepts this minor arc.
3. The angle is $110/2=55^\circ$.

</details>
