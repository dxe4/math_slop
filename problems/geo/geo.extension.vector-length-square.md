# Compute a squared vector length

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

For $v=(3,-4)$, find $|v|^2$.

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":"25","checker_scope":"The exact requested finite result only; a correct entry does not certify a geometric argument or award reviewed evidence."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Squared Euclidean length is the sum of squared components.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The negative component becomes positive after squaring.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Apply the coordinate norm definition.

1. The norm square is $3^2+(-4)^2$.
2. The two contributions are $9$ and $16$.
3. Thus $|v|^2=25$.

</details>
