# Use the stated altitude

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A triangle has base length $14$ and perpendicular altitude $9$ to that base. Find its area.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Use half the base times its corresponding perpendicular height.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

A sloping side is not the height in the area formula.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The perpendicular altitude gives the correct area factor.

1. Triangle area is one-half of base times altitude.
2. The given base and altitude are $14$ and $9$.
3. The area is $14\cdot9/2=63$.

</details>
