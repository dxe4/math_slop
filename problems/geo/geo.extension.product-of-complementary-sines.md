# Evaluate a product without separate radicals

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Find $\sin75^\circ\sin15^\circ$ as a rational number.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Use $2\sin u\sin v=\cos(u-v)-\cos(u+v)$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The resulting angles are $60^\circ$ and $90^\circ$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Convert the product to two standard cosines.

1. The product-to-sum identity gives $2\sin75^\circ\sin15^\circ=\cos60^\circ-\cos90^\circ$.
2. The right side is $1/2-0=1/2$.
3. Dividing by two gives $1/4$.

</details>
