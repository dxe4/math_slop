# Compare the two disks of a square

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A square has an incircle tangent to all $4$ sides and a circumcircle through all four vertices. Find the ratio of circumdisk area to incircle area.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Express both radii in terms of the square side $s>0$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The circumradius is half the diagonal; compare squared radii to compare disk areas.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Cancel the common square scale and pi.

1. The incircle radius is $r=s/2$.
2. The diagonal is $s\sqrt2$, so the circumradius is $R=s\sqrt2/2$.
3. The area ratio is $\pi R^2/(\pi r^2)=(s^2/2)/(s^2/4)=2$.

</details>
