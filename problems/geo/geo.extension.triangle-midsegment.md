# Find a midpoint connector

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$D,E$ are the midpoints of $AB,AC$ in triangle $ABC$. Given $BC=18$, find $DE$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Use the theorem for the connector of two side midpoints.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

That connector has half the third-side length.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Apply the midpoint theorem.

1. The two midpoint conditions give a common ratio $1/2$ from $A$.
2. The midpoint theorem gives $DE=BC/2$.
3. Thus $DE=18/2=9$.

</details>
