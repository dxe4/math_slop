# Separate two same-side parallel chords

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A circle has radius $10$. Two parallel chords, of lengths $16$ and $12$, lie on the same side of the center. Find the perpendicular distance between their supporting lines.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The center perpendicular bisects each chord.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use the half-chords in Pythagoras, then subtract the two center distances.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Recover each chord distance and use the same-side condition.

1. The chord half-lengths are $8$ and $6$.
2. Their center distances are $\sqrt{100-64}=6$ and $\sqrt{100-36}=8$.
3. On the same side of the center, the line separation is $8-6=2$; opposite sides would instead require a sum.

</details>
