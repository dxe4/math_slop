# Find a squared space diagonal

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A rectangular box has three perpendicular edge lengths $2,3,6$. Using $d^2=a^2+b^2+c^2$, find the square of a space diagonal.

2. The three terms are $4,9,36$.
3. Their sum is $49$.
