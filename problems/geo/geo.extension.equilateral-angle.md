# Find an equilateral triangle angle

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Find one interior angle, in degrees, of an equilateral Euclidean triangle $ABC$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Equal sides give equal opposite angles.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Divide the angle sum equally among three angles.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use three equal angles.

1. All three sides are equal, so the three opposite angles are equal.
2. Write their common measure as $x$. The angle sum gives $3x=180$.
3. Thus $x=60^\circ$.

</details>
