# Recover an incircle radius

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A triangle has area $30$ and semiperimeter $15$. Using $\Delta=rs$, find its inradius $r$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Isolate the inradius.

1. Substitute $\Delta=30$ and $s=15$.
2. The equation is $30=15r$.
3. Thus $r=2$.
