# Measure a regular polygon boundary

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A regular nonagon has side length $5$ cm. Find its perimeter in centimetres.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A nonagon has nine sides.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Add nine equal boundary lengths.

1. There are nine sides, each measuring $5$ cm.
2. The perimeter is $9\cdot5$.
3. Thus the complete boundary measures $45$ cm.
