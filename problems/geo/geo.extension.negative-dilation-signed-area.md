# Track signed area through a negative dilation

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A counterclockwise triangle has signed area $6$. Apply a planar dilation of factor $-2$. Using signed-area factor $(-2)^2$, find the image signed area.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Both coordinate directions are multiplied by $-2$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The supplied area multiplier is positive.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the squared planar dilation factor.

1. The signed area multiplier is $(-2)(-2)=4$.
2. The image signed area is $4\cdot6=24$.
3. The sign remains positive; a negative planar dilation reverses each direction but preserves orientation.

</details>
