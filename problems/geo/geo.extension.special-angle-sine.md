# Evaluate a standard sine

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Find $\sin(\pi/6)$ as a rational number.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

$\pi/6$ radians is $30^\circ$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Halving an equilateral triangle gives the opposite-to-hypotenuse ratio.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the thirty-degree right triangle.

1. Bisect an equilateral triangle of side $2$ into two right triangles.
2. The leg opposite its $30^\circ$ angle has length $1$ and the hypotenuse length $2$.
3. The sine is therefore $1/2$.

</details>
