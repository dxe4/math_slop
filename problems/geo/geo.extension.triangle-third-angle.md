# Complete three triangle angles

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Two interior angles of a Euclidean triangle are $40^\circ$ and $65^\circ$. Find the third angle in degrees.

The three interior angles sum to $180^\circ$.

<details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the triangle angle sum.

1. The two known angles total $40+65=105^\circ$.
2. The remaining angle is $180-105$.
3. Thus its measure is $75^\circ$, a positive angle as required.

</details>
