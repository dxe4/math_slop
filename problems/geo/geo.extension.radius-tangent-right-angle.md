# Read a radius-tangent angle

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A line is tangent to a circle at $T$ and $O$ is the center. Find the smaller angle, in degrees, between line $OT$ and the tangent.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The radius to the point of tangency is perpendicular to the tangent.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Perpendicular lines meet at a right angle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the tangent-radius perpendicularity.

1. The line touches the circle at $T$ and $OT$ is its radius.
2. The tangent-radius theorem gives perpendicularity at $T$.
3. The angle is $90^\circ$.

</details>
