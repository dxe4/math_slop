# Preserve the sign of a secant

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$\cos\theta=-4/5$. Find $\sec\theta$, where $\sec\theta=1/\cos\theta$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Apply the supplied reciprocal definition.

1. The definition gives $\sec\theta=1/(-4/5)$.
2. Division by a nonzero fraction multiplies by its reciprocal.
3. Thus $\sec\theta=-5/4$.
