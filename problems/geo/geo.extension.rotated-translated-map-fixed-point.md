# Find a fixed point of a rigid motion

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A plane map is $F(x,y)=(2-y,x)$, a quarter-turn followed by a translation. Find its fixed point as $[x,y]$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A fixed point has the same coordinates before and after the map.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Solve $x=2-y$ together with $y=x$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Solve the fixed-point equations.

1. The fixed-point equations are $x+y=2$ and $y=x$.
2. Substitution gives $2x=2$, so $x=1$ and $y=1$.
3. Directly $F(1,1)=(1,1)$, confirming the unique fixed point.

</details>
