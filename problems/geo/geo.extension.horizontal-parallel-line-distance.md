# Measure separation of horizontal lines

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Find the perpendicular distance between the lines $y=7$ and $y=-2$.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A perpendicular to a horizontal line is vertical.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Take the absolute difference of the two fixed ordinates.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Measure the vertical separation.

1. A common perpendicular segment can have endpoints $(0,7)$ and $(0,-2)$.
2. Its length is $|7-(-2)|$.
3. The distance is $9$.

</details>
