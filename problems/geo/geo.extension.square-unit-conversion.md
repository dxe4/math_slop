# Convert a square unit

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Given $1$ m $=100$ cm, express $7$ square metres in square centimetres.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Convert both side dimensions of a one-metre square.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

One square metre contains $100\cdot100$ square centimetres.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Square the length-unit conversion.

1. A $1$ m by $1$ m square is $100$ cm by $100$ cm.
2. Thus $1$ square metre is $10000$ square centimetres.
3. Seven such units contain $7\cdot10000=70000$ square centimetres.

</details>
