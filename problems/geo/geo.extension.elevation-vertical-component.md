# Extract a vertical component

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A straight segment of length $10$ makes an angle of $30^\circ$ above a horizontal line. Use vertical component $h=10\sin30^\circ$ to find its vertical rise.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Evaluate the supplied component formula.

1. The full segment is the right-triangle hypotenuse.
2. The supplied formula gives $h=10(1/2)$.
3. The vertical rise is $5$.
