# Recover the internal part of a secant

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

From exterior point $P$, one secant has near point $A$, far point $B$, with $PA=3$ and $PB=12$. Another has near point $C$, far point $D$, with $PC=4$. Find $CD$.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Power of a point equates external times whole lengths.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

First find $PD$, then remove the exterior piece $PC$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Separate the whole second secant from its internal part.

1. The secant power is $PA\cdot PB=3\cdot12=36$.
2. Thus $4PD=36$, so $PD=9$.
3. The stated order gives $CD=PD-PC=9-4=5$.

</details>
