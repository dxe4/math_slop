# Add ordered collinear lengths

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$A,B,C$ are collinear with $B$ strictly between $A$ and $C$. Given $AB=4$ cm and $BC=9$ cm, find $AC$ in centimetres.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The stated order puts the two segments end to end.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use $AC=AB+BC$, rather than a difference.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the between-point addition relation.

1. Because $B$ lies between $A$ and $C$, the segments partition $AC$.
2. Therefore $AC=AB+BC=4+9$.
3. The length is $13$ cm.

</details>
