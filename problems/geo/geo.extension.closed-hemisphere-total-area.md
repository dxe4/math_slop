# Include a hemisphere circular base

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A solid hemisphere has radius $4$. Its entire boundary includes the curved half-sphere and its flat circular base. Using curved area $2\pi r^2$ and base area $\pi r^2$, write the total as $q\pi$ and find $q$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Count both types of boundary surface.

1. The curved part has area $2\pi\cdot16=32\pi$.
2. The base has area $16\pi$.
3. The total is $48\pi$, giving $q=48$.
