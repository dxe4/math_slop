# Use the specified tangent-chord angle

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A tangent at $A$ makes a $35^\circ$ angle with chord $AB$, measured in the segment opposite a point $C$ on the circle. Find the inscribed angle $ACB$ in the alternate segment, in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The tangent-chord theorem equates the stated angle with the inscribed angle in the alternate segment.

1. The tangent touches the circle at endpoint $A$ of chord $AB$.
2. The alternate-segment hypothesis says $\angle ACB$ equals the specified tangent-chord angle.
3. Hence $\angle ACB=35^\circ$.

</details>
