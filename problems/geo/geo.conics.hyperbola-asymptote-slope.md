# Read a hyperbola asymptote slope

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

For the hyperbola $x^2/9-y^2/16=1$, find the positive slope of its two asymptotes.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The transverse axis is horizontal.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The asymptotes in standard form have slopes equal to plus or minus the vertical-to-horizontal semiaxis ratio.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The homogeneous quadratic part determines the asymptote directions.

1. The semiaxis parameters are $a=3$ and $b=4$.
2. Setting $x^2/9-y^2/16=0$ yields the asymptotic lines $y=\pm(4/3)x$.
3. The positive slope is $4/3$.

</details>
