# Measure a diagonal cube section

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A cube has edge $6$. A plane contains one vertical edge and the opposite parallel vertical edge, so its horizontal trace is a diagonal of the square base. The rectangular section has area $q\sqrt2$. Find $q$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

</details>

The other is a diagonal of the horizontal square base.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Identify the true dimensions of the section rectangle.

1. The vertical dimension is $6$.
2. The horizontal base diagonal is $\sqrt{6^2+6^2}=6\sqrt2$.
3. The section area is $6\cdot6\sqrt2=36\sqrt2$, giving $q=36$.

</details>
