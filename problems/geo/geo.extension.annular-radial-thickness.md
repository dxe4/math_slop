# Find an annular outer radius

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Two concentric circles bound a ring. The inner radius is $5$ cm and the radial thickness of the ring is $3$ cm. Find the outer radius in centimetres.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

It is the difference between outer and inner radii.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Add the inner radius and radial increment.

1. On any common radius line, the outer boundary is $3$ cm beyond the inner boundary.
2. Thus $R-5=3$.
3. The outer radius is $R=8$ cm.
