# Match corresponding angles

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Two distinct parallel lines are cut by a transversal. An angle is $68^\circ$. Find the corresponding angle at the other intersection, in degrees.

Apply the corresponding-angle equality.

1. The stated parallelism is the hypothesis needed for corresponding angles to agree.
2. The requested angle corresponds to the given $68^\circ$ angle.
3. Its measure is therefore $68^\circ$.
