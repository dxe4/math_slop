# Use the centroid division ratio

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$D$ is the midpoint of $BC$ in a nondegenerate triangle $ABC$, and $G$ is its centroid. Given $AG=12$, find $GD$.

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The centroid divides a median $2:1$ from its vertex.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The longer piece $AG$ is twice $GD$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the centroid ratio.

1. The midpoint condition makes $AD$ a median.
2. The centroid gives $AG=2GD$.
3. Therefore $GD=12/2=6$.

</details>
