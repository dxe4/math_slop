# Choose a component to force perpendicularity

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Find $t$ such that vectors $(2,-3)$ and $(3,t)$ are perpendicular.

```answer

```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Perpendicular nonzero vectors have dot product zero.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use $2\cdot3-3t=0$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Impose the dot-product condition.

1. The dot product is $6-3t$.
2. Setting it to zero gives $3t=6$.
3. Thus $t=2$; both resulting vectors are nonzero.

</details>
