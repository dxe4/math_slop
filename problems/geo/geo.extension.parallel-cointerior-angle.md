# Complete same-side interior angles

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. A pair of interior angles lies on the same side of that transversal. One is $74^\circ$. Find the other in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Same-side interior angles are supplementary for parallel lines.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Write an equation with total $180^\circ$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the supplementary interior pair.

1. Parallelism gives $74+x=180$.
2. Therefore $x=180-74=106$.
3. The pair is supplementary; equality would apply to an alternate, not same-side, pair.

</details>
