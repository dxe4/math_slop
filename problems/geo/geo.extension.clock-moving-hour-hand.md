# Include continuous hour-hand motion

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

On an ideal 12-hour clock, both hands move continuously at constant rates. At $3{:}20$, find the smaller angle between them in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The minute hand turns $6^\circ$ per minute.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The hour hand has moved beyond the $3$ mark by one third of the angle between consecutive hour marks.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Measure both hand directions from twelve.

1. The minute hand is $20\cdot6=120^\circ$ clockwise from twelve.
2. The hour hand is $3\cdot30+20(1/2)=100^\circ$ clockwise from twelve.
3. Their absolute difference is $20^\circ$, already the smaller angle.

</details>
