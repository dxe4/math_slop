# Measure a rectangular boundary

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A rectangle has side lengths $7$ cm and $4$ cm. Find its perimeter in centimetres.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Each of the two side lengths occurs twice.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Add all four sides, or double the sum of adjacent sides.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Count both pairs of opposite sides.

1. Opposite sides of a rectangle have equal lengths.
2. The perimeter is $7+4+7+4$.
3. The sum is $22$ cm; multiplying the two lengths would instead find area.

</details>
