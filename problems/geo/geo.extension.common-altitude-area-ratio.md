# Compare triangles above one line

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

$B,D,C$ are collinear in that order, with $BD=9$ and $DC=6$. Point $A$ lies off their line. Find $[ABD]/[ADC]$ as a rational number.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The two triangles have the same perpendicular altitude from $A$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Their areas are therefore in the ratio of their bases on the common line.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Cancel the shared altitude.

1. Let the common altitude be $h>0$.
2. The area ratio is $(9h/2)/(6h/2)=9/6$.
3. Thus it equals $3/2$.

</details>
