# Recover a diameter

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A circle has radius $6$ cm. Find its diameter in centimetres.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A diameter passes through the center.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

It is made of two radii lying in opposite directions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Double the radius.

1. The center separates a diameter into two radii.
2. Each half has length $6$ cm.
3. The diameter is $6+6=12$ cm.

</details>
