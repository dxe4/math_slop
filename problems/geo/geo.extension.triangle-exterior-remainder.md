# Resolve a triangle exterior angle

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A triangle has an exterior angle of $112^\circ$ formed by extending one side. One of the two remote interior angles is $35^\circ$. Find the other remote angle in degrees.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The interior angle adjacent to the exterior is its supplement.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The two remote angles together equal the exterior angle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Combine the straight-angle and triangle-angle sums.

1. The adjacent interior angle is $180-112=68^\circ$.
2. The remaining two angles have sum $180-68=112^\circ$.
3. Therefore the requested angle is $112-35=77^\circ$.

</details>
