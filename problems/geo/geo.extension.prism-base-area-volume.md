# Use a nonrectangular prism base

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A right prism has a triangular base of area $12$ and perpendicular distance $5$ between its bases. Find its volume.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Prism volume is base area times perpendicular height.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The triangular base area is already supplied.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Extrude the given base through the prism height.

1. The constant cross-sectional area is $12$.
2. The perpendicular extrusion length is $5$.
3. The volume is $12\cdot5=60$.

</details>
