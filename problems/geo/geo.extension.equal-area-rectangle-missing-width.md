# Preserve an area during reshaping

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A rectangle of dimensions $6$ by $6$ is reshaped into another rectangle of the same area with length $4$. Find its width.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The area stays $6\cdot6$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Divide that area by the new positive length.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use equality of the two rectangle areas.

1. The original area is $36$.
2. If the new width is $w$, area equality gives $4w=36$.
3. Therefore $w=9$.

</details>
