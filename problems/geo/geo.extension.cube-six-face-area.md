# Measure a cube surface

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A cube has edge length $3$. Find its total surface area.

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":"54","checker_scope":"The exact requested finite result only; a correct entry does not certify a geometric argument or award reviewed evidence."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

There are six congruent square faces.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Each face has area $3^2$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Sum the six equal face areas.

1. Each face has area $9$.
2. The six face interiors do not overlap.
3. The total is $6\cdot9=54$.

</details>
