# Read a supporting line from an attained bound

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A nonempty convex region $K$ lies in $x\le4$ and contains a point with $x=4$. Its right supporting vertical line is $x=c$. Find $c$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the supplied attained coordinate bound.

1. Every point of $K$ is on or left of line $x=4$.
2. The point with coordinate $4$ lies on that line.
3. Both support conditions hold, so $c=4$.
