# Unroll a cylinder lateral surface

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A right circular cylinder has radius $4$ and height $7$. Its curved surface area, excluding both bases, is $q\pi$. Using $A=2\pi rh$, find $q$.

```answer
{"answer":{"kind":"integer","expected":"56","checker_scope":"The exact requested finite result only; a correct entry does not certify a geometric argument or award reviewed evidence."}}
```

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Only the curved surface is requested.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The coefficient is $2rh$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Use the unrolled rectangular lateral surface.

1. The rectangle width is circumference $2\pi r=8\pi$.
2. Its height is $7$.
3. The area is $56\pi$, so $q=56$.

</details>
