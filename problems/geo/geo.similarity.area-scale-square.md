# Scale area rather than length

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A polygon of area $24$ is enlarged by a positive length scale factor $3/2$. Find the area of the enlarged polygon.

<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Area uses two independent length dimensions.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Square the length factor before applying it to the original area.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Similarity squares the length scale for area.

1. A length scale $3/2$ gives area scale $(3/2)^2=9/4$.
2. The original area is $24$.
3. The enlarged area is $24\cdot9/4=54$.

</details>
