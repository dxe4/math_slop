# Distinguish cone height from slant height

*Easy · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

A right circular cone has base radius $3$ and perpendicular height $4$. Find its volume divided by $\pi$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The cone occupies one-third the volume of the cylinder with the same base and height.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Use the perpendicular height, not a sloping edge.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The cone volume uses the base disk area and the perpendicular height.

1. The base area is $9\pi$.
2. The cone volume is $(1/3)\cdot9\pi\cdot4=12\pi$.
3. The ratio to $\pi$ is $12$.

</details>
