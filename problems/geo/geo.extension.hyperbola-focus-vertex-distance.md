# Find a focus-to-near-vertex distance

*Medium · [Geometry and trigonometry problems](index.md)*

For hyperbola $x^2/9-y^2/16=1$, find the distance from its positive-axis focus to its positive-axis vertex.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

For a hyperbola, $c^2=a^2+b^2$.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The two specified points lie on the same positive axis, so subtract their coordinates.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Recover the focal distance before measuring the segment.

1. Here $a=3,b=4$, so $c=\sqrt{9+16}=5$.
2. The specified focus is $(5,0)$ and vertex is $(3,0)$.
3. Their distance is $5-3=2$.

</details>
