# Combine two derivatives in a curvature formula

*Medium · [Calculus](index.md)*

For $y=x^2$ find the curvature at x=0 using κ=\|y″\|/\[1+(y′)²\]^(3/2). Coordinates use the same length unit; report the numerical curvature in its reciprocal unit.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Curvature depends on both slope and its rate of change.

1. Here y′=2x and y″=2.
2. At zero the denominator is (1+0)^{3/2}=1.
3. Therefore κ=\|2\|/1=2 per length unit.
