# Reverse a scalar arc-length integral

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Along C, $\int_Cf\,ds=3$. Find the scalar integral along the same geometric curve traversed backward.

<details>

Arc length uses speed, a nonnegative magnitude.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Reversing velocity does not change its norm.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Scalar arc-length measure does not record a traversal direction.

1. Reversal changes r′ to its negative after reindexing.
2. The speed \|\|r′\|\| and geometric sampling are unchanged.
3. Therefore the scalar integral remains 3.

</details>
