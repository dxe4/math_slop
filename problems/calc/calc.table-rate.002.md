# Use the requested part of a motion table

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

A position table gives times $0, 2, 5$ seconds and corresponding positions $-4, 8, 11$ metres. Find the average velocity from $t = 2$ to $t = 5$ seconds. Values between the listed times are not supplied and are not needed.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Find the two rows corresponding to the requested interval.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtract the earlier position from the later position, then divide by elapsed time.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Average velocity needs endpoint positions only; an intermediate measurement does not change the interval quotient.

1. The requested endpoints have positions $8$ m and $11$ m.
2. Position change = $11 - (8) = 3$ m; time change = $5 - 2 = 3$ s.
3. Average velocity = $3/3 = 1$ $m/s$.

</details>
