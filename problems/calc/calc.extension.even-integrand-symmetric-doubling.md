# Use even symmetry with a supplied half integral

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Let f be even and integrable on \[−2,2\], with $\int_0^2f=3$. Find ∫₋₂²f.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Even symmetry duplicates the signed half integral.

1. Evenness gives ∫₋₂⁰f=∫₀²f=3.
2. The full integral is the sum of both halves.
3. Its value is 6.
