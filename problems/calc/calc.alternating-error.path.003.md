# Alternating-series error: check zero limit hypothesis

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

An alternating series has positive magnitudes $b_{n}=1+2^{-n}$, decreasing to $1$. Which statement is correct? Select the mathematically correct statement from the four alternatives.

**Options**

1. Alternation and decrease alone ensure convergence.
2. The zero-limit hypothesis fails; its terms do not tend to zero.
3. Alternating signs force the magnitudes to tend to zero.
4. It converges absolutely because the magnitudes are bounded.
