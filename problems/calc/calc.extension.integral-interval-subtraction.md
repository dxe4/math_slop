# Recover accumulation on the remaining interval

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

An integrable f satisfies $\int_0^5 f=9$ and ∫₀²f=4. Find ∫₂⁵f.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Interval additivity preserves the shared orientation.

1. Additivity gives ∫₀⁵f=∫₀²f+∫₂⁵f.
2. Thus 9=4+∫₂⁵f.
3. The remaining integral is 5.
