# Accumulate a supplied constant flow

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Water enters at a constant rate of $3$ litres per minute for exactly 5 minutes, with no outflow. How many litres enter?

<details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Constant-rate accumulation is rate times duration.

1. The rate is 3 L/min.
2. Over 5 minutes the units cancel as (3 L/min)(5 min).
3. The entering volume is 15 L.

</details>
