# Average rate of change: endpoint velocity

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Position is $s(t)=4t^{2}$ metres at time t seconds. Find the average velocity from $t=1$ to $t=3$, including its unit. Here $t^{2}$ means t multiplied by itself.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Divide displacement $36-4=32$ m by elapsed time $3-1=2$ s: the average velocity is $16$ $m/s$.

1. $s(1)=4$ m and $s(3)=36$ m.
2. Divide displacement $36-4=32$ m by elapsed time $3-1=2$ s: the average velocity is $16$ $m/s$.
