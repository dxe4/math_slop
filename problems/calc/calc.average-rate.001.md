# Interpret an average velocity

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

A cart has position $s(t)=3t^2+t$ metres, where $t$ is in seconds. Find its average velocity from $t=1$ to $t=2$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Average velocity is position change divided by elapsed time.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Calculate $s(1)$ and $s(2)$ separately.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An average rate compares endpoint change with the whole elapsed interval.

1. $s(1)=4$ metres and $s(2)=14$ metres.
2. Elapsed time is $1$ second, so the average velocity is $(14-4)/1=10$ m/s.

A table lists $(1,4)$ and $(2,14)$, followed by position change $10$ m and time change $1$ s.

</details>
