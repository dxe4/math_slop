# Average velocity over a longer interval

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

A moving object has position $s(t) = t^{2}/2 - t$ metres at time t seconds. Find its average velocity from $t = 2$ to $t = 6$ seconds. Give an exact value and its unit.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Average velocity is the change in position divided by the change in time.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Evaluate the position rule at both endpoints before subtracting.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An interval rate compares endpoint change with elapsed time; it need not equal every instantaneous velocity.

1. $s(2) = 0$ m and $s(6) = 12$ m.
2. The change in position is $12$ m and the elapsed time is $4$ s.
3. Average velocity = $(12 - 0)/(6 - 2) = 3$ $m/s$.

</details>
