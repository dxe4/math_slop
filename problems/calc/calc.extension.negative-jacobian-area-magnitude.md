# Use an absolute determinant for area measure

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

A one-to-one planar coordinate map has $\det DT=-3$ throughout its source region. What positive factor multiplies source area in an ordinary scalar area integral?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Area uses the determinant’s magnitude.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The negative sign records orientation, not negative area.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Unsigned area and oriented determinant are different quantities.

1. The determinant magnitude is \|−3\|.
2. It equals 3.
3. Thus the scalar area factor is 3.

</details>
