# Use tangency to annihilate a scalar differential

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Let f be differentiable at p and v tangent there to a differentiable curve lying in the level set f=f(p). What is $Df(p)[v]$?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Compose f with the level curve.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

That composition is constant, so its derivative is zero.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A differential vanishes on tangents to its own level curves.

1. Along the curve, the scalar output f does not change.
2. The chain rule identifies its derivative with Df(p)\[v\].
3. Hence Df(p)\[v\]=0.

</details>
