# Track a signed ladder endpoint velocity

*Medium · [Calculus](index.md)*

A rigid 5 m ladder has bottom distance x and top height y, both positive, so $x^2+y^2=25$. When x=4 m,y=3 m and dx/dt=3 m/s outward, find dy/dt in m/s.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Differentiate the fixed-length equation with respect to time.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The top must move downward as the bottom moves outward.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The two coordinate rates are linked by a fixed geometric constraint.

1. Differentiating gives 2x x′+2y y′=0.
2. Hence y′=−(x/y)x′=−(4/3)×3.
3. The vertical rate is −4 m/s; its sign indicates downward motion.

</details>
