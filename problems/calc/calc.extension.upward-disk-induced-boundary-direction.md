# Determine the induced disk-boundary orientation

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

A horizontal disk has upward normal $(0,0,1)$. Seen from above, is its positive Stokes boundary orientation clockwise or counterclockwise? Return {cw} or {ccw}.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Use the right-hand orientation rule.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

With the normal pointing toward the observer, the positive boundary turns counterclockwise.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A chosen surface normal determines a boundary orientation.

1. The normal points upward toward the stated view.
2. The compatible right-hand traversal is counterclockwise.
3. Return {ccw}.

</details>
