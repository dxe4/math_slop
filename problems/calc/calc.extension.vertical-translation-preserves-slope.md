# Keep the derivative under a vertical translation

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Let $g(x)=f(x)+7$ and suppose f′(a)=3. Find g′(a).

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A vertical shift changes heights but not local slopes.

1. Differentiate to get g′=f′+0.
2. At a the supplied f′ value is 3.
3. Thus g′(a)=3.
