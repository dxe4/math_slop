# Represent an arctangent difference by its tangent

*Medium · [Calculus](index.md)*

Let $I=\int_0^1[x^2+2x+2]^{-1}\,dx$. Find tan I exactly.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A trigonometric difference identity gives a rational exact observable of the integral.

1. The denominator is (x+1)²+1, so I=arctan2−arctan1.
2. The tangent difference formula gives tan I=(2−1)/(1+2·1).
3. Therefore tan I=1/3. The angle difference lies between 0 and π/2, so its tangent is defined.

</details>
