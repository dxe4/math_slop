# Find a power-series radius from a geometric ratio

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Find the radius of convergence of $\sum_{n=0}^\infty((x-2)/3)^n$ about its center 2.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The geometric convergence condition is \|(x−2)/3\|&lt;1.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Read the allowed distance from the center.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A power-series radius is a distance, not the right endpoint.

1. The condition becomes \|x−2\|&lt;3.
2. Thus convergence holds within distance 3 of center 2.
3. The radius is 3.

</details>
