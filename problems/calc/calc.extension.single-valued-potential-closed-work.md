# Use a globally supplied potential on a closed curve

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Let F=∇φ for a single-valued C¹ potential on a neighborhood of a closed piecewise-C¹ curve C. Find $\oint_CF\cdot dr$.

<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Gradient work is the potential difference between endpoints.

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

For a closed curve those endpoints coincide.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A specified global potential is stronger than a curl test alone.

1. The fundamental theorem for line integrals gives φ(end)−φ(start).
2. The start and end points are the same.
3. Thus the closed work is 0.

</details>
