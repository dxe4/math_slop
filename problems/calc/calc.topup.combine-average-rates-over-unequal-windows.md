# Combine interval averages using their lengths

*Medium · [Calculus](index.md)*

The average velocity on $[0,2]$ is 3 m/s and on \[2,5\] is -1 m/s. Find the average velocity on \[0,5\], in m/s.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Endpoint changes add across adjacent intervals.

1. The two signed displacements are six metres and negative three metres.
2. Their sum is three metres across a total of five seconds.
3. Thus the overall average velocity is three fifths metre per second.

</details>
