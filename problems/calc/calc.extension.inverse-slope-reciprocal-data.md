# Invert a nonzero local slope

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

A differentiable local inverse exists at a point where $dy/dx=3/5$. Find dx/dy for that inverse.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Inverting a regular graph exchanges the coordinate-rate quotient.

1. The inverse derivative formula gives dx/dy=1/(dy/dx).
2. The reciprocal of 3/5 is 5/3.
3. Thus dx/dy=5/3.
