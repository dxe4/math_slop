# Read the polar area factor

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

For x=r cosθ,y=r sinθ with r≥0, the area factor is $|\det DT|=r$. Find it at r=2.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A polar wedge has one radial and one angular scale factor.

1. The supplied Jacobian magnitude is r.
2. At radius 2 its value is 2.
3. Thus dA has factor 2 at that coordinate point.
