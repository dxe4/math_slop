# Limits at infinity: identify positive infinite limit

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

For $x^{2}/(x+1)$ as x tends to positive infinity, choose the correct behavior. Select the mathematically correct statement from the four alternatives.

**Options**

1. Tends to +∞.
2. Tends to $1$.
3. Tends to $0$.
4. It tends to negative infinity.
