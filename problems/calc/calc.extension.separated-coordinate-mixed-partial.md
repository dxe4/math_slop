# Use variable separation to eliminate a mixed derivative

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Let f(x,y)=g(x)+h(y) with g,h C². Find $f_{xy}$ everywhere.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Additively separated coordinates have no mixed interaction.

1. First f\_x=g′(x).
2. That expression is independent of y.
3. Therefore f\_{xy}=0 everywhere.
