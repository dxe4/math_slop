# Read an average velocity from a table

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

A position table gives times $1, 4, 7$ seconds and corresponding positions $8, 2, -10$ metres. Find the average velocity from $t = 1$ to $t = 7$ seconds. Values between the listed times are not supplied and are not needed.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Find the two rows corresponding to the requested interval.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtract the earlier position from the later position, then divide by elapsed time.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Average velocity needs endpoint positions only; an intermediate measurement does not change the interval quotient.

1. The requested endpoints have positions $8$ m and $-10$ m.
2. Position change = $-10 - (8) = -18$ m; time change = $7 - 1 = 6$ s.
3. Average velocity = $-18/6 = -3$ $m/s$.

</details>
