# Describe a disk cross-section before integrating

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A cross-section is a one-coordinate slice of the original region.

1. The inequality becomes 9/25+y²≤1.
2. Thus y²≤16/25, or \|y\|≤4/5.
3. The slice is \[−4/5,4/5\].

</details>
