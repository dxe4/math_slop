# Find the mesh of an unequal partition

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

The mesh of a partition is its largest subinterval width. Find the mesh for endpoints $0,1,3/2,3$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Choose the largest positive width.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Mesh records the coarsest interval, not the average spacing.

1. The widths are 1,1/2 and 3/2.
2. Their maximum is 3/2.
3. Thus the mesh is 3/2.

</details>
