# A negative fractional average velocity

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

A position table gives times $0, 3, 8$ seconds and corresponding positions $12, 9, 2$ metres. Find the average velocity from $t = 0$ to $t = 8$ seconds. Values between the listed times are not supplied and are not needed.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Find the two rows corresponding to the requested interval.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Subtract the earlier position from the later position, then divide by elapsed time.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Average velocity needs endpoint positions only; an intermediate measurement does not change the interval quotient.

1. The requested endpoints have positions $12$ m and $2$ m.
2. Position change = $2 - (12) = -10$ m; time change = $8 - 0 = 8$ s.
3. Average velocity = $-10/8 = -5/4$ $m/s$.

</details>
