# Find the contact condition for an origin-passing tangent

*Medium · [Calculus](index.md)*

The tangent to $y=\log x$ at x=a&gt;0 passes through the origin. Find log a.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

A tangent at a has slope 1/a.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Set the tangent line’s value at x=0 equal to zero.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The unknown contact point is determined by the tangent’s intercept condition.

1. The tangent is y=log a+(1/a)(x−a).
2. At x=0 its intercept is log a−1.
3. For the origin to lie on it, log a−1=0, so log a=1.

</details>
