# Average velocity from a quadratic position

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

A moving object has position $s(t) = 2t^{2} + 3$ metres at time t seconds. Find its average velocity from $t = 0$ to $t = 2$ seconds. Give an exact value and its unit.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

Average velocity is the change in position divided by the change in time.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Evaluate the position rule at both endpoints before subtracting.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

An interval rate compares endpoint change with elapsed time; it need not equal every instantaneous velocity.

1. $s(0) = 3$ m and $s(2) = 11$ m.
2. The change in position is $8$ m and the elapsed time is $2$ s.
3. Average velocity = $(11 - 3)/(2 - 0) = 4$ $m/s$.

</details>
