# Read the dimension of a regular level surface

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

For a C¹ scalar F on R³ with F(p)=0 and $\nabla F(p)\ne0$, what is the dimension of its local tangent space to F=0?

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

One independent scalar equation removes one dimension.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

The tangent space is the kernel of a nonzero linear functional on R³.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The nonzero differential has rank one.

1. The differential DF(p) maps R³ to R and has rank one.
2. Rank-nullity gives kernel dimension 3−1.
3. The tangent-space dimension is 2.

</details>
