# Geometric partial sums and series: evaluate signed ratio infinite sum

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Find the sum of the infinite geometric series with first term $3$ and ratio $-1/2$. You may use $a/(1-r)$ when $|r|<1$.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The sum is $3/(1-(-1/2))=3/(3/2)=2$.

1. The ratio has magnitude 1/2&lt;1, so the convergence formula applies.
2. The sum is $3/(1-(-1/2))=3/(3/2)=2$.
