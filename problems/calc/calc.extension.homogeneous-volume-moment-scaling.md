# Combine integrand degree with volume scaling

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Under x=λu in R³ with λ&gt;0, a degree-one homogeneous integrand gains factor λ and dV gains λ³. What exponent k gives the total factor $\lambda^k$?

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

A moment scales by both its density degree and spatial dimension.

1. The two factors are λ¹ and λ³.
2. Their product is λ^{1+3}.
3. Thus k=4.
