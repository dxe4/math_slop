# Differentiate a parametric slope with respect to x

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

A parametric curve has x′(t)=2 at t=t₀, and the t-derivative of its slope h(t)=dy/dx is h′(t₀)=6. Using $d/dx=(1/x')d/dt$, find d²y/dx² there.


<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The derivative operator divides by x′.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Apply it to the already-computed slope h.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The second graph derivative is not merely the parameter derivative of its slope.

1. The requested derivative is h′(t₀)/x′(t₀).
2. The denominator is nonzero, so this equals 6/2.
3. Thus d²y/dx²=3.

</details>
