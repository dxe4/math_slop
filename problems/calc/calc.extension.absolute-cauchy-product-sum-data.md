# Use the sum law for an absolutely convergent Cauchy product

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

Two absolutely convergent series have sums $2$ and $3$. What is the sum of their Cauchy product, whose nth term is the finite convolution of coefficient pairs with total index n?

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

The Cauchy product models multiplication of sums when absolute convergence controls rearrangement.

1. The product theorem applies to the two absolutely convergent series.
2. Its sum equals the product of their sums.
3. Thus the value is 2×3=6.
