# Use supplied parametric velocity components

*Easy · [Calculus](index.md)*

At a parameter value, a regular plane curve has $dx/dt=4$ and $dy/dt=12$. Find dy/dx there.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The horizontal velocity is nonzero.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Divide vertical velocity by horizontal velocity.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Parametric slope is the ratio of the coordinate derivatives.

1. Because dx/dt=4≠0, the curve is locally a graph in x.
2. Its slope is (dy/dt)/(dx/dt)=12/4.
3. Thus dy/dx=3.

</details>
