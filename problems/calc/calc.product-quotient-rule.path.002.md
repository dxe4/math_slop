# Product and quotient rules: quotient rule point

*Medium · [Calculus](index.md)*

For $f(x)=(x^{2}+1)/x, x\neq 0$, use the quotient rule to find $f^{\prime}(2)$.

<details>
<summary>Hint 1</summary>

The quotient-rule numerator is top derivative times bottom minus top times bottom derivative.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Hint 2</summary>

Square the denominator before evaluating.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

At $x=2$ this is $3/4$. The derivative formula still excludes zero.

1. The quotient rule gives $f^{\prime}(x)$=\[$2x\cdot x-(x^{2}+1)$\]/$x^{2}=(x^{2}-1)/x^{2}$.
2. At $x=2$ this is $3/4$. The derivative formula still excludes zero.

</details>
