# Average a velocity by its durations

*Medium · [Calculus](index.md)*

An object moves with velocity $3$ m/s for two seconds and -1 m/s for the next four seconds. Find its average velocity over all six seconds, in m/s.

<details>
<summary>Worked solution</summary>

Unequal durations require time-weighted averaging.

1. The first displacement is six metres and the second is negative four metres.
2. The total displacement is two metres over six seconds.
3. The average velocity is one third metre per second.
